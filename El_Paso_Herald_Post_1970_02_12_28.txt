 

El Paso Herald-Post (El Paso, Texas) - Thu, Feb 12, 1970 - Page 28

KINGS MEN-—Sal Berroteran, left, Bob Morris, center, and
Jack Niland are a top trio now in the affairs of El Paso Sun
Kings. Berroteran and Niland head a steering committee for
the Kings and Morris is general manager. Plans for a season
ticket drive for the coming season are being made.

Herschleder Leaves
EI Paso Sun Kings

Fred Herschleder, business
manager of the El Paso Sun
Kings and former general
manager of the club when it
‘was owned by the California
Angels, is leaving baseball for
a job in the 6th Judicial Dis-
trict court clerk’s office here.

Bob Morris, general manag-
er of the Kings, made the
announcement at a meeting of
EI Paso baseball supporters.

MORRIS SAID that the club
will have no regular business
manager during the coming
season but will have Bob Red-
man to do bookkeeping. Her-
schleder will remain partly

active with the club in an advi-
sory capacity.

The co-chairmen of the
steering committee of the Sun
Kings, Jack Niland and Sal
Berroteran, appointed coordi-
nating committee chairmen at
the Sun Kings’ meeting.
Niland himself will work with
the Chamber of Commerce
and city officials. Berretoran
will coordinate activities with
the Lulac, softball organiza-
tions and Juarez groups, Haw-
ley Richeson will introduce
political tie-ins and Andy
Cohen will work with older
baseball groups.

Hank Iba to Speak
At Mt. Sinai Fete

Henry P. (Hank) Iba, Okla-
homa State’s famed basketball
coach, will be the speaker at
the Temple Mount Sinai’s 8th
Annual father-son sports
award dinner to be held next
Tuesday at Temple Mount
Sinai.

Iba, who is retiring as Okla-
homa State’s athletic director
and head basket coach after
this season, has won two
national championships and
numerous conference titles.
He's coached two winning U.S.
Olympic teams.

THE DINNER will honor El
Paso’s Sportsman of the Year.
Past winners are George Gen-
ovese, Don Haskins, George
McCarty, Bobby Dobbs, Saul
Kleinfeld, Andy Cohen and
