Miners Blasted In Twinbi

By MARK LEIBSON
Times Sports Writer

TEMPE, Ariz. — The University of
Texas at El Paso baseball team no
longer worries about trying to defeat
the mighty Arizona State Sun Devils.
Ali the Miners want to do is be
respectable.

“T don’t relish these 21-4 games,”’
said Coach Andy Cohen Saturday
afternoon after his Miners dropped
the first half of a day-night
doubleheader to the defending
Western Athletic Conference
champions.

“We really played the kind of ball
we are capable of playing through the
first six innings,"’ added Cohen. ‘‘The
score was only 83 at that time which
is respectable.

“T made a mistake when I pulled
Dave (Jannusch). He threw the ball
pretty good and the reason I pulled
him was because I was trying to avoid
a rout. But that’s exactly what hap-
pened. I brought in a couple of
relievers who couldn’t get anybody
cut. I should have left Jannusch in.”

No matter if the UTEP starter
stayed or not, the Miners were still
bombarded once again, dropping its
WAC record to 0-6 after losing the
second game 13-2. ASU stayed within

an arms distance of Arizona in the
