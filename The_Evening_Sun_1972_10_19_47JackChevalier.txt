Ken Harreison’s funniest baseball story concerns a 16-
hour eating and drinking spree with Frank Howard. It seems
that Howard had cut down to 255 pounds, on orders from
the late Gil Hodges, and lost his batting strength. So Hondo
went on a tour of Pompano (Fla.) and regained 23 pounds
in one night.
