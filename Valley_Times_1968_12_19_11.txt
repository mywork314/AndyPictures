FREE BASEBALL CLINIC
FOR PREP COACHES

The American Association of College Baseball Coaches will
hold a free clinic for high school coaches at the Biltmore
Hotel Friday, Jan. 3, starting at 7 p.m., it was announced by
AACBC president Dan Litwhiler of Michigan State.

The featured clinic speakers will be Bobby Wren, Ohio
University, on “The Double Play”; Jack Stallings, Wake
Forest, on ‘“Baserunning”; Leon Lande, Wayne State, on
‘Hitting Faults and Remedies’; and Andy Cohen, Texas at
E! Paso, on “Defensive Strategy.”

“We are hopeful that all Southern California prep coaches
will take advantage of this special clinic for them,” Litwhiler
said, He stressed there’s no charge and reservations need not
be made.
