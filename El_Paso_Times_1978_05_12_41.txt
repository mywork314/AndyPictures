UTEP Hosts UNM

For a last-place team, the University of Texas at
El Paso certainly has several incentives going for it
when the Miners host New Mexico for a three-game
series beginning at 7:30 p.m. Friday at Dudley
Field.

“We need to win two of them to stay out of the
cellar,” said Miner Coach Andy Cohen. “We only
need one to end up with a .500 record and have the
most victories of any other team in UTEP histo-

One of UTEP’s two Western Athletic Conference
victories was against the Lobos earlier in the sea-
son in Albuquerque but since that time, the Miners
have lost seven WAC games in a row.

UTEP’s overall record currently stands at 26-24
and 213 in conference while the Lobos have assured
themselves of a winning season with a 29-24 record
but only 3-12 in WAC.

Cohen indicated that junior right-hander Jerry
Walker, who has experienced some soreness in his
shoulder, will be on the mound for the Miners.

Both teams concluded the 1978 season with a
Saturday afternoon doubleheader.
