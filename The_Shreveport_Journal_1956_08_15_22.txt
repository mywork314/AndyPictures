Hiked by Pels

By The Associated Press

Refreshed by a five-day vaca-
tion at All-Star time last month,
the New Orleans Pelicans ‘have
soared toward the Southern Assn,
playoffs at .750 pace with 21 tri-
umphs in their last 28 games.

The Birds had two open dates
wrapped around the All-Star game,
and two rainouts followed, giving
manager Andy Cohen time to re-
group his forces for the torrid drive
for the first division. In addition
to the 21 victories, New Orleans
leads Montgomery 3-2 in a sus-
pended game with one inning to
play.

Last night the Pelicans swept a
doubleheader from Chattanooga
2-0 and 4-2 to move to within a
game and a half of the fourth place
Nashville Vols, who lost a pair to
Mobile 5-0 and 3-2.

Vern Kildoo, New Orleans left-
hander, ran his string of scoreless
innings to 2123. The big southpaw
etched his second straight shutout
with a two-hitter in the short
opener and zeroed Chattanooga
again in the ninth inning of the
second game. Kildoo also fanned
the last two Lookouts in the eighth
after rescuing Hyman Cohen. Chat-
tanooga had two men on base when
Cohen departed.
