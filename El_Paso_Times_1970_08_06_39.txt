El Paso Times (El Paso, Texas) - Thu, Aug 6, 1970 - Page 39

Two Enter
United Way
Queen Race

Two entrants have been
made into the Miss United
Way Contest to be held at 7:30
p.m. Aug. 28 in the Festivai
Theater;

Carla Walicek with City
Engineering and Christina
Ramirez with City Traffic and
Transportation are the
entrants. Both young ladies
filled out forms and mailed
them along with a black and
white photograph to the United
Fund office.

Charlie Tupper is working
with Mrs. Milton Leach, Andy
Cohen and D. B. Parker on the
committee, judges selected
are: Marlin Haines, Mrs.
Doris Thompson, John Con-
boye, Fred Witt and Marjorie
Graham.

Jack Rye will act as MC
during the hour and a half cer-
emory. Judges will be asked
to select the first three win-
ners on the basis of poise, per-
sonality, grooming and hair
style.

Tupper said any young lady
who works for a fair share
company and believes in the
United Fund concept of giving,
is between the ages of 18 and
25 and will be able to repre-
sent the United Fur at lunch-
eons and banque's is eligible
to enter.

Further information may be
had by calling the United
Fund office. Deadline for

entering is Aug. lf.
