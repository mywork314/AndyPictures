PLANT YUCCAS—Members of the Beautify El Paso Assn.,
planted eight yuccas today at the new park at U.S.-Mexico
Boundary Monument No. 1, located across the Rio Grande
from Smeltertown. From left: Mrs. George Kistenmacher,
association vice president; Morris Raney, real estate division,
U. S. Boundary Commission; Andy Cohen, association president,
and Mrs. C. V. Christensen, association secretary. The yuceas
were made possible through the co-operation of Ross Hickok
and Dick Hulbert of El Paso City Street Dept. Monument Park
is a joint venture of the U.S. Section, IBWC, the Beautify El
Paso Asso., and the El Paso and Dona Ana Historical Societies.
