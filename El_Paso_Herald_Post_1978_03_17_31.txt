Miners can't
get hits

UTEP had little trouble getting on
base, but after the Miners reached
base, they stayed there, And that is
the story in a nut shell, of last night's
twin bill loss to Denver,

Denver and the Miners meet at
Dudley Field at 8 tonight in another
game, with one more contest slated
tomorrow night at the same time and
place,

‘We left 11 men on in the second
game, We just couldn't hit with men
on. The pitching wasn't that bad,
although it was spasmodic,’’ Coach
Andy Cohen said following the 4-0 and
3-1 double loss by the Miners.

Sam Paves and Jeff Tarter both
slugged home runs for Denver in the’
second game, but the Miners outhit
Denver, 10-6. ‘We didn’t hit with men
on and that’s the story of both
games,’’ Cohen said,
