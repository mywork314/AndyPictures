Friday, March 10, 1967

Miner Nine,
Albuquerque
Clash Friday

University of Texas at El Paso
baseball season swings into its
second weekend Friday at Dud-
ley Field when the Miners meet
University of Albuquerque in the
first of a pair of doubleheaders.

First games of the twin bills,
both Friday and Saturday, are
set for 1 p.m. with second games
to follow immediately after.

Coach Andy Cohen has named
junior lefty Lynn Hunt and
freshman righthander Jesse Lu-
jan as starters for the Friday
doubleheader although he has
not decided which hurler will
Start the first game.

Cohen’s lineup is becoming
more settled after some experi-
menting in the season-opening
series with University of New
Mexico last weekend. Larry Mc-
Farlin, the Miners’ leading hit-
ter the last two seasons will
move back to his old position in
centerfield and John Miller, a
freshman will take over at first
base where he played in the
last two games against UNM.

Charlie West will .be behind
the plate for UTEP and Grady
Caveness will start in left field.
Juan Sandoval, a freshman from
Jefferson High, will be at sec-
ond base.

Danny Blanco, one of the top
Miner hitters and in his second
year as Starting third baseman,
is a question mark for the Al-
buquerque series. The sopho-
more infielder has been ill all
week and Cohen is uncertain
how.much he will be able to play.
Ron Rush, a freshman from
Midland Lee will fill in at third
if Blanco is not able to play.

The Miners take a 1-1-1 record
into the four-game series. UTEP
played UNM to a 9-9 tie in a
game that was called because
of darkness after nine innings
last week and then split a dou-
bleheader with the Lobos.
