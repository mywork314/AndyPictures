Wildcats Explode In Fifth To
Coast Past Miners, 11-2

By DAVE ADAM
Star Staff Writer

The University of Arizona baseball team,
handcuffed through four innings by Texas-El
Paso pitcher Roy Stringer, exploded for 10
runs in the fifth inning and coasted to an 11-2
victory Friday in a Western Athletic Confer-
ence Southern Division game.

Wildeat right-hander Dave Breuker scat-
tered five hits and struck out 15 batters in
posting his 11th win of the season against no
defeats,

The victory boosted Arizona to 44-2 for the
season and 4-0 in the Southern Division. The
Miners fell to 21-14 for the year and 1-3 in the
division.

The two teams wind up their three-game
series today with a 1 pm. doubleheader at
Wildcat Field. KZAZ-11 will televise the nine-
