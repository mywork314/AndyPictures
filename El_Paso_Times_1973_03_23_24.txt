Tribe Raps
8-6 Victory
Off Miners

Seven unearned runs in the
fifth inning gave Southern Col-
Orado State an 8-6 college
baseball victory over the Uni-
versity of Texas at El Paso
Thursday afternoon at Dudley
Field.

The big inning, in which
Miner Coach Andy Cohen used
four pitchers, erased a 4-0,
U.T. El Paso lead, and a ninth
inning rally was cut short with
a bases loaded double play by
SCSC.

Second baseman Mark Mil-
ler led Miner hitters with a
two-for-four performance, in-
cluding a solo home run in the
seventh.

Tony Eaton, (0-2), the sec-
ond U.T. El Paso pitcher, took
the loss. Larry O’Breza (1-0)
needed late inning help for the
win.
