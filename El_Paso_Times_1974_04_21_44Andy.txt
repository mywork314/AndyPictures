Special to The Fl Paso Times

ALBUQUERQUE, N.M. —
The University of Texas at El
Paso baseball team outhit the
University of New Mexico all
afternoon but the Lobos came
away with a doubleheader
sweep, 3-2. 94, in Western
Athletic Conference play
Saturday.

All the scoring in the opener
occurred in the second inning.
The visiting Miners led off with
Joe Pennies’ double. Terry
Dillingham singled him across
for the first run. After Keith
Rushing walked, Jim Brundage
laid down a sacrifice bunt
which the first baseman could
not handle. Dillingham scored
from second base on the play
with the second Miner tally.

The Lobos countered in their
half of the frame with three
runs. An error, two singles and
Ron Smith’s two-RBI double
gave New Mexico its winning
total.

Miner coach Andy Cohen
said, ‘‘Hogan pitched a good
game. We just didn’t get any
breaks.”

Hitting stars in the contest
were Pennies with a four-for-
four performance including
two doubles and Smith who
went two-for-two including his
game-winning hit.

Holding a 1-0 lead in the
bottom of the fourth, the
Miners got into trouble.

UNM loaded the bases with a
single and two walks before Bill
Pikorsky’s double chased three
runs home. Then Bob Knox
relieved Miner starter Dan
Modlinski and retired the first
man he faced. Another walk
and an error loaded the bases
again before Mark Hiller
delivered a single to up the
UNM lead to 5-1.

The Miners picked up 11 hits
but could not bunch them to
score more than one run in any
inning. In the fifth, Cohen's

team garnered three safeties
and had the bases loaded with
one score in. However, Bob
Downing hit into a force play at
second to end the inning.

In the ninth, trailing 8-3, the
Miners put three runners on
with no outs. Rushing walked,
Roy Stringer singled and Herb
Von Hagen walked to mount the
threat.
