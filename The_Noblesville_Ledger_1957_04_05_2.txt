With Nine-Game
Home Schedule

The Indianapolis Indians will
open the American Association sea-
son with a nine-game home stand
facing four western teams in the
first home schedule.

Minneapolis’ Millers will help the
Indians open the season April 16.
All seats in Victory Field are re-
served for that game.

Minneapolis will again play the
Indians April 17 before the St.
Paul Saints pull into town. The
Saints also will play two days, April
18 and 19, before moving out for
the Omaha Cardinals. Omaha will
make a three day stand. -

On April 22 and 23, the Denver
Bears, New York Yankee farm club
and last year’s AA runnerup, will
visit Naptown.

The Indians then leave for a
three-week road trip before return-
ing home for an eight-game stand.
It will be in the second appearance
at Victory Field the Indians will
play their first Sunday home
doubleheader of the season.

Wichita, which is to make its
initial appearances May 10 and 11,
will encounter manager Andy Co-
hen’s Chicago White Sox “farmers”
May 12 for two games.

Charleston and Louisville follow
the Braves into town before the
Indians leave on another road trip.

The complete Indianapolis home
schedule appears elsewhere on to-
day’s sports page.

Wichita will furnish the Indians
the opposition when the Indian-
apolis club sponsors its annual
July 4 fireworks extravaganza.

The American Association All
Star game is slated for the 15th of
July.
