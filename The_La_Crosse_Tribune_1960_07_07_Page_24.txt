TRIPLE PLAY RHUBARB—A triple play by the
Milwaukee Braves Wednesday night at County Sta-
dium created quite a rhubarb involving the Phila-
delphia Phillies and umpire Augie Donatelli, The
bases were loaded and B. G. Smith lined a drive to
Chuck Cottier for one out. Cottier fired the ball to
Joe Adcock to double up Tony Taylor at first, Ad-
cock hurled the ball to Del Crandall at the plate,
shown here as pitcher Dick Farrell crosses the
plate. Crandall then threw to Eddie Mathews at
third for the triple play, Farrell failing to tag up
believing Cottier had trapped the ball, Lower photo
shows Phillies Gene Mauch, Peanuts Lowery and
Andy Cohen arguing with Donatelli after the inci-
dent. Mauch was ejected.—Unifax.
