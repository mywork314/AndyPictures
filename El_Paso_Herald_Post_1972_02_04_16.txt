Cold Air Hits
All of Texas

By United Press International

A frigid blast of Arctic air
settled over the entire state
today but southerly winds
gave hope of perhaps a milder,
if not warmer, weekend.

Freezing temperatures, how-
ever, were prevalent almost
over the entire state with only
a few south Texas cities
reporting mercury above the
32 degree mark. Readings
were in the teens over much of
northern and southwest Texas.
Central Texas dropped into the
20s.
