Score Probe

 

CLEVELAND—Fye Special-
ist Dr. Charles I. Thomas said
today that Pitcher Herb Score
probably will not return to ac-
tion this year.

However, Thomas declined
to say definitely that the hard-
throwing pitcher was through
for the season. Thomas, who
has treated Score since the
hurler was struck in the eye
by a batted ball May 7, said
the southpaw would undergo
a new training program to
improve his depth perception.

The specialist declined to
reveal the nature of the new
program, but indicated Score
will focus his eye on various
objects. The program will be
conducted in Thomas’ office
and Score’s_home.
