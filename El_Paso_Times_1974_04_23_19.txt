Miners, NM Ags
Set Doubleheader

Special To THE EL PASO TIMES

LAS CRUCES, N.M. — At-
tempting to get back on the
winning track, the University
of Texas at El Paso Miners will
meet New Mexico State in a
baseball doubleheader here
tonight.

The Miners own a 22-18
record after three straight
losses to the University of New
Mexico, but Coach Andy
Cohen's crew is only four wins
away from an all-time school
record. The games at NMSU
are the team’s last games out
of the Western Athietic Con-
ference’s Southern Division.

Following Tuesday’s games,
U.T. El Paso travels to Tempe
for a three-game series with
Arizona State Friday and
Saturday.

Arizona leads the WAC-South
with a 7-2 record, while ASU is
6-3. New Mexico is 3-6, while
the Miners trail at 2-7, but with
wins over both Arizona schools.
