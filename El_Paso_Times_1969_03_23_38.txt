Miner Game
Canceled

By Showers

Rain washed out the final
game of the baseball set
between University of Texas
at El Paso and Colorado State
College Saturday and gave
Andy Cohen’s Miners an
added day of rest.

Saturday morning’s show-
ers put Dudley Field’s playing
area in such condition that the
game was cancelled.

The Miners return to their
tight schedule Monday when
they meet Denver University
at 2:30 p.m. to open a two-
game set. Second game is
scheduled Tuesday afternoon.

Colorado State University’s
Rams will be here for single
games Wednesday and Thurs-
day, followed by Colorado

University on Friday and Sat-
urday.
