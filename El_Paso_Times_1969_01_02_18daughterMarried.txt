Cohen-Nickerson Vows
Read In New York Rite

Miss Marina Allen Cohen
exchanged wedding vows with
John Charles Nickerson at 2
p.m. Dec. 28 in Park Congre-
gational Church in Elmira,
N.Y, Parents of the couple are
Mr. and Mrs. Andrew Howard
Cohen, 4341 N. Stanton, who
attended the rite from El
Paso, and Mr. and Mrs, War-
ren G. Nickerson of Elmira.

The Rev. Robert Lester offi-
ciated before an altar banked
with evergreen trees and bas-
kets of white gladioli and
flanked by silver candelabra
holding lighted tapers. Soloist,
William Tanner, was accom-
panied at the organ by Albert
Zabel.

The bride, given in mar-
riage by her father, wore a
formal A-line gown of white
velvet with Venise lace
sleeves. She carried a cascade
of white roses.

Mrs. Robert W. Nickerson,
sister-in-law of the  bride-
groom, was matron of honor.
Miss Catherine Cohen was
maid of honor. Bridesmaids
were Miss Connie Smith, Miss
Wende Frutchy, Miss Joanna
Wurtele, Miss Georgianna
Underhill, Miss Betsy Van-
Duzer, Miss Nicola Griggs
and Miss Sandra Gerdes.

All wore gowns of cranberry
red velvet with a satin float-
ing back panel and tiaras of
holly and roses in their hair.
Each carried a white muff
with holly and red roses.

Mrs. Cohen wore aqua blue
silk with lace and chiffon
accents and a white gardenia.
Mrs. Nickerson was in beige
brocade with a beige garden-
ia.

Robert Nickerson was his
brother’s best man. Grooms-
men were Norman Nickerson,
brother of the bridegroom;
Henry Cohen, brother of the
bride; William Fitzgerald,
Ronald Witmer, Russell F.
Gee Jr., William French, John
Brand and George Underhill.

A reception followed in

MRS. JOHN CHARLES NICKERSON

Elmira Country Club.

Out-of-town guests included
an aunt, Mrs. John Reznikov,
of El Paso.

Following a wedding trip to
Miami Beach, Fla., the couple
will be at home in Elmira,
where the bridegroom is
employed by Hardinge Broth-
ers, Inc.
