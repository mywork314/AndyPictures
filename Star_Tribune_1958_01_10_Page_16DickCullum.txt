WELCOME TO ANDY

It was good to learn, after returning from an ab-
sence, that Andy Cohen is back in the American Asso-
ciation as manager of Denver.

If this means that Andy has found
a permanent place in the New York Yan-
kee organization, so much the better.

Ever since his early days as a player
Cohen has been good for baseball. He
has loved and respected the game and
has lived it in thought and action the
year around.

When you talk of baseball men, true
blood members of that great fraternity,
you must include Andy Cohen.
