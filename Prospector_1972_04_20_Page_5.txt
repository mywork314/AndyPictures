THE PROSPECTOR

Mistakes Cost Miners Wins;
ASU Triumphs 10-4,6-4,12-1

by Mac Balod

The WAC cellar door flung open
even wider for the Miners over the
weekend as the U.T. El Paso
baseball team found the going
rough with the number two
nationally ranked Sun Devils of
Arizona State University.

The Miners had several bright
moments in the three-game series
but couldn’t come up with the
winning runs. The local
diamondsmen dropped all three
games.

The first game played Friday
saw the Miners pull out to a 4-0
lead going into the fourth inning
and it looked as if U.T. El Paso
