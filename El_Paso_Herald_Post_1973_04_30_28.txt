Could it be that the new ace
of the U.T.-El Paso mound
staff is a shortstop?

Miner coach Andy Cohen is
thinking twice about convert-
ing junior Herb von Hagen to a
pitcher after his performance
against powerful Arizona State
University last weekend.

Von Hagen came in relief of
three Miner hurlers in the
third inning of the Sun Devil-
Miner series and hurled 6 1-3
innings, allowing only three
hits and walking two batters.

“HE HAS MORE poise at
pitcher,” remarked Cohen,
“than he does at any Other
position. He showed a lot of

stuff out there against a great
team. He had a lot Of confid-
ence in what he was doing.”

Von Hagen won't, however,
be on the mound when the
Miners travel to Las Cruces to
face New Mexico State tomor-
row at 6 p.m. in a doublehead-
er. Cohen is expected to start
Mark Miller (3-1) and Mike
Hilton (1-4).

“We've got to watch out for
New Mexico State,” Cohen
said. ‘‘They have improved a
lot from when they played
here last.”

THE AGGIES are 12-31 on
the season, the worst record
since 1969 when they were
9-27. U.T.-El Paso is 17-20 for
the season and is 2-10 in West-
ern Athletic Conference play.

UTEP suffered through
defeats of 20-7, 17-2 and 13-3 to
ASU this weekend. Miner first
baseman Barry Alburtis made
his first appearance in two
weeks and drove in one run in
the first game and two in the
second.
