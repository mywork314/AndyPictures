the Cajun coach, and events that led to his resignation this
week. There were many trials and tribulations for Shipley,
culminating with the Southland Conference ousting him for
calling it a Mickey Mouse league. Headline in -Phoenix
Paper: “Miners Diamond Champs.” It didn’t refer to Andy
Cohen's Miners but to San Manuel High School Miners in
Arizona. What they’re doing now: Bill Michael, former U.T.
El Paso assistant who was forced to resign his coaching job
at Oklahoma after the transcript tampering case, is in Little
Rock taking a2 management course for a restaurant chain.
There are reports that Michael will join Chuck Fairbanks,
the former Oklahoma coach, on the staff of the New England
Patriots. Two quarterbacks named Jackson have been in the
news. Kerry Jackson of Oklahoma has been ruled ineligible
next season because his credentials from Galveston Ball
High were changed and Mark Jackson is leaving UTEP for a
Southwest Conference school. Mary Huana has been reading
about so many junk pitchers in baseball that she’s thinking
of opening a junk yard to supply them with materials. If the
St. Louis Cardinals continue their surge, shouldn't they be
named the Busch Whackers? And did you hear of the circus
performer who took a bath and left three rings?
