El Paso Times (El Paso, Texas) - Fri, May 29, 1970 - Page 41

Formal Boys
Baseball
Opening Set

Some 1500 potential major
leaguers of the future will
gather for formal opening cer-
emonies of the 19th season of
Boys Baseball of El Paso at
6:15 p.m. Friday at Memorial
Park.

Gene Stogner, athletic direc-
tor for the City Parks and Rec-
reation Department will serve
as master of ceremonies for
the event. Stogner said that the
public is invited, and particu-
larly the parents and friends
of the youngsters involved.

Boys Baseball will field 96
teams this year in major and
minor league competition.
Boys 11 and 12 will play in the
majors, and the minor leagues
will be made up of boys 8
through 10 years of age.

Andy Cohen, one of El Paso’s
few ex-major leaguers and
now coach of the U.T. El Paso
baseball team, will be guest
speaker at Friday evening’s
ceremonies. Others on the pro-
gram include Larry Welsh,
commissioner of Boys Base-
ball; Art Ranew, executive
director of the program, and
his assistant, Al Sepkowitz;
and Rev. Harland M. Irvin,
Jr., who will give the invoca-
tion.

Two abbreviated exhibition
games will follow the formal
opening ceremonies A three-
inning minor league contest
will be staged between Magof-
fin School, sponsored by Down-
town Lions, and Bassett
School, sponsored by Lee and
Scott Insurance Co.
