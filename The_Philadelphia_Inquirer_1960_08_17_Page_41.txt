Phony Fine
Stuns Cohen

PITTSBURGH, Aug, 16.—All
day long the Phillies kidded
first base coach Andy Cohen
about his quick ejection in the
final inning of Monday night’s
game with the Pirates.

Cohen took the “stay around
a little while tonight, Andy”
jibes in good grace, but the
swarthy coach turned a little
white when he got to the club-
house Tuesday afternoon and
found a wire in his locker no-
tifying him that he had been
fined $100 by the league presi-
dent for Monday night’s inci-
dent.

Andy quickly recovered when
some of the Phils told him they
had faked the wire. A first of-
fender such as Cohen doesn’t
usually draw a fine.
