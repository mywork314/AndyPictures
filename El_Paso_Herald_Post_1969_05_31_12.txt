1500 Young
Baseballers
Open Season

Play ball!

Thats what 1500 youngsters
from all over El Paso heard
yesterday at Memorial Soft-
ball Field as Larry Welsch,
commissioner of Boys Base-
ball officially opened the sea-

son for the small fry.

Andy Cohen, University of
Texas at El Paso baseball
coach, was guest speaker and
also hit the first baseball
pitched by Mayor Pete de Wet-
ter.

Forty eight major and 48
minor boys baseball teams
were present for the first time
together.

Parks and Recreation direc-
tor Gene Stogner was Master
of Ceremonies and introduced
all of the officials of the pro-
gram.
