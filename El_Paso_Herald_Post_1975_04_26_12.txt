Miners Play Best
Game of Season,
Still Lose to ASU

Spectal to The Herald-Post

TEMPE — Even U.T.-El
Paso’s ‘‘best game of the year”
wasn't good enough to stop
Arizona State.

Andy Cohen’s Miner baseball
team suffered its 10th straight
Western Athletic Conference
loss last night to ASU 9-2. The
Miners will try to get in the win
column today with a= split
doubleheader against the Sun
Devils.

Roy Stringer started today’s
first game while left Ron
Wrobel will start the nightcap
for UTEP.

“IT WAS CLOSE,“ Cohen
said of the disheartening loss.
“We were right in the game
until the eighth inning. It was
one of our best games all
year."”

“(Jeff) Hogan pitched a
beautiful game,’ Cohen added.
“He pitched with his head,
heart and arm. It is probably
his best game ever. He tried a
bit in the seventh.”
