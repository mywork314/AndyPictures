Lowrey Signs
To Manage
New Orleans

NEW ORLEANS—(P)—Harry
Lee (Peanuts) Lowrey, 38, was
named playing manager of the
New Orleans Pelicans yesterday
with the solid backing of New
York Manager Casey Stengel.

General Manager of the Pels,
Vince Rizzo, made the announce-
ment. Lowrey got the nod when
approved by Lee MacPhail,
farm director of the Yankees.
The Pels, of the Southern Asso-
ciation, and the Yanks have a
working agreement this year.

Rizzo said Lowrey came
“highly recommended.” In fact,
Casey was the one who recom-
mended him for the job, He was
picked over eight or nine others
considered to succeed Andy
Cohen.”
