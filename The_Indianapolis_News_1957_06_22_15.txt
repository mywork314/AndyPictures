AN ALL-STAR PROBLEM
MINNEAPOLIS—Something of a problem is con-
fronting the July 15 American Association All-Star game
which only Omaha or Denver can solve.

The host team will be the
club leading the league after
the July 4 games, and if either
Minneapolis or St. Paul is in
that position, the press, players

and dignitaries attending the

event may have to bed at the
Stadium.

Minneapolis is host to the Na-
tional Shrine convention at that
time and all hotel rooms and
motels in the Twin City area are
booked solid for the occasion.
Ironically, St. Paul never has had
an All-Star game and this easily
could be the year,

If Wichita qualifies for the
All-Star game, the league
stands to lose a good chunk of
money because the Wichita
park can’t accommodate a
crowd large enougl to bear the
expenses of the game.

with either Omaha or Denver,
