PHILADELPHIA (UPI) —A
federal judge assumed juris-
diction of a court case involv-
ing the suspension of New
Mexico State Basketball play-
ers John Williamson and
Roland (Tree) Grant.

U.S. district court Judge
Louis C. Bechtle said he would
set a hearing in two weeks for
arguments on certain ‘critical
questions.”"

The players were suspended
last January by the NCAA for
allegedly receiving excessiv
financial aid. Their attorney,
Richard Phillips, obtained an
injunction against the NCAA,
but later claimed that William-
son, of New Haven, Conn., the
team’s leading scorer, and
Grant, of Philadelphia, still
were not permitted to play.
Phillips filed a contempt peti-
tion against the NCAA.

The contempt petition was
heard by city court judge Her-
bert Levin and recessed until
March 13.

The issues before Bechtle
will include the NCAA motian
to dissilve and vacate the pre-
liminary injunction and claims
by the players that they were
denied due process of law and
should be compensated for
damages to their reputations.

The two were barred from
playing after they were
charged with receiving more
money than they were entitled
to under NCAA rules while
working at a Las Cruces,
N.M., bank. They said they
needed the $110-a-week jobs to
meet tuition payments.
