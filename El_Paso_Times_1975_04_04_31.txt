Face Miners Tonight

‘Cats Record Does Talking

By MARK LEIBSON
‘Times Sports Writer

Sometimes records speak for
themselves and when it comes
to the University of Arizona,
Andy Cohen is well aware of the
Wildcat credentials.

“Coach Jerry Kindall's
record sure talks for itself."”
said the University of Texas at
EI Paso baseball coach as he
prepared the Miners for their
three-game series with fifth-
rated Arizona in Tucson
beginning at 1 p.m. Friday.

The initial Western Athletic
Conference series continues
with a doubleheader Saturday
before U.T. El Paso returns to
El Paso for an exhibition game
at 7 p.m. Wednesday against
the El Paso Diablos of the
Texas League.

“Arizona is a very powerful
ball club and I'd be happy to
win one game over there,”
added Cohen.

The problem U. T. El Paso
faces is a lineup that averages
.337 per hitter with six batters
holding .359 averages or better
paced by Steve Powers’ .414
and Bob Woodside’s .413 bat-
ting marks.

“You know,” said Cohen. “TI
was talking with Kindall the
other night and he’s reailly
worried about our team. We
asked him if we could use their
field Thursday night for
practice and he said his squad
would be going through an
extremely tough workout. I

sure hope we can live up to his
expectations.””

U. T. El Paso doesn't possess
any .400 hitters, but it does
have a few players who have
been known to rip the cover off
the hard balls.

Centerfielder Joey Pennies
leads the Miners with a .352
average, has three homers and
six doubles to his name and
leads the entire squad in stolen
bases with six. He has only
struck out twice. _

Glenn Villarreal at third is
the team’s leading home run
collector with four. He also has
more doubles (9) than any
other teammate, shares runs
batted in leadership with first
baseman Terry Dillingham
with 17 each, and has a .349
mark.

Outfielders Bob Alsup and
Steve Dau have identical .327
patting averages while Keith
Rushing rounds out the .300
hitters with a .309 mark.

Big George Landreth, a
southpaw from Midland, Tex.,
has compiled a 3-1 record
during his four appearances
while establishing the lowest
earned run average (0.85)
among the Miner hurlers.

Landreth has allowed only
three earned runs during the
3124 innings of mound duty and
has been tabbed by Cohen to
pitch the WAC openr Friday
against Arizona.
