Injuries hit
Miners at ASU

Injuries hit the UTEP Miners base-
ball team during their Arizona trip
and although the Miners came home
hurting, they are not as bad off as
they could have been.

Arizona State beat the Miners
twice, the first game, a 21-4 rout. “We
got beat badly the first game and we
were beset by injuries the second
game," said coach Andy Cohen.

‘In the second game, Jerry Walker
was pitching a fine game — it was 0-0

when he was hit by a line drive on his
knee cap and had to leave.

“Bill Roberts, our third baseman,
tore some cartilage and is out, Brad-
ford, our catcher, dived back into first
base to avoid a pick off throw and dis-
located a shoulder. Keith Cooper and
Terry Dillingham were in what I
thought would be the most serious ac-
cident, though, when they ran to-
gether in the outfield chasing a fly
ball. They left the field under their
own power,’’ Cohen said.

After losing 15-0 and 21-4, the
Miners host the University of New
Mexico Lobos at Dudley Field Friday
and Saturday. ‘‘We are going to try to
get ready for UNM,” Cohen said.
