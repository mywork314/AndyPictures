Says Cohen—
‘Miners

Have Made
Progress’

Andy Cohen’s U.T. El Paso
Miner baseballers are 12-12 as
the Western Athletic Confer-
ence part of the schedule
approaches. Andy, pointing his
team toward the league foes,
is satisfied the Miners have

made progress and expects
more improvement by the
time Arizona comes to Dudley
Field on April 10 and 11 fora
three-game series.

The former big leaguer and
veteran manager of profes-
sional clubs will send his Min-
ers against New Mexico High-
lands at Dudley Field Friday
and Saturday.

“THE LEFT handed pitch-
ing has looked all right so
far,’ Cohen said. “I am not
pleased with the right handed
pitching. I hope to get more
balance in this department by
the time our conference season
opens.”’

Adolf Varela, one right han-
der, will be tested against New
Mexico Highlands and Andy
hopes he comes through.

Cohen was well pleased with
Jim Gregerson, left hander
who had not allowed an earned
run in 25 innings until he faced
Regis Saturday. Gregerson
beat the Coloradoans 6-2 and
had 14 strikeouts for a U.T. El
Paso record.
