Kleinfeld Has His Night
Saul Kleinfeld, formerly of Passaic, was honored as the.
“Sportsman of the Year’ in El Paso, Texas, last week. It
~ ‘was at the Mount Sinai’s annual banquet.
' Kleinfeld, known as the leading El Paso baseball, basket-
ball, softball, bowling and golfing promoter, and noted for his
‘contributions to the happiness and welfare of underprivileged
groups, received an “Oscar.”

“Andy Cohen, former N.Y. Giant third baseman, and vice-
president of the El Paso Sun Kings, a Texas. League affiliate
of the California Angels, was the master of ceremonies. He
made the presentation, and Gene Autrey made the principal

speech. : =
