McFarlin Fills Spot

The University of Texas at El Paso named
Larry McFarlin to succeed Andy Cohen as head
baseball coach Friday. Cohen had served as
coach since the university first fielded an inter-
collegiate team in 1963 to earlier this summer
when he resigned.

McFarlin, a former star player for the
Miners, played pro ball for one year with the
California Angels system before returning to El
Paso to finish his degree and teach in 1968,

“Tam elated on the selection of Larry as the
new head coach,” said Cohen, “In fact, I’m
proud of the fact that one of my former players
was chosen to succeed me.”

In addition to his duties as coach, McFarlin
will also aid the department in promotion and
recruiting for all sports.
