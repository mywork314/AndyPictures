EI Paso Hosting
Jewish Cage Tilt

El Paso is hosting the 1976
Western Area Basketball
Championships of the Jewish
Welfare Board Friday through
Sunday at the Jewish Com-
munity Center, 405 Mardi Gras.

This is an official tournament
with the winner advancing to
the national finals to be held in
mid-April in Washington, D.C.
The age group involved is 14-18,

The three-day competition in
El Paso will be between the
Jewish community centers of
El Paso, Phoenix, Ft. Worth,
Dallas, San Diego and Los
Angeles. This year’s basketball
championships are also known
as the “Bicentennial Hoopla.”

Action starts at 12:30 p.m.
Friday with a first-round game
