Miners’ Loss Blamed On Umpire

By MARK LEIBSON
Times Sports Writer
University of Texas at El Paso
coach Andy Cohen blasted home plate
umpire Scott Ehret for causing the
Miners a 9-6 loss to Colorado State
Monday at Dudley Field.

“That uy behind the plate was...
poor," charged Cohen. “He was tak-
ing a lot of strikes away from Nick
(Fiorillo) there in the ninth and there
was no reason for him to throw Nick
out of the game.”’

The game turned into turmoil in the
top of the ninth after UTEP rallied for
five runs the inning before to take a 6-
5 advantage.

Fiorillo, a hard-throwing southpaw
with a dropping curve ball, kept the
ball low to the first two batters he fac-
ed in the ninth. Ehret, who had been
calling strikes in the exact area the
entire game, changed course in
midstream and started calling
Fiorillo’s pitches balls.

“It's a shame this had to happen to
the kids because they made one
helluva comeback there in the eighth

and deserved to win this game," said
Cohen.

After Galen Kitzmura and Nick
DeGennaro were issued free passes to
the base paths, Tony Antonlini’s at-
tempted sacrifice bunt fell out of the
out-reached glove of Miner first
baseman Dan Feagler for an error,
loading the bases with no outs.

Wes Zotz, who had won two games
over the weekend with a relief ap-
pearance Friday and a complete nine-
inning stint Saturday, was called to
the mound again but was tagged with
a Jim Bryan double which landed
squarely on the chalk lines down the
left field line, scoring the tying and
go-ahead runners.

Eric Kurosawa knocked in two
more runs with a single up the middle
for Colorado State.

Zotz’ appearance was not planned
by Cohen but after Fiorillo had been
thrown out of the game for “‘unsports-
manlike language” by Ehret, Cohen
had no other choice than to bring in
the over-worked Zotz.

UTEP scored first in the third when
third baseman Rick Cramer walked,
moved to second on a wild pitch, ad-
vanced to third on Bill Roberts’ short
single to left and scored on a fly to
deep right field by Art Bravo.

Colorado State, now 5-5 for the
season, began its scoring an inning
later when Jack Upton doubled to
right-center off UTEP starter K. C.
Nix and scored on back-to-back
singles by Jim Bryan and Kurosawa.
Alert UTEP wiped out a potential
CSU run in the same inning when Nix
appealed at third base that Byran left
too soon when tagging on a fly to
center by Bernard Brown.

The Rams broke the game open
with a run in the sixth and three more
in the seventh but the Miners battled
back to take a one-run lead in the
eighth with a five-run rally on four
singles and a pair of costly CSU er-
rors.

With one out, Bravo singled and
moved to second on a wild pitch. After
UTEP made its second out, Bob Alsup
hit an opposite field single to left scor-
ing Bravo. When Dan Feagler walk-
ed, Rams’ coach Tom Wheeler sent
Robert Brogan in to releave starter
Bill Moren.

Rich Hildebrand slapped a high
bouncer to Upton at first who threw
the ball past Brogan who was cover-
ing the bag, allowing Alsup to score
with Feagler and Hildebrand moving
up to third and second respectively.

Danny Haygood continued the com-
eback with a two-run single to right-
center followed by another two-run
single by Rick Perez in the same loca-
tion to give the Miners the lead.

The two teams return to Dudley
Field noon Tuesday to conclude the
three-game series with a
doubleheader. Jerry Walker and Jim
Sacco will start on the mound for
UTEP.
