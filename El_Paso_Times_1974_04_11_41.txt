Wind, Dust

Nix Mi
Nix Miners

High winds and blowing dust
forced postponement of the
University of Texas at El Paso
and McNeese State baseball
game Wednesday at Dudley
Field. Remainder of the series
is Slated with a doubleheader at
noon Thursday, weather
permitting.

Coach Andy Cohen will
probably give the starting
assignment to Mike Kruta, with
either Steve Klein or Sam Cox
seeing duty the second contest.

The Miners are now 21-11 on
the season, taking two-of-three
from Lamar University earlier
this week while McNeese was
splitting with the Aggies of New
Mexico State.

U.T. El Paso heads for
Tucson Friday and Saturday
for a three-game road show
against the Wildcats of Arizona
in Western Athletic Conference
play.
