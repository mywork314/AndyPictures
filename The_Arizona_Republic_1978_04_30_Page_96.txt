UTEP talking
to UA’s Wing

Special to The Republic

EL PASO — University of Arizona
assistant baseball coach Jim Wing may
consider applying for the head baseball
coaching job at the University of
Texas-E] Paso should it become open
after the season.

While here for a three-game series
Friday and Saturday, Wing discussed
the job with UTEP Athletic Director
Jim Bowden. It is believed that Andy
Cohen, in his 16th year as the Miners’
head coach, will retire after this sea-
son.

“I'd be interested,” Wing said,’ “But
there are a lot of factors involved.
First of all, Andy hasn’t announced -his
retirement, and I’d have to be sure
that baseball would receive some prior-
ity. .

“It's not that I’m unhappy at the
University of Arizona. I have enjoyed
my association with Jerry Kindall, But
if you are in the game, you want to be
head coach.

“Right now, though, I have just one
thing in mind — helping the Wildcats
win games the rest of the season.”

Cohen has a 15-year record of 274-327.
His Miners are 26-21 this season, tying
the: school record for most wins in a
season.

Wing has been Kindall’s assinant for
six years. Previously he was the, head
coach at Tucson Palo Verde High
School. His combined high school
coaching record is 162-73. While at Palo
Verde, he coached major league pitch-
ers Andy Hassler (Kansas City) | vand
Bob Lacey (Oakland).
