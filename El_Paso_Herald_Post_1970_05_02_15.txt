Minermen
Play Lobos
‘Hair Raiser’

Coach Andy Cohen’s U.T. El
Paso Miner baseball team was
hoping for no last inning prob-
lems today when it met New
Mexico in the final two games
of their three game Western
Athletic Conference series
today at Dudley Field.

This is what happened in the
opener yesterday and almost
cost Cohen victory. But reliev-
er Larry Smith came in put
the fire out and save a 10-8 win
for the Miners.

COHEN SAID, “We won
what should've been a breeze,
but ended up a hair raiser. We
got sloppy and it hurt us.”

The Miners held a 10-4 lead
going into the last inning and
allowed four more runs on five
hits.

Rick Butler collected four
RBI's off a single and a triple.
Bob Prokopowicz also had
three RBI’s and went three for
four.

The Miners were looking to
face the Lobo ace Jim Krem-
mal in the opener of the twin
bill today.
