MISSES NO HITTER—Baltimore rookie southpaw Don
Ferrarese is reaching high in the dressing room after just
missing baseball immortality. He blanked the Yankees with
no hits until the ninth inning when Andy Cohen led off with
an infield hit. The young hurler, starting his second major
league game, was touched for another single but managed
to shut out New York, 1 to 0, for the Yanks’ first whitewash

in 97 games. Ferrarese also drove in the game's only run.
