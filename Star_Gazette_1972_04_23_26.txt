Big Shindig

By AL MALLETTE

You think Kip Horsburgh and Carl Fazio

aren’t on the baseball ball!

Listen to this.

This is the 50th anniversary year of the
Eastern League (it was New York-Penn when
organized at Elmira’s old Rathbun Hotel in
1923) and Kip and Carl are going to celebrate
the anniversary—hopefully with the help of
EL prexy Roy Jackson and other Eastern
League officials.

The date has been fixed for Aug. 14 and
the energetic, enthusiastic Elmira owners are
hoping for a real shindig.

They're going to spice the Elmira part of
the affair with 1971-72 Hall of Fame in-
ductees—plus the selection of Elmira’s 50th
anniversary all-star team, a team that will
be selected by you the fans on ballots at the
ball park.

. The ballots will be ready by the next
homestand and will be inserted in the
scorebooks.

TO MAKE IT that much easier for you
fans, ballots will list current members of
Elmira’s Baseball Hall of Fame, those who
have come within a few votes of entry and
members of the 25th anniversary team
selected in 1948.

THAT’S THE selection chart—and,
believe me, it’s quite an array of talent of
hallowed names in the 50-year history of
Elmira professional baseball in the Eastern
League.

It should make for some deep
reminiscing, some heated debates and cer-
tainly a lot of fun for many long time Elmira
baseball fans.

The balloting should kindle a lot of in-
terest throughout the Twin Tiers among
young and old alike as to who were the better

ball players when they wore Elmira

uniforms—not for what they later ac-
complished in the majors.

Horsburgh and Fazio definitely hope it
doesn’t shuttle into a popularity contest—but
that voters think before balloting.

For you fans’ information the 25-year
team listed Robinson at first, Fallon at
second; White at short, Madura at third,
Denning catcher, Reiser, Staller and Terry
Moore in the outfield and Krausse (RH) and
Zoldak (LH) as the pitchers.

The balloting that time was so close that
Rogers, Grossman, Heise, Rizzo, Hannahoe,
Graham, Al Moore, Sperry and Maglie
missed by only a handful of votes.

Now it’s 25 years later and time for the
