Cohen Club
Gets Rest
Before ASU

Andy Cohen’s University of
Texas at El Paso baseball team
gets time off this week and
according to their coach,
“‘We’re gonna need it.”

The Miners, who dropped all
three games in their series with
Western Athletic Conference
foe the University of New
Mexico in Albuquerque over
the weekend, are off until
Friday when they play at
Arizona State.

“We have three games over
there in two days,’ Cohen
commented yesterday. “I think
our pitching staff right now can
use some rest. We're a little
down but these kids have a way
of bouncing back.”

Last Saturday the Orange
dropped a twinbill to UNM, 3-2
and 9-4. Righty Jeff Hogan
hurled a strong opening game
but two Miner errors proved
costly and the club left im-
portant runs on the bases.

IN THE NIGHTCAP UNM
battered starter Dan Modlinski
from the box during a five-run
fourth inning and went on to an
easy win. Modlinski is now 3-4
on the year while first game
loser Hogan is 3-3. i

The three-game sweep in the
Duke City dropped Cohen’s club
to 22-18 overall and 2-7 in WAC
play. With Ll games remaining,
all conference except a twinbill
with New Mexico State, the
Miners are four wins away
from the most triumphs ever
chalked up by a Cohen club in
13 years at-UTEP.
