Baseball Clinic

Starts Next Week

Would you like to learn
how to play baseball from
your home-owned Indianapo-
lis Indians?

Then here's an opportunity
you'll want to take advan-

tage of.

Now is the time to make
plans to participate in the
fifth annual Indianapolis
News baseball clinic for boys
8 to 16 years of age. Clinic
dates are July 9 and 10, so
be sure you're on hand when

Tribe Manager Andy Cohen
shouts “Play ball” at Victory
Field.

Every boy in the state is
eligible to take part in the
free clinic, which will be held
on the Victory Field diamond.
You'll have fun as you learn
the fine points of the game.
And what you learn you can
apply to your Little League
play.

If you have a uniform, wear
it and bring your glove and,
if you’re a catcher, your back-
stop attire. Every boy will
play. At your position you'll
receive instructions from In-
ot who play that position.
