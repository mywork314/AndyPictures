Andy back, Miners
play Arizona Cats

Andy Cohen rejoined his U.T. El Paso baseball
team today after illness of his wife Barbara forced
him to miss the team’s trip to Albuquerque to play
New Mexico last week.

The Miners were directed by Andy's brother
Sydney Cohen, and former Miner star, Jeff Hogan.

Mrs. Cohen was recovering from her illness.

The Miners were 1-2 on the New Mexico trip, los-
ing the opener 9-4, winning the second game 8-6 and
losing the third game 3-0.

A BASES loaded homer by Jerry Walker helped
the Miners score their victory. Walker pitched good
enough to win the second game of the twinbill but
did not get any runs.

The Miners will play Arizona in a single game
Friday at Dudley Field and will meet the Wildeats
in a twinbill Saturday, starting at 11 a.m.
