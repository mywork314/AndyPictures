Arizona ‘Wins In 11 Innings

Miners Give Cats A Scare

By MARK LEIBSON
Times Sports Writer

So close, yet so far.

Facing the powerful Arizona
Wildcats for the fourth time
this season, the University of
Texas at El Paso put forth its
best effort at breaking a 12-
game Western Athletic Con-
ference baseball slump.

Although Coach Andy
Cohen's Miners were able to
score a dozen runs, Arizona
scored more as it took the
Wildcats 11 innings to nip the
Miners 15-12 at Dudley Field
Friday.

“We really hit them today,”
commented Cohen after his
Miners dropped their WAC
mark- to 0-13 while AU in-
creased its conference record
to 10-3 to keep the Wildcat
hopes of catching Arizona State
University alive. “It's too bad
we couldn’t hold Arizona’s
hitters.”

Arizona was probably the
most surprised team in the
park Friday as U.T. El Paso
jumped out to a 6+2 advantage
after only two innings of play.

Glenn Villarreal, who
collected six runs batted in for
the afternoon, blasted a home
run deep to left-center field
after Art Bravo had doubled
and Bob Alsup walked to
leadoff the contest.

Powerful Steve Powers
helped the Wildcats tally two in
the second with a towering
homer to right but the Miners
added a trio - runs in the
second.

Instead of going for the
