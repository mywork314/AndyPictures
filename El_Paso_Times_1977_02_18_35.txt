Miners Catch Ranger
In Baseball Opener

By TIM MORRIS
Times Sports Writer

The University of Texas at El Paso
baseball team will open its 1977
season here Friday but Miner Coach
Andy Cohen is not so much concerned
with who will throw out the first ball
as he is with who will catch it.

The Miners get things under way
Friday with a 1 p.m. doubleheader
against Ranger Junior College on the
new diamond on the UTEP campus
and Cohen is still looking for a star-
ting catcher.

“We look to be a solid team on
defense and I think our pitchers will
come along all right,’ Cohen said.
“The only position that is wide open
right now is catcher, We're pretty
well set everywhere else.”

The Miners return an experienced
infield built around senior second
baseman Art Bravo. Keith Dwinell is
back at first with shortstops Rusty
Kennedy and Warren Cooper. Jim
Bradford is also returning at third
base, but may have to split time
between that position and catcher,
where he has been working in
preseason.

“Art Bravo will be our leader in the
infield,’’ Cohen said. “He's a good
defensive player and a natural leader.
I think we're going to have a good,
tight infield. We’re not going to be
real big hitters but it looks like we'll
be able to play defense.”

The only returning letterman for
the Miners in the outfield is Bill
Roberts, who will probably be joined
by Sal Flores, Brad George or Jose
Dominguez in the starting lineup.
Flores is also being considered for the
job behind the plate.
