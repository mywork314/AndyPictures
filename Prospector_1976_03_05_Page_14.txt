Prospector (El Paso, Texas) - Fri, Mar 5, 1976 - Page 14

Andy Cohen, Miner Basketball coach, is bubbling
with confidence that his University baseball team will
sweep both games against Abilene Chirstian College
March 5 and 6 at the Ysleta cutoff.

This will be the first time the Miners have ever
played Abilene Christian and with this being the
opening of Miners regular season the fans can be
assured of two exceptional contests.

“T really don’t know that much about them” ,
Cohen stated. “but this is the hardest working team I
have ever coached wich makes me eonbinens that they
can do the job.”

The Miners played four exhibitions with Ranger
Junior College and wound up with a disappointing
slate of 2-2. However, according to Cohen Texas-El
Paso has been drilling hard in practice on their hitting
and pitching wich appears be jelling alongside their
‘strong defensive infield.

Ailing Jeff Hogan is expected to get the starting
nod at pitcher Hogan has been suffering with a bad
backache but shoul be in fine condition to open the
contest. If he is in no condition to start Jerry Walker
will replace him.

Keith Dwinell will start at first base. Art Bravo at
second, Glenn Villarreal at third, David Patt at
shortstop and Terry Goodro will be starting catcher.
