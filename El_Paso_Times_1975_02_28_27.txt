Doubleheader

George Landreth and possibly
Sam Cox have been tabbed starters
for the University of Texas at El

Paso baseball duel Friday against
Hardin-Simmons University.

The doubleheader begins at 1 p.m.
at Dudley Field and the only com-
parison between the two squads is
the fact that they've both defeated
Texas Tech.

Two weeks ago, U.T. El Paso took

two of three contests from the Red.

Raiders and last weekend, Hardin-
Simmons split a pair.

Landreth, a big lefty from
Midland, Tex., is slated to hurl for
the Miners in the opener while Cox, a
steadily improving local boy from
Riverside could see action in the
nightcap.

Landreth's first mound ap-
pearance two weeks ago was short-
lived when a Texas Tech single
bounced off his leg, making for a
quick exit for the 200-pound junior.

While the Miners continue to
engage in non-Western Athletic
Conference action until April 4
against Arizona, the athletic
directors have been busy adopting a
new playoff system which will allow
two conference schools to par-
ticipate in the NCAA district
playoffs.

The WAC officials said the nor-

champions would meet in a best-of-
three series for the conference title,

On the same date, the runner-up

meet in a similar series at the

19, a day-night doubleheader will be

tourney. If a third game is needed, it
would be played the next night.

The victor of that series, along
with the league champion, would
represent the conference in the
district playoffs.
