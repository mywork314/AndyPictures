COHEN AT
SPORTS SHOW

Andy Cohen, new man-
ager of the Indianapolis
Indians, is scheduled to
make his first public ap-
pearance here tonight at
the sports show inthe
Coliseum. He will be in-
troduced during the stage
show, starting at 8 o'clock.
Later, he will greet fans
from the Indians’ booth.

Cohen and Ownie Bush,
president of the Indianap-
olis club, will leave by auto
tomorrow for Tampa, Fla.,
where Andy will help out
in the training program of
the Chicago White Sox. He
will remain there until the
Indianapolis camp opens
on March 12 at Hollywood,
