Banquet Recognizes
Top Junior Athletes

Father Yermo elementary school held its annual
sports award banquet June 2.

Ismael Tercero was master of ceremonies for the
event and Andy Cohen was guest speaker. Coaches
Susan Mena, John Maya and Rick Orona presented
awards to the outstanding pee wee and varsity boy

and girl in each sport.

In addition, ribbons from the
Parochial School District
Track Meet were presented.
