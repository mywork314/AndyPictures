Miners Get Pair
With Timely Plays

Timely pitching, pinpoint hitting
and a game-saving fielding gem lifted
the University of Texas at E] Paso to
a 4-3, 5-2 sweep of a doubleheader
over Lubbock Christian College Tues-
day at Tech Field.

The Miners were leading 4-1 in the
opener until the Chapparals closed to
within one run in the seventh inning
and had the tying and winning run-
ners on third and second respectively
with only one out.

Reliever K.C. Nix, who had come
into the game to strike out two bat-
ters with runners on second and third,
got himself into trouble in the seventh
but caught LCC’s Steve Brigante look-
ing al three pitches for the second out.

Lubbock Christian's Kenny Toney
then looped a short fly ball behind
Dan MeMahon at shortstop.
McMahon turned his back to the in-

head catch to end the game

“That was some play,” praised
Miner Coach Andy Cohen after the
Miners raised their record to 9-3.
“McMahon saved the game for us and
Nix didn’t do a bad job coming in and
getting us out of trouble.”

McMahon produced UTEP’s first
run in the second inning with a single
to right after freshman Art Flores had
walked, moved to second on a
sacrifice bunt and stolen third.
