MINERS GET UNIFORMS—Jim Paul, El Paso Diablos’
general Manager, left, presents University of Texas at
El Paso Head Baseball Coach Andy Cohen with a new
stock of baseball uniforms Tuesday at Dudley Field.
The Diablos donated all the old Sun Dodger uniforms
including batting helmets, pitching jackets and travel-
ing bags. The El Paso club has ordered new uniforms
for the start of the 1974 Texas League Baseball
campaign. (Times Photo by Luis Villalobos}
