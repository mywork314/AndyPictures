El Paso Times (El Paso, Texas) - Sun, Jul 26, 1970 - Page 41

UF Broadens

Queen Test
Competition

United Fund of El Paso has
opened entries for the Miss
United Way Contest. The
annual beauty contest is open
to all Fair Share Giver compa-
nies in the E! Paso area.

Any young woman in El
Paso area between the ages of
‘8 and 25 who works for a firm
which gives its ‘fair share” is
eligible to enter the contest.
Judging will be held at 7:30
P-m. August 28 at Festival
Theatre, Montana. The four
man committee in charge of
the contest is made up of
Charlie Tupper, chairman,
Mrs. Milton Leech, Andy
Cohen and D. B. Parker.

Entries are judged on the
basis of poise, personality,
grooming, accessories and
makeup. Contestants will
make one appearance before
the judging begins. They will
give their name, age and place
of employment to the judges
and then appear before the
judges and audience in an aft-
er-five dress of their selection
for the final judging.

Prizes for first, second and
third place finishers will be
donated by local firms. There
will also be several consolation
prizes.

Last year the winner was
Miss Bernice Steely from the
State National Bank. She won
the title over 22 other contest-
ants and received her crown
from Maj. Gen. Richard T.
Cassidy, Ft. Bliss commander.

Miss Steely represented
United Fund at luncheons and
banquets throughout the cam-
paign, wearing her pear! tiara
at all functions. She also won a
$75 gift certificate from the
Popular and a traveling tro-
traveling trophy has been on
raveling trophy has been on
display at the bank for the
past year.

United Fund mailed nearly
300 letters this week to eligible
firms in the area. Entry forms
were included in the letters
and should be returned along
with a black and white photo-
graph to Mrs. Joan Ferguson
at United Fund no later than
August 16.
