Miners

Look For New
Win Streak

The Texas Miners go back
to the playing field in search
of a new winning string Mon-
day when they host the Den-
ver University Pioneers ina
single game at 2:30 p.m. at
Dudley Field.

Coach Andy Cohen’s Uni-
versity of Texas at El Paso
diamond crew saw an eight-
game winning streak snapped
last week when they lost two
games, to Southern Illinois
University and to Colorado
State College. Their second
game with CSC, Saturday,
was canceled by wet grounds.

Monday's game, first of a
two-game set with the Pioneers,
Starts a six-game grind with
games scheduled each day.

Following the Denver game
Tuesday will come tilts with
Colorado State University
Wednesday and Thursday and
University of Colorado Friday
and Saturday.

University of Oklahoma is
scheduled for games Monday
and Tuesday of next week
here to wrap up the most con-

centrated portion of the Miner
schedule.
