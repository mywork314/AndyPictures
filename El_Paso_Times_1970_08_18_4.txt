WRITER DEFENDS
PROPOSAL TO CHARGE

Editor, El Paso Times:

John J, Hammer's bizarre
complaint about the proposal
to charge a nominal admission
to our Washington Park Zoo
(SPM, Aug. 14), is vulnerabie
to easy rebuttal.

Hammer brands as “irre-
sponsibile’’ the action of the
City Parks and Recreation
Board in endorsing the propos-
al of the El Paso Zoological
Society to charge 10 cents
admission for children up to
age 14 years and 25 cents for
adults. While deploring the
burden thus placed on low-in-
come families, Hammer neg-
lects to mention the proposal
Provides for one day per week
of free admission to the zoo.

Surely no reasonable resi-
dent can object to the long-
overdue idea common in other
cities where, if admission is
free at the main gate of a zoo,
charges are levied when enter-
ing various areas therein.

The Washington Park Zoo is
a municipal, tax-supported
institution, but it has always
occupied a position of very low
priority in deference to innu-
merable programs of pressing
public need which Hammer
cites as being more worthy.

The enlightened proposal
accords congratulations to our
City Parks and Recreation
Board and El Paso Zoological
Society for: (1) the attempt to
accomplish gradually a dire
need to upgrade a facility
enjoyed particularly by our
children; (2) letting those who
CHOOSE to visit the zoo share
in such upgrading rather than
putting the burden on the tax-
paying bloc disinterested or
disdainful of the project; (3)
getting a start, however late,
on a sound plan which is an
integral part of the essential
process of improving El
Paso's general image
