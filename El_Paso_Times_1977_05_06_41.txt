Miners Seek
Game Streak

The cliche ‘Records are meant to be broken’
holds true once again for the University of Texas at
El Paso baseball team.

Last week, Coach Andy Cohen's Miners shocked
Arizona by winning the series two games to one,
thus snapping a 19%game losing streak to the
Wildcats.

Another losing streak is on the line when the
Miners face Arizona State in a three-game series
starting with a single game at 1 p.m. at Dudley
Field. A doubleheader will be played Saturday.

The Miners have not defeated ASU in the past 20
meetings between the two schools while UTEP has
managed only one win out of the last 35 con-
frontations.

Yet, Arizona State isn’t about to take the Miners
lightly after UTEP stunned Arizona in both ends of
a doubleheader last Saturday.

“You know Arizona State’s coach (Jim Brock)
will make sure his kids won’t be too overconfident
because of what we did last week,’’ said Cohen
Thursday.

The Sun Devils will send Larry Eiler to the
mound Friday in search of his second straight win
over UTEP this season. Earlier in Tempe, Eiler (7-
1) blanked the Miners on three hits in a 15-0 romp.
