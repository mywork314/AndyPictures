INDIANS DROP TWO

Two pitchers—Joe Dahike and
Glen Rosenbaum—were dropped
from the Indianapolis roster as
the team prepared to leave for
Minneapolis to open a two-week
road trip. Rosenbaum, the Hoo-
sier from Union Mills, was op-
tioned to Colorado Springs, but
Dahlike will remain here pending
a transfer by the Chicago White
Sox.

Two more hurlers are expected
to be cut before the Tribe returns
home, reducing the hurling staff
to nine, the number that Man-
ager Andy Cohen will carry dur-
ing the season.
