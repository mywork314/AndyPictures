Thursday, February 10, 1972

PLANTING CEREMONY — A tree planting ceremony
was held Tuesday when 50 mulberry trees, donated
by the Beautify El Paso Association, were planted
at a proposed park site near International Boundry
Monument No. 1. The historic site is to be made an
attractive place for visitors by landscaping it with
trees, flagstones and benches. The monument is
located across the Rio Grande from Smeltertown
where it marks the point where the Rio Grande
begins to form the boundry between the United
States and Mevzico for 1,200 miles to its mouth at
the Gulf of Mezico. Present at the ceremonies were,
from left, Andy Cohen and Mrs. Mabel Welch, rep-
resenting the El Paso Beautify El Paso Association,
Miss Louise Garrett, from the Dona Ana County
Historical Society and Leon Metz, president of the
El Paso Historical Society.
