At Plaza Monumental—

Smouldering Rivalry
May Explode Sunday

The bitter, long-smouldering
rivalry between Mexico's top
bullfight box office attraction,
Curro Rivera and Leonardo
Manzano is expected to reach
its peak during Sunday’s bull-
fight spectacle at Plaza Monu-
mental.

Six San Antonio de Triana
fighting bulls, estimated to
weigh over 1000 pounds each,
will oppose Curro, Manzano
and dynamic young Mario Se-
villa in action which begins at
6 p.m.

THE HEATED rivalry
between Curro and Manzano
began last September at Plaza
Monumental’s competition for
the Golden Sword Trophy,
Mexican bullfighting’s most
coveted honor.

Although Curro had won the
only ear during the hotly-con-
tested competition, Manzano
was awarded the Golden
Sword by the president of the
Mexican Matador’s Union
despite the fact that both
young matadors appeared to
receive an equal ovation dur-
ing the voting at the close of
the corrida.

WHILE MANZANO was
overjoyed by the selection,
Curro fumed, claiming the
important trophy was rightful-
ly his for having been awarded
the only ear of the fight.

Tickets and reservations are
available in El Paso at Central
Ticket Agency, 120 E. Franklin
and at the Kentucky Club in
Juarez.

At Ysleta Cutoff Field

Ysleta Boys Baseball
Schedules Opening

Ysleta Boys Baseball-Bel Air
Optimist League will open its
20th season Saturday at the
Ysleta Cutoff Field. The
league is affiliated with the
National Boys Baseball
League, which is composed of
Boys from the Ysleta School
District ages 8-10 years old in
the Minors; 11-12 in the Bron-
cos and 13-14 in the Ponys.

The Bronco all star team
won the State Region last year
and went on to Birmingham,
Ala. for division play. They
won the sportsmanship award
and were awarded a division

flag.

THIS YEAR the Bronco all
star team will travel to San
Antonio for the Section Tour-
nament and then to Birming-
ham, Ala. for the Division
Play.
