El Paso Herald-Post (El Paso, Texas) - Mon, Apr 20, 1970 : Page 21

Miners Are Last,

But Gain

Coach Andy Cohen’s U. T. El
Paso Miner baseball team
might be in the cellar in the
South Division of the Western
Athletic Conference today, but
they were more respected than
ever.

Cohen’s team had shown
how explosive they could be in
unloading nine runs in one
inning against the Arizona
State Sun Devils at Phoenix.

This was the first game of
a doubleheader which the Min-
ers won ll-l, but dropped the
second 7-2.

BRIAN HARPER led the

Miner rout in the fourth inning |

of the first game. Harper hit a
bases loaded double to score

three RBI’s and then come |

back with a single in the same
inning to score two more.
Added to this were six more

Miner hits, four walks and an |

ASU miscue.

After the game ASU coach
Bobby Winkles said to Cohen,
“what did you feed those
boys?”

IN THAT GAME Marc Bom-
bard recorded what Cohen
called the finest game of his
career. “The game Bombard
threw was a masterpiece,”
said Cohen. ‘‘He mixed his
speeds well and kept all his
batters off balance.”

Also in that game pitcher
Harry Milner turned outfielder
and hit a homer and two sin-
gles.

In the second game Ken
Hanson defused the Miner bats
and struck out 15. But Milner
had his own fine performance
striking out 13.

“Milner had a fine game, he
just ran out of gas in the last
inning,” said Cohen,
