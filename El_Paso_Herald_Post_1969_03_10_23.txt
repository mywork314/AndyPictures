Bombard Beats Tech-

Surprise!

Miners

Hit .301 Average

By MIKE PEMBERTON

Andy Cohen’s U.T. El Paso
Miner baseballers just finished
knocking off their fifth
Straight game with southpaws
and are looking forward to
their sixth in a row, but with
a slight change in tactics.

Tomorrow when the mound
strong Miners hit the field
against the New Mexico State
Aggies at Dudley Field Cohen
will use a right hander. ‘I”
either go with Ron Peterson or
Larry Smith and doing the
work behind the plate will be
Ron Hix,” Cohen said.

The Aggies are led by second
basemen Lloyd Bates at the
plate with a .428 average and
Chuck Bowden with a .346
mark. The team as whole is
hitting .250 and is 1-16 for the
year.

PREDICTIONSS had it at
the start of the season the
Miners weren’t a hitting ball
club and if they were to win
they would have to do some-
thing at the plate. They have
done just that in the first
seven games of the season.

As a team they are batting
.301 and five starting players
are batting over the .300
mark. John Miller is at the
top of squad with a .450 mark

followed by Mike Ward witha
391 average. Behind Miller
and Ward are Ron Hix at .353,
Juan Sandoval with .346 and
Danny Blanco with a .305.

AS EXPECTED their pitch-
ing is the top department. As
a team they have given up
onty 1.96 earned runs per
game and have avergaed 5.67

€arned runs a game.

Saturday the Miners met
Texas Tech in the finale of a
three game series and made
it a sweep, with their second
5-2 win over the Red Raiders
at Dudley Field.

The Miners gave up only six
runs to an always strong hit-
ting Tech team, but none of

these were earned.

Cohen said, “Mare Bom-
bard was the difference. His
fast ball was working real
well and he had good control.
Even if a pitcher has good
stuff he must have control to
win and Bombard did.”

The Miners rallied in the
bottom of fourth inning be-
hind some clutch hitting and
an error. Joe Donohue added
two runs to the Miners column
with two run scoring single.
