Devils boost
record to 7-1
in first game

By BOB JACOBSEN

TEMPE — Arizona State got an eight-
hit, 10-strikeout route-going performance
from Don Hanna, a two-run home run
from Gary Allenson and 14 other assort-
ed safeties to down Texas-El Paso here
Saturday afternoon, 11-2, before a Bicen-
tennial crowd of 1,776 fans in Packard
Stadium.

The victory was the Sun Devils’ 46th
against eight losses, and upped their
Western Athletic Conference mark to 7-
1, UTEP dropped to 7-17 and’ 1-7, respec-
tively. The clubs met later Saturday
night in the second game of the split
doubleheader.

Hanna notched his 12th win without a
defeat, and walked just two. It followed
a strong performance last week against
New Mexico, another complete game,
and gave him five in 12 starts. He also
lowered his earned run average to 3.22,
and the 10 whiffs were his best in quite
a while.

“I felt stronger today than last Satur-
day,” said Hanna. “I had more com-
mand, and my slider is coming back.
They had a lot of handle shots for their
hits. They got their runs (both in the
second) simply because I wasn’t hitting
my spots.”

Allenson, who banged out his sixth
homer with a man aboard in the fifth,
agreed.

“His last two outings have been his
most consistent,” said the Devil catcher.
“He had all his pitches under control
and around the plate, especially his fast
ball and slider. His ball was sinking so
much I took a beating trying to stop it.””

Known only to the on-the-field partici-

pants, the clubs were playing with a 10-
run rule after seven innings.

“But we could never get ahead by
10,” said coach Jim Brock, whose club
walloped the Miners, 21-0, Friday night.
“The umpire (Lee Cook) brought it up,

and Andy (UTEP coach Andy Cohen)
said:

‘T'd love to have one.’

“I ran for Allenson in the seventh
(with Gary Rajsich) hoping we could get
him home. Of course we didn't want to
Steal in that situation.”

The Devils, hitting .332 coming into
this series, are now 34-for-75 (.453) in
the two games. They have hit five
homers (by an equal number of
players), and have collected 16 extra
base blows. .
