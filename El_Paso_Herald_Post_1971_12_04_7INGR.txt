The Miners' Fireball

LITTLE BETO BAUTISTA is going to develop into the most
spectacular buzz bomb UTEP has had in basketball since Nate
Archibaid. He really can bring that ball down the court. Coac
Haskins says he’s not trying to bring his big boy, James Forbes
(6-7 or 6-8?), along very fast. The good news is that Andy Cohe
is mending fast. For one of the few times in his life, he was hit
by illness. To the man at the Touchdown Club who was unaware
of the name change and had been searching in vain for Lev
Alcindor’s name in the basketball boxes: Don’t try to find
Kareem Jabbar’s name in the Milwaukee box score last night
He missed the game. He was hit by the flu bug. Quote in True
Magazine’s ‘Play For Kush and You Can Play For Anybody,’
written by David Wolf: ‘“‘The Sun Devils have . . . been ham
pered by schedules that include the likes of San Jose State anc
Texas at El] Paso.” Not surprising from an Eastern writer whe
can’t spell Camp Tontazona correctly. He might as well have
said ASU was hampered by playing the other six teams in the
Western Athletic Conference. Mary Huana says she doesn’t like
the idea of basketball officials watching the teams warm-up
Pretty soon, she says, they’ll want to go into the dressing roo
and watch the players dress. Whatever happened to the college
dunk, anyway? McMurry’s basketball team last night had a
Penny and a Nickels in the lineup. The Miners had more folding
money. Woody Hayes of Ohio State made quite a spectacle of
himself in the Bucks’ game with Michigan.
