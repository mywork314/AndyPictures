Wildness,
Errors Hurt
Miner Club

PHOENIX—Coach Andy
Cohen was hopeful today that
his U.T. El Paso Miners have
overcome their awe of the Ari-
zona State’s Sun Devils, per-
ennial power of the Western
Athletic Conference and
national champions.

The Miners, nervous and jit-
tery, committed three errors
in the first inning against the
ASU in a WAC game at Mesa
last night. The Sun Devils
scored and unearned run and
had four more in the third plus
one in the eighth to win 6-1,

COHEN STARTED Harry
Milner against the Sun Devils
in an afternoon game today
and had Marc Bombard ready
for a night battle.

Jim Greggerson had good
stuff for the Miners yesterday
but was wild and issued seven
walks. He was succeeded by
Bill Diesselhorst in the sev-
enth. The Miners scored their
only run in the seventh, Tim
Peden doubled and was driven
in by Juan Sandoval. Craig
Swan, going the distance for
ASU, collected 15 strikeouts.

E.P. Opener
Set Tomorrow;
Haskins Hurls

Everything is set for the
opening of the Sun Kings home
season in the Texas League
tomorrow at Dudley Field.

Don Haskins, U.T. E Paso
basketball coach, will throw |
out the first ball. There’ll be
band music and player intro-
ductions.

Ray Johnston, owner of the
Sun Kings who lives in Dallas,
will be here for the opening.

Starting time for the game is
2:15 p.m.
