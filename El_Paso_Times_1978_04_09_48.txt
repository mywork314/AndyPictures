Devils Rap Miners ‘Twice

By MARK LEIBSON
Times Sports Writer

Arizona State has a way of making opponents
look silly on the baseball diamond — especially
Saturday when the Sun Devils ran over the Univer-
sity of Texas at El Paso in both ends of a
doubleheader.

The Miners failed to come up with a pitcher that
could stop ASU's bats from ripping the cover off the
ball as the Sun Devils took both of the Western
Athletic Conference games 15-3 and 12-6 at Dudley
Field.

“See what pitching can do to ateam?" said Miner
coach Andy Cohen. ‘We couldn't stop them but they
had some pitchers who did a fine job."

Injuries plagued the Miners somewhat. Regular
second baseman Art Bravo was sidelined with a
pulled hamstring and shortstop Danny McMahon
was spiked in the first game and sat out the second
game,

Their absences proved costly, especially in the
nightcap when Orlando Cordova misplayed a two-
out, bases-loaded grounder which eventually led to
five unearned Arizona State runs. UTEP also allow-
ed five unearned runs in the opener.

“We'll be back,"’ said Cohen. ‘We need to get our
injured players back in the lineup.”

Switch-hitting Chris Bando, a spitting image of
his major league brother Sal, rapped a pair of two-
run doubles off the center field wall in the first
game while teammate Steve Michael scored four
runs and knocked home two with his first of two
homers for the day, never allowing the Miners a
chance at staying in the game.

Miner left fielder Danny Haygood was the only
batter to produce runs, scoring Dan Feagler and
Jerry Walker with a fourth inning single and again

sending Feagler home in the sixth with a double to AS

center,

Starting UTEP hurler Jim Sacco took the loss, his
third against three wins. Tom Van Der Meersche,
who walked five and gave up nine hits, went the
seven inning distance for ASU while improving his
record to 5-0,
