Miner Baseballers
Trying to Relax

Andy Cohen’s U.T. El Paso
Miner baseball team today
hoped to relax a little and
piay good baseball.

Cohen said, “I think the
boys need to relax more.
They want to win as bad as
anyone, but it seems the har-
der they try the more errors
they make.”

THE MINERS showed the
desire to win by staying out
two hours after Colorado’s
game yesterday and having
practice. ‘“‘They worked for
two hours on infield and hit-
ting,’”’ Cohen said. “‘And they
were the ones who wanted to
do it.”

The Miners met the Buffs in
the opener and dropped it 13-3
with the Buff’s getting eight
runs in the last inning.

Colorado hits compiled with
some Miner errors was the
difference.

THE MINERS left 12 men
stranded on the bases.

Colorado opened the game
with three runs in the top of
first inning and kept adding
insurance runs which left the
Miners playing catch-up base-
ball.
