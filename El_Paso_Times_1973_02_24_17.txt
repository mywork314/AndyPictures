Miners’
Opener
Today

The University of Texas at
El Paso baseball team, its
opener delayed Friday by wet
grounds at Dudley Field, tries
once again to lift the lid on the
1973 baseball season with a
doubleheader against Hardin-
Simmons.

Miner coach Andy Cohen
will send sophomore Ray
Greggerson to the mound for
the opener against the Cow-
boys, but the long-time U.T. El
Paso mentor was undecided on
the rest of his rotation.

“I'd like to look at them (H-
SU) before I make any deci-
sion on who to pitch the other
game,” Cohen said this week.

Otherwise, the Miners will
start Larry McKay in left
field, Malcolm McLean in cen-
ter, and either Joel Kuenstler
or Eloy Alvarado in right.
Around the infield, it will be
Barry Alburtis at first, Terry
Baker at second, Herb Von
Hagen at shortstop, and Glen
Villareal at third. Tim Peden
will be the catcher.

Following the Hardin-Sim-
mons, series, U.T. El Paso will
host Texas Tech next Friday
and Saturday.
