Ranger In Town For Two

By MARK LEIBSON
Times Sports Writer

Mike Rima, a transfer from
Indian Hills Junior College, has
been given the go-ahead to
start Friday's first game for
the University of Texas at El
Paso against Ranger Junior
College.

The Miners host Ranger for a
four game exhibition series
beginning at 1 p.m. with a
doubleheader at the Ysleta
Cutoff. The two teams will
meet in a twinbill against
Saturday at1p.m.

“Rima’s all-around mound
poise, good delivery and con-
centration is what impresses
me,”’ said Andy Cohen of his
starter who will be filling in for
injured Jeff Hogan who is
suffering a back injury.

“I had planned on starting
Hogan for the first game," said
Cohen, “‘but he came down with
a sore back and it doesn't look
like he'll get to pitch Friday.”

Still two weeks away from
the season opener against
Abilene Christian College on
March Sth, Cohen plans to see
as many of his pitchers during
this weekend series as possible.

“In the first game, I also
want to see how some of my
freshmen do, I'll probably use
Bill Watts, Steve Hernandez
and Gary Kobat,’’ explained
Cohen, “As for the second
game, I'll start Dave Jannusch
and use Joe Dehn and Andy
Grooms in relief.”

Converted football player
Jerry Walker, a punter for the
Miners, is scheduled to start
Saturday's opener but Cohen is
undecided on his second hurler
for the nightcap.

Freshman Terry Goodro will
be behind the plate for Friday's
opener while Mike Miller and
Joe Segapelli will split game
two.

 

“We've had two real good

practices this week,’ said
Cohen of his team’s workouts
leading up to the Ranger
Series. ‘‘Unfortunately, our
first baseman, Keith Dwinell,
hurt his arm while practicing
sliding drills and he may not
Start for us.”

If such is the case, Cohen
plans on using John Hriniak or
Mike Salazar at first. y

Also on the injury list is}
outfielder Terry Dillingham)
with a pulled hamstring. ‘‘It’s|
kind of rough going into these
games with three of my best
players out with injuries but
these are only exhibition
games,’’ said Cohen.

The rest of the lineup has Art
Bravo at second, Glenn
Villarreal playing third and
Dave Patt at shortstop. Making

up the outfielder will be David
Carrillo in center, Keith
Rushing in left and possibly
Dillingham in right.
Outfielder-pitcher Steve Dau
is slated for designated hitter.

“We should have some good
power in the lineup wit!
Rushing, Dau and Glenn
(Villarreal),"’ said Cohen
“Rushing has been hitting the
ball real hard lately.”
