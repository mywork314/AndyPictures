: This Is the End of the LINE

Former Fargoan, George Rulon, the American Legion
baseball director, was in Houston for the college baseball
meetings. He talked with Andy Cohen, former Eau Claire
manager, who was a popular Northern League figure. Andy
is in his fifth year as baseball coach at Texas Western in
his home town of El Paso. George also talked with LeRoy
King, who addressed the Elks high school football banquet
here in November, and Harold (Pinky) Kraft, University of
North Dakota baseball coach and president of the Associa-
tion of North Dakota Amateur Baseball Leagues, who was
named a district director by the college baseball coaches.

Vern Benson, former Winnipeg manager, who was with
Johnny Keane as a coach with the St. Louis Cardinals and
the New York Yankees, was a pallbearer at Keane’s funeral
in Houston Monday. Ancther pallbearer was Clark Nealon,
sports editor of the Houston Post, who was scheduled to
come to Fargo-Moorhead in 1934 with Jack Knight as a
player in the Northern League.

Kenny Stabler, the Alabama football quarterback, was
signed by the New York Yankees as a southpaw pitching
prospect in the free agent draft in June. The Yankees no
longer have a claim to his services, and he may be drafted
by one of the pro football leagues.

The Minnesota State High School League may vodte on
March 17 on a proposal to divide track and field competition
into three classifications.
