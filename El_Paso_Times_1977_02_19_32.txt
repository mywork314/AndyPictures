arly Viiner Leads

By TIM MORRIS
Times Sports Writer

The University of Texas at El Paso
baseball team got off to a couple of
quick leads against Ranger Junior
College here Friday, but in the end it
was the Rangers who made an early
evening of it as they swept the Miners
9-3 and 4-3 in a season-opening
doubleheader.

UTEP grabbed a 3-1 lead in the
second inning of the first game when
catcher Jim Kennedy doubled home
Terry Dillingham, Brad George and
pitcher Wes Zotz. But the Miners
couldn’t hang on as the Rangers
erupted for a six-run inning in the fifth
to overtake the Miners in the first
game played on UTEP’s new field on
the school's campus.

The Miners lost another lead in the
second game but appeared to be
making a comeback of their own in
the sixth inning, trailing 4-3 with the
bases loaded and two out, when the
game was Called because of darkness.

The twoteams meet again Saturday
in another doubleheader beginning at
noon, an hour earlier than Friday’s
starting time.

“T hate like hell to lose to a junior
college,’’ Miner Coach Andy Cohen
said after the second game was called
at about 6:10 p.m. “But that’s what
we did. It looked like we might have a
rally there at the end but rules are
rules. I had agreed to call it at the end
of five but we decided to go ahead and
play one more. We just couldn’t get it
in. We'll start earlier Saturday.”
