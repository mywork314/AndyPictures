FORTY YEARS AGO
April 12, 1928
(Thursday)

Andy Cohen, hometown boy
making good in the big
leagues, was hero of the sea-
son’s opener for the New York
Giants. It was his two-bagger,
with two runners on bases,
that beat Boston 4-2.

Mrs. Mary C. Hills, one of
El Paso’s early pioneers and

aunt of Mrs. Otis Coles, died

yesterday in Grand Rapids,
Mich.

El Paso’s bid for the pro-
posed Eagles’ $1 million
national sanatorium, and the
1929 national convention, was
made yesterday, by E. W.
Kayser, first vice-president of
the Chamber of Commerce.
