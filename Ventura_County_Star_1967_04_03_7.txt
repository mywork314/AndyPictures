Did you know there
was once a major league
baseball manager who
never lost a game?...
His name was Andy Co-
hen and he retired unde-
feated . . . It happened in
1960 when Eddie Sawyer
resigned as manager of
the Phils after the first
game of the season and
Cohen took over for the
next game . . . He won it
... Then he stepped
aside as Gene Mauch
joined the team to_be-
come manager... That
was the only game Cohen
ever managed in the big
leagues and he bowed out
with a perfect record.

nament on the pro golf
tour? ... Answer is
Sammy Snead who set the
record by winning the
Greensboro Open in 1965
at the age of 52,

Giants . . . This was a
team to remember be-

Whitey Lockman, Eddie

Mueller . . . Catcher was
