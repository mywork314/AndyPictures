Gardeners
Tell Plans
For Show

The 12th annual! flower
show, “Jewels of Joy,” spon-
sored by the Desert Hills Gar-
den Club wil! be held from 3 to
5 p.m. tomorrow in the home
of Mrs. Hal Dean Jr. of 904
Thunderbird drive. The public
is invited. A minimum charge
will be made for persons over
12 years of age. Refreshments
will be served.

Entries must be made
between 8 and 10:30 a.m. the
day of the show and are open
to the members of the Desert
Hills Club only.

There will be a special edu-
cational display of written and
illustrated material concerning
phases of garden club work of
national, state, and local clubs
projects. A file of “National
Gardeners” and “Lone Star
Gardeners” as well as books
on horticulture and flower
arranging will be displayed.
Also on view will be garden
tools, plant foods and insecti-
cides. Litterbags will be dis-
tributed.

chairman and Mrs. Charles
Calhoun is cochairman. Other

bury and Edmund Jones, Tally
