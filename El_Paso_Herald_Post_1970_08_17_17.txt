El Paso Herald-Post (El Paso, Texas) - Mon, Aug 17, 1970 - Page 17

EP. Group
Cleans Up
Cemetery

A long, hot day in the sun
spent cleaning up someone
else’s trash is the contribution
made by the Young Marines
Saturday to help beautify El!
Paso.

The First Platoon of the
Dean Hawkins Detachment,
composed of boys from 8 to 17
years of age, turned outina
clean-up detail to tidy the area
east of Concordia Cemetery,
Andy Cohen, president of the
Beautify El Paso Assn., said,

“THAT PLACE was a dis-
grace," Mr. Cohen said. “I
don’t know whose responsibili-
ty it is to maintain the area,
but I should think families
whose loved ones are buried
there would be concerned.”

The Young Marines expect
to take the next several weeks
to get the trash and weeds
cleaned up. ‘‘The Beautify El
Paso Assn. is going to do
everything in its power to help
these young men clean it up,”’
Mr. Cohen said. The lots are
filled with weeds, bushes,
barbed wire, broken bottles,
cans and paper. “I didn’t real-
ize how awful it was until I
took a look at it.’”

The Young Marines, a drill
organization headed by Ex-
Marine Ralph Orner, provided
their own tools and lunches for
the day-long task.

Mr. Cohen appealed to other
groups of young people to
adopt similarly abused city
areas for special clean-up proj-
ects. “‘We need to educate our
citizens to do something about
careless litter besides criticiz-
ing it, as well as make them
conscious about disposing of
trash in proper receptacles.
Pollution has to be stopped. . .
whether it’s in the air, on the
ground or in our minds.”’
