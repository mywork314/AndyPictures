New State National
Bank Holds Tree

Planting Ceremony

El Paso Herald-Post (El Paso, Texas) - Tue, Nov 16, 1971 - Page 3

““TREEBREAKING”—Taking part in ceremonies today at the
State National Bank Plaza were, from the left, Charles

Black, owner of Black’s Nursery; Mrs. Malcolm Welch,

“founder of the Beautify El Pase Assn.; Mrs. Ira Brawley,

president of the El Paso Council of Garden Clubs; and, William

M. Updike, vice president of the marketing division of State
Bank.

National

A “treebreaking” was cele-
brated by the State National
Bank today at its soon-to-open
22-story tower in downtown El
Paso.

The occasion, attended by
beautification and bank offi-
cials, marked the first plant
ing of trees in the Downtown
area other than in San Jacin-
to and Pioneer Plazas, in
many years.

THE LANDSCAPING
around the bank’s plaza
includes 13 10 to 12 foot live
oak trees, a long row of Dwarf
Yaupon Holly plants, and the
‘accenting of entrances at three
sides of the tower with Golden
Euvonymus.

“You have painted a picture
that everyone will enjoy,” said
Mrs. Malcolm Welch, founder
of the Beautify El Paso Assn.,
to William M. Updike, State
National vice president in
charge of the marketing divi-
sion, who introduced guests.

The trees each weigh 1,060
pounds and were lifted intc
place 30 feet apart by a 40-foot
crane. There are nine trees on
the widened Kansas street
side, and two each on both the
Mills and Texas avenues sides.

THE LANDSCAPING, done
by: Black's Nursery, was

@esigned to co-ordinate the tall
structural elements in the
tower, according to James G.
Lewis, registered landscape
architect, who directed the
work for Black’s.

Each of the trees was anc-
hored in a 36-inch ball of spe-
cial earth calculated to keep
the trees healthy as they grow
to a height of 20 feet on the
site, where the Rio Grande
once rolled 10 million years
ago.

Other members of the Beau-
tify El Paso Assn. present
were Andrew H. Cohen, presi-
dent, Mrs. George Kisten-
macher, vice president, and
Mrs. John Jagoe and Mrs.
Cramer Williams, co-chairmen
of the association’s Downtown
Clean-Up Committee.

OFFICIALS OF the El Paso
Council of Garden Clubs pre-
sent were Mrs. Ira Braley,
president, Mrs. Orville L. Mill-
er, past president, and Mrs.
Fred K. Huber and Mrs. Sid-
ney W. Howard, chairmen of
the last garden club show.
There were also bank and
building officers present.

Participants were presened
with the famed engrossments
produced by the late J. R.
Rosen entitled ‘America, the
Beautiful.”
