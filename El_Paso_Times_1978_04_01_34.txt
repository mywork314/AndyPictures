JIM SACCO
-.. aids Miners

By MARK LEIBSON
Tumes Sports Writer

“What else could you ask out of one
ball player?” said University of
Texas at El Paso coach Andy Cohen of
senior Bob Alsup. ‘He did everything
we could have possibly asked.”

Without Alsup, the Miners probably
would not have swept a doubleheader
7-5 and 3-2 from Southern Colorado
Friday at Cruz Blanca Stadium in
Juarez.

First of all, the left-hander pulled a
single to right in the bottom of the
sixth inning in the opener, breaking a
5-5 deadlock. An inning later, Alsup
was called to the mound from center-
field when K.C. Nix, who relieved
Russ Bortell in the third, ran into
trouble.

Alsup, without having time to warm
up like most relievers, retired three
consecutive Indians after Nix had
allowed a runner to reach third base
with no outs.
