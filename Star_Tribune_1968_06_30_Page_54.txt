COHEN, BEJMA TO PLAY
IN OLD-TIMERS GAME

Andy Cohen and Ollie Bejma, two longtime, popular
second basemen of Twin Cities professional baseball, will
be in the starting lineups of the second annual Saints-
Millers Old-Timers Game at Metropolitan
Stadium, Tuesday, July 16.

Cohen, who played for the Millers
from 1932 to 1939 and saw service with
the New York Giants in 1926-28-29, is
now baseball coach of the University of
Texas-El Paso as well as of El Paso in
the Texas League.

Bejma, the American Association’s
Most Valuable Player in 1938 while a
member of the Saints and now an Indiana
state employe, also played with St. Paul Cohen
in 1940-41. Bejma played for the St. Louis Browns from
1934 through 1936 and with the Chicago White Sox in
1939.

Also among the 30 Old-Timers accepting bids were
Gene Kelly, Saints’ pitcher in 1945-46 who is now Bloom-
ington recreation director, and Lefty Johnson, a Miller
hurler-outficider in 1943 who is now employed as a bar-
tender in Minneapoiis.

Boston Red Sox manager Dick Williams, a 1954
Saint, and Boston coach Eclie Popowski, the Millers’ last
manager in 1960, wil! he playing managers of the squads
which will play a one-hour or three-inning game before
the Boston-Minnesota Twins game.
