Athletes Due

Sinai Honor

Fred DeBernardi, Gus
Bailey, and Dick Gibbs will be
in line for honors at the Temple
Mt. Sinai’s 10th annual sports
awards dinner Thursday night.

Dave Wottle. the Munich-

Olympic champion on the 800
meters and participant in
Saturday's El] Paso Pro Track
Classic at Kidd Field, will be
the featured speaker.

The dinner, open to the
public, will also name a
Sportsman of the Year in El
Paso. and honor Dr. Arleigh
Templeton, president of the
University of Texas at El Paso,
for his outstanding con-
tributions to E] Paso and Miner
sports.

Past sportsman of the year
awards have been presented to
Bobby Dobbs. Dnn Haskins,
Saul Kleinfeld, Andy Cohen,
John Phelen. Bob Ingram, and
Wayne Vandenburg.

Also many of the participants
in the EPPTC will be in at-
tendance.

Tickets for the banquet,
scheduled for 7:30 p.m., at the
Temple, will be available at the
door. A social hour is set for
6:30.
