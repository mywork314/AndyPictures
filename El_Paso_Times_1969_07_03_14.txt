Greggerson
| To Attend
U.T. El Paso

Jim Greggerson, All-Dis-
trict hurler for the District
2-AAAA champion Ysleta,
announced Tuesday that he
has signed a letter of intent
to attend University of
Texas at El Paso and play
baseball under coach Andy
Cohen.

Greggerson finished the
1969 season with a 10-4
record and was instrumental
in the Indians’ title driYe.
Greggerson also won the
opening bi-cistrict tilt
against the Eastwood Troop-
ers but ran into trouble in
the second round of the
Class AAAA baseball tourna-
ment and lost two games to
Midland Lee, runners-up in
the state meet.

The 6-foot-5 pitcher was
the only EI Paso baseball
plaver to be drafted in the
June professional baseball
draft but turned down a bid
by the Seattle Pilots of the
American League.
