Special to EL PASO TIMES

SCOTTSDALE, Ariz. — El Paso’s Sun
Kings split a double-bill with the San
Antonio Missions here Sunday as manag-
er Chuck Tanner got good mileage out of
pitchers he is counting on during the
sere League season which opens April
16.

Bill Harrelson, moving up to the Sun
Kings from San Jose, hurled five strong
innings in losing cause in the first game,
won by San Antonio in nine innings, 3-2.
Harrelson struck out nine batters in his
five frames and showed strong right-
handed talent.

Ken Tatum, who has been mentioned
as Tanner’s starter for the Sun King
home opener at Dudley Field April 18,
worked five innings but did not get the
victory as the Kings scored late for a 2-1
advantage. Tanner said he was pleased
with Tatum’s condition, and that the
young Alabaman was “throwing real
strong.”

Jim DeNeff led El Paso’s stickwork,
getting four for six in the two games
including a double and a two-run homer.
He appeared fully recovered from a groin
injury which had sidelined him several
days.

With Sunday’s series finale cancelled
by the Cub organization in tribute to the
death of Dr. Martin Luther King, the
Kings will return to Holtville, Calif., and
resume heavy work Monday. They will
break camp April 14, gathering in Albu-

querque Aug. 15 to prepare for the ope-
ner.
