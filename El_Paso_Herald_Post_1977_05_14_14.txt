Miners beat
Lobos, cinch
winning year

Special to the Heraid-Post

ALBUQUERQUE — Coach Andy Cohen and his
U.T. El Paso baseball team can celebrate a winning
season today after the Miners defeated New Mexico
Lobos here 13-12 yesterday in a Western Athletic

Conference game.

The Miners and Lobos played a doubleheader
today. Regardless of the outcome, UTEP will have
a winning overall season. Going into today’s twin-
bill they were 26-22-1 for the season and 3-13 in WAC

play.

MIKE RIMA was the winner for the Miners yes-
terday. He relieved David Jannusch in the sixth,
and struck out Bartell of the Lobos in the ninth with

the tying run on base.

“They were all heroes,”’ said Cohen of his team.
“We kept coming back in the game. Keith Dwinell,
who hit a 410-foot homer, Bob Alsup, who had four
hits, Jim Bradford, Bill Roberts and Rusty Ken-
nedy were really good with the bat.”

Roberts drove in the winning run for the Miners
in the ninth, Dwinell led off with a double and
scored on Bradford's single. Roberts’ single scored

Bradford from second.

IT WAS THE Miners’ first winning season since
1970 and their first win over New Mexico this sea-

son.

New Mexico, now 30-20 overall and 5-11 in the

WAC, used five pitchers.

The game pitted the Rima brothers against each
other, Randy Rima was at first base for New Mexi-
co. He did not come to bat against Mike Rima, the

Miner pitcher.
