Miners
Split
With Tech

University of Texas at El
Paso baseballers split a dou-
bleheader with Texas Tech’s
Red Raiders Friday, losing the
opener 11-3 but coming back
with freshman Joel Kuenstlar
on the mound to win the night-
cap 2-1.

Action continues with anoth-
er Raider doubleheader Satur-
day, the first tilt set for noon
at the Ysleta cutoff diamond.

Texas Tech got five runs in
the second inning of the first
game. Miner pitcher Phil Dar-
win went three innings giving
up eight runs on five hits. Gil-

bert Rodriguez took over in
the fourth, was tagged with six
runs and relieved by Ernie
Carreon.

Frank Del Toro doubled Joel
Kuenstler home during the
fourth for the Miners. U.T. El
Paso picked up their other two
runs the next inning. John
‘Bickley went all the way for
the Raider win.

The second game was tied
1-1 until the bottom of the
ninth inning when Miner Barry
‘Alburtis took a base-on-balls
then advanced to third on Del
Toro’s single. Second baseman
Terry Baker took the sign
from Miner coach Andy
Cohen, layed down a perfect
suicide squeeze bunt and
Alburtis beat the throw home
‘giving the Miners a 2-1 victo-
ry. Kuenstlar went all the way
on the Miners in his first colle-
giate mound start.
