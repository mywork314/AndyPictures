NEW ORLEANS CLUB RED-HOT .. . If we were asked
the straight question of who has the best club’ in the Southern
Association at the present time, we would have to say New
Orleans. . . . Andy Cohen’s red-hot Pelicans have been the
talk of the league since right before the All-Star game.

Perched next to Montgomery in seventh place on July 22,
the Pelicans have set a terrific pace in the AA Southern
circuit since that time . . . They have won 27 of 36 games
and are now in fourth place, only a game and a half out of
third and only five and a half games back of the leading
Atlanta club. ms

New Orleans opened the season with a bang and they
have had good hitting all season but they sagged when their
pitching fell. In recent weeks the Pirates sent them Curt
Raydon and the mound staff rallied and is now doing a
good job. As a result the Louisiana club is setting a terrific
winning pace...

Cohen has had four good .300 hitters all season in out-
fielders Gail Henley, .321; Emil Panko, .302, and John Pow-
ers, .316, and first baseman Al Grunwald, 324. . . The four
sluggers have accounted for 77 home runs between them... .
Powers currently has 28 and Panko is next with 27 _. .
