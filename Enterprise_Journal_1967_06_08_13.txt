Get in Free Tonight

All who enter Rotary Field tonight for the Jimmy Yawn
Day program and the regularly scheduled Dixie Boys and
Dixie Senior baseball games—the first of which will start at
5:30 p. m.—must have a ticket, with one exception.

The exception is the boy in baseball uniform. Members
of Dixie Youth teams of the area, or of the leagues that nor-
mally play at Rotary Field will get in free.

Tickets are on sale at $1 each by many members of
Rotary and others, and will be sold at the gate at Rotary
Field beginning at about 5 p. m.

Proceeds from the ticket sale go into the Rotary’s pro-
gram for providing baseball for boys 13-17.
