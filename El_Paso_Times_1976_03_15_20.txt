Miners Host Ags For Two

Andy Cohen thinks it’s time for his
“University of Texas at El Paso baseball
team to wake up and begin playing the style
of baseball his players are capable of
playing.

“I’m disappointed with the way the boys
have looked. Our pitching hasn’t come up to
expectations and part of the blame is due to
our poor fielding,’ said Cohen Sunday as he
talked about Monday’s doubleheader with
New Mexico State beginning at 1 p.m. at
the Ysleta Cutoff.

“Our pitching can’t be effective if the
defense makes five or six errors a game,”
Cohen said. ‘‘We just haven't jelled as
much as we should have and I may be
moving some of the boys around.”

One move Cohen has considered could
take place in the catching department
where Terry Goodro and Joe Segapelli
have been sharing the duties.

“T hoped my catching would be improved
this year but it looks like it hasn't. There’s a
chance I might start Joe Dehn who caught
for us last year or maybe Dave Patt,”
explained the Miner coach.

If Cohen decides on moving Patt from his
shortstop’s position to catch balls and
strikes, Louie Morales would fill the gap in
the infield. Dehn, who has been used as a
relief hurler so far, has caught some
practice sessions this year and is readily
available to step in at the request of Cohen.

Dave Jannusch is slated to start the
opener on the mound against the Aggies
while Cohen is yet undecided upon the
second game hurler. “I'll have to wait and
see how we do the first game,” said Cohen.
“It might be Jerry Walker or possibly Gary
Kobatt. He looked pretty good last week
against Denver.”
