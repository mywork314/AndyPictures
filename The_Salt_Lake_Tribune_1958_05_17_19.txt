John Mooney

Tribune Sports Editor

The ripples from the major league cutdown of man power
are passing through the Pacific Coast League now, and it may
be a week or so before they hit. the Pioneer League.

Until that time, the players of questionable status will be
fighting it out, never sure some player will be sent down from

higher classification to bump them to a lower league.

Andy Cohen, when he was managing Denver, was recalling
his experiences in those desperate days of the approaching cut-
down, Several writers were sitting around the dugout and An-
drew was expounding thusly:

“I.WAS up for a cup of coffee with the old Giants under
John McGraw, and I was sweating it’ out, trying to stick in the
big show. The more nervous I became, the more it showed in my
playing. One day I had a bad time on pop flies and when I came
to the bench I mentioned to Mr. McGrgw that the terrible air
current was doing-things and making me misjudge pop flies. I
mentioned whirlwinds, but Manager McGraw snapped, ‘Those
aren't wWhirlwinds, son, those are trade winds and you're leaving
for Minneapolis tomorrow.’ ”
