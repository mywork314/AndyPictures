Owe Mans Opinion

Incompetent Umpiring Raises
Furor Around the Southern

wp 4 RAYMOND JOHNSON

Frank Girard, grandpappy of
Prexy Charlie Hurth’s Blind Toms,
blew a play in the first game in
Sulphur Dell Sunday and it cost
Matt Batts, Nashville catcher...
Matt was put out of the game for
disputing the decision and chances
are that Girard recommended a
suspension to his boss for Matt's
actions.

Girard will never admit he blew the play...
But pictures made by one of this paper’s .photogra-
phers leave no doubt about it... Dick Barone ran out
of the base paths in attempting to elude Batts, who in-
sisted that he touched the speedy Pelican... Barone
never touched home plate, either.

Bill Melvin, Girard’s partner, and Frank were in
hot water on more than one occasion during the two
games, just as they were the previous Sunday here
... Andy Cohen, New Orleans manager, wasn’t happy
over several calls... Neither was Phil Page, Birming-
ham’s skipper, the previous Sunday.
