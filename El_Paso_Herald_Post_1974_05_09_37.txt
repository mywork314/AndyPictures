Two Victories

Will Put Smile
On Andy’s Face

Concluding his 12th season as baseball coach at the
University of Texas at El Paso, Andy Cohen is just three
games away from a summer vacation. It does not come as too
much of a surprise to the still vigorous skipper that
tomorrow and Saturday's season ending series with the
University of New Mexico will determine just what type of
summer it will be

“I knew a month ago that our season would come down to
this series,” Cohen says. “We've won 24 games this year and
none of these boys can walk away ashamed. I would,
however, like to finish with . winning mark and at least tie
my school record for most victories in a season, 26 (set in
1970).”

Andy will open with righty Roy Stringer at noon

WHILE PITCHING stats on the Miner squad resemble
quarterly profits for Exxon, Cohen's offense has inflicted an
equal amount of damage on the opposition. In straight
figures the Miners have actually outhit their opposition in 48
games, though by the narrowest of margins — .2979 to .2974.
Hits don’t mean it all however, UTEP foes have outscored
the Orange 360-284 primarily because Miner hurlers have
issued 264 bases on balls to become the walking man’s best
friends.

“We've got a good club that has done a sound job this
year,” Andy observes. “Nothing would please me more than
to close out at home with at least two wins. Maybe we'll need
some luck but we're due some. We'll just have to wait and
see.”
