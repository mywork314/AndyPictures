Miner BB Opener
In Two Weeks

Two weeks remain before the
University of Texas at El Paso
baseball squad begins the 1975
season and already Coach
Andy Cohen has laid the burden
of the Miners’ success on the
pitching staff.

“The key to our whole
season will be the pitchers and
how well they come around for
us,"” explained Cohen after he
sent his Miner diamondmen
through an intra-squad game
this week.

As for U.T. El Paso's overall
play, Cohen said, “‘I’m satisfied
with the team as of this
moment, but we need a lot of
work. Our hitting is our main
concern. We also have to work
hard on fundamentals.”

Cohen praised the per-

formances of pitchers Bob
Knox, Wrotell Modlenski and
Dave Jannusch, a sophomore,

during U.T. El Paso’s workout
at Hugo Meyer Field.

During Wednesday's game,
the Bandits blanked the
Muggers (Cohen's names of his
divided squad) behind junior
Keith Rushing's long homerun
over the right field fence with a
teammate aboard.
