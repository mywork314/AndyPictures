ASU outlasts Miners

By JAY COLEMAN

TEMPE — Texas-E] Paso coach
Andy Cohen may be an “old dog” in
the coaching ranks of the Western
Athletic Conterence, but Saturday after-
noon he and his Niners refused to play
dead.

When Arizona St. took a 92 lead
after six innings, UTEP came to life in
the seventh inning for five runs and
made a game out of what started out
to be an all too-familiar ASU rout.

But senior reliever Dale Hrovat came
on in the seventh to pitch the Devils
out of trouble and A-State went on to
take the first game of a Southern
Division doubleheader, 11-8.

The teams met again Saturday night
in the finale.

“Tt wasn’t as bad as it looked,’ ASU
coach Jim Brock said. “We had a
couple of defensive lapses and weren't
stinging the ball well, but overall it
was a pretty good effort.”

An open house displaying the new
Arizona State Activity Center will
be held Monday at 7:30 p.m. The
public is invited to attend the event
which will be preceded by a short
dedication ceremony and feature a
tour of the new $9-million facility.
