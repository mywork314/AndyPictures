ONE GAME, ONE WIN

Andy Cohen, above, managed the Philadelphia
Phillies for one game—Thursday night—and won
while the club awaited for Gene Mauch who takes
the helm as a replacement for Eddie Sawyer who
resigned. The Phils beat Milwaukee 5-4. The Braves
got revenge Saturday 13-3 in Mauch’s first game as
manager. Cohen, a Phils’ coach, managed at Eau
Claire several years ago—Unifax.
