Around El Paso

By RUBY BURNS

Miss Shelley Agatha Scott and her parents, Mr. and Mrs.
Louis Scott Jr. will go to Lubbock to spend the weekend with
friends as they start Shelley on her way to Stephens College,
Columbia, Mo., where she will be a freshman. Shelley, a
graduate of Austin High School, will go from Lubbock to
Amarillo to ride the train from there with a group of other
girls en route to Stephens.

Another EI Paso girl going to Stephens
is Miss Marina Cohen, daughter of Mr. and
Mrs. Andrew H. Cohen. Marina, a sopho-
more, will leave later for college.

*x* * x

Miss Susan Funkhouser will arrive
Thursday from Ocean City, N.J., where she
has spent the summer. She will visit her
parents, Mr. and Mrs. Hugh Funkhouser,
6108 Pinehurst, and will leave Sept. 16 for
the American University, Washington, D.C.,
to enroll in the Semester-in-Washington pro-
gram. She will be returning to Randolph-Macon Woman’s Col-
lege, Lynchburg, Va., later in the year.

Ruby Burns
