To Denver University

Denver University scored all
its runs in the sixth inning
‘Thursday and went on to defeat
the University of Texas at El
Paso Miners 3-1 at Dudley
Field. ‘

Paul Steimac started the

pioneer outburst with a single.
Bob Tarvin followed with a
triple to the base of the cen-
terfield wall, driving in
Steimac. A double by Monte
Aspden chased Tarvin in to
score, making the tally 2-0..
. Then Miner coach Andy
Cohen pulled starting pitcher
Dave Jannusch and inserted
Jeff Hogan to quell the rally.

Hogan walked Carl Renn to
put men at first and second.
Jim Flynn put down a bunt
which Hogan fielded and threw
to third attempting a forceout.
The throw was late and the
Miners faced a bases-loaded,
no-out situation.

Denver second baseman

Glenn Schwab grounded to

third baseman Glenn Villarreal
who threw to the plate for a
forceout. The next Denver
batter hit a ground ball to
shortstop David Nance who
failed to make the play. Renn
scored on the error upping the
margin to 3-0. Hogan retired
the next batter to end the in-
ning.

Until the Pioneers got to
Jannusch in the sixth, he had
yielded only three hits and was
in serious trouble only once.

In the fourth, Denver loaded
the bases but came away
empty. The Miner starter, now
0-2, also chalked up five
strikeouts.

Jannusch’s mound opponent,
John Blair, hurled seven in-
nings of four-hit, shutout ball
before being charged with an
earned run in the eighth frame.
Blair, 1-0, picked up the win
with relief help.

In the Miner eighth, Herb
Von Hagen and Mark Miller

received bases on ball.
Villarreal was retired on a fly
to left. Bob Downing brought in
the sole Miner run of the day
with a single.

After two outs in the ninth,
the Miners loaded the bases
with singles by Joe Pennies,
Jim Brundage and a walk to
Von Hagen. Doug Elliott came
in to pitch to Miller and retired
the Miner batter on a ground
ball te first to end the game.

Pennies paced the Miner
offensive attack with three hits
in four trips. He and Downing
lead the regulars with .333
averages. Villarreal and Miller
round out the .300-plus hitters
with .306 and .300 respectively.

The Miners host Denver
again at 2 p.m. Friday in a
single game at Dudley Field.
