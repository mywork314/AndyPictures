Staggering Blow

THE WORD SPREAD. Defeat of the Dallas Cowboys for
the NFL championship and the Longhorns’ loss in the Cotton
Bowl are almost too much for Texas to bear. Bear Bryant
hasn’t had any luck in Alabama’s last half dozen or so bowl
appearances. The trouble with those bowls is that they
haven't supplied any water for Bear to walk on. Performance
_of Roosevelt Leaks in the Cotton Bowl was under par and the
play of Heisman Trophy winner John Cappelletti in the
Orange wasn’t outstanding. New UTEP head football coach

_Gil Bartosh and his aides were quick to use the Sun Bowl as
a recruiting leverage. They called 35 prospective UTEP
players and told them to watch the telecast of the game here
. between Auburn and Missouri. Most of the players called
.back and said they enjoyed watching the game. If Bartosh
did not mention it to the players, he probably inferred that
they might be playing in the Sun Bowl some day if they came
to UTEP. The Boston team in the World Tennis League is
“called the Lobsters. Lobs for short. What they’re doing now:
‘Buck Fausett, former El Pasoan who was a teammate of
Andy Cohen at Minneapolis in the American Assn., and
managed and operated clubs in Albuquerque and Amarillo,
- and who now lives at College Station, Texas, will be honored
“by the Panhandle Athletic Hall of Fame at Amarillo
tomorrow. And did you hear that St. Bernard dogs will be
used now to carry cans of gasoline to desperate and freezing
motorists?
