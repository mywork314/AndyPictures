Of Miners—

S! Seen
As Best
Opponent

Coach Andy Cohen’s U.T.
El Paso Miner baseball team
will have its work cut out for
it Wednesday when the Min-
ers meet Southern Illinois
University at Dudley Field.

“This is probably the finest
team that we have ever
faced,” Cohen said. “They've
just in a double header from
Oklahoma University and they
finished second in the nation
last year to UCLA.”

FIRST BASEMAN Larry
Currie and right fielder John
Miller are the only undecided
starters for Wednesday’s
contest. Miller had an infec-
tion before last week's series
with Northern Arizona and
Currie became ill after Fri-
day’s game.

Saturday the Miners were
the victims of a lucky break.
Northern Arizona had them
down 6-2 going into the fourth
inning. The game was called
due to blinding snowstorm.

“They were the best hitting
ball club we have faced so
far.” Cohen said. “It was a
lucky break for us and a bad
one for them.”’ The snow-out
kept the Miners severn game
win streak alive.
