Miner 9
Will Clash
With Lobos

The University of Texas at
E! Paso baseball team will
meet New Mexico University
tomorrow at 2:30 p.m. in the
first of a two game series at
Dudley Field. The second
game is slated for Saturday at
the same time and place.

Marc Bombard or Harry
Milner is expected to get the
nod for the opener. Both had
fine first outings last week
with Milner winning a 3-0 deci-
sion and Bombard dropping a
close 1-0 outing.

IF EITHER of the two start-
ers, get into trouble coach
Andy Cohen has Jim Gregger-
son, Don Wilson or Bill Disel-
horst in relief.

Cohen’s nine will have to get
the wood on the ball if they
expect to better their 2-2 pre-
sent record. Last week they
had early inning rallies, but
couldn’t materialize the runs.

Although the Miners will be
meeting a Western athletic
Conference team the game
won’t count on the conference
record. But it will be a pre-
view of things to come for both
teams.
