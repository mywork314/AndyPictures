Miners Open
Diamond

Set Today

Ready or not, University of
Texas at El Paso peels the
cover off of the 1970 baseball
season at Dudley Field Friday
and Saturday with a four-
game series.

Coach Andy Cohen, still not
ready to pick his regular line-
up, will send his Miners out
against Odssa Friday and
Ranger Saturday. The Miners
will be involved in two games
both afternoons.

The infusion of new blood,
Cohen hopes, should give the
Miners a big boost as they
tune up for their second season
in the Western Athletic Confer-
ence.

Cohen is hopeful that such
transfers as Bob Prokopowicz,
an outfielder who joined the
Miners after University of
Albuquerque dropped athletics
last spring, Brian Harper,
from Odessa, and Gary Mol-
berg, also of Odessa, can give
the Miners a boost.

Jim Greggerson, a 6-foot-6
frosh from Ysleta, has shown
signs of being capable of giv-
ing the Miners some badly
needed pitching strength.

Cohen’s pitching staff, no
doubt, will be headed by south-
paw Marc Bombard, a hard
dealing junior from El Paso,
and Harry Milner, a right
hander from Kilgore.

Returning for his senior sea-
son will be John Miller, who
can patrol the outfield or work
at first base, as well as Rick
Butler, a good hitting outfield-
er and Juan Sandoval, a good
fielding second baseman with
three years of experience.
