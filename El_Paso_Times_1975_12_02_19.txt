Baylor's Teaff Speaks
At Lions Club Banquet

Baylor Coach Grant Teaff will be the featured speaker for the El
Paso Downtown Lions Club second annual Dinner with the Miners at
6:30 p.m. Wednesday at the Civic Center.

Jim Phillips will emcee the event with Bill Latham giving the
invocation and Andy Cohen the benediction.

University of Texas at El Paso Coach Gil Bartosh will present
awards to the players selected by Miner team members as this year’s
best offensive back, offensive lineman, defensive back and defensive
lineman. A spirit award will also be given to the player showing the
most spirit.

Tickets are available from any Downtown Lions Club member,
Circle K Store or Savings and Loan Institution.

Tickets will not be sold at the door.
