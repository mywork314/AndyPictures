Cohen Signs,
Completes Phil
Coaching Staff

The Phillies announced today
that Andy Cohen, former major
league second baseman and minor
league manager, has been signed
as infield and first base coach for
the 1960 season.

The Phils’ coaching staff is now
completed, it was revealed by
Manager Eddie Sawyer. Held over
from last season are Ken Silvestri
and Dick Carter. John Riddle and
Tom Ferrick were dropped from
the staff.

“We're happy to get Cohen,” de-
clared general manager John
Quinn. ‘‘He will be a valuable ad-
dition because of his rare skills
as an infield instructor.”

Cohen, 55, broke into organized
baseball in 1925, and from 1926
through ’29 was the regular sec-
ond sacker for the New York Giants
He began managing in 1939, and

o|has worked in the farm systems

of the Dodgers, Braves, Pirates,
White Sox and Yankees.

In 1959, he piloted the Yankees’
Denver farm to the finals of
the American Association playoffs.
Last season, Cohen dropped out of
the game to go into private busi-
ness in El Paso, Tex., where he
makes his home.
