Prep Baseball 3

Irvin
Hits
Stride

Ysleta looked ahead, Bel Air
didn’t and man you oughta see
those “‘Batgirls”’ at Irvin!

Bel Air gained a two game
advantage in District 2-AAAA
yesterday by trouncing El
Paso High 5-1 while Bowie’s
Juan Sanchez four-hit second
place Ysleta, 2-1. The 1-AAAA
race was knotted up when
Irvin roasted Eastwood 9-2
while Burges fell 2-0 to south-
paw Ricky Baca and the
Andress squad.

“This makes it a new ball
game,” Rockets’ coach Bob
Berryhill stated afterward.
“We're hitting the ball well
and our defense is showing up
every game. Bobby Rodriguez
(the Rockets starter) has been
pitching with a sore shoulder
but he’s doing a great job for
us. We have to play them one
at a time from here.”

That would have been a
great lesson for Ysleta. The
Indians werent looking ahead
as much as Bowie’s Sanchez
was looking down their
throats, however. Two first
inning runs were all the Bears’
righthander needed in posting
his initial victory in district
play after one loss.

BEL AIR got its normal
dose of nine hits which Bobby
Hernandez made more than
enough with his five-hitter
over El Paso. The Clan
stomped the plate twice in the
fifth, and again two times in
the seventh for the win. El
Paso’s only run came on a
fourth inning ‘tater’ by right-
fielder Charl.e Campos.

Coronado tripped Austin, 7-5
behind Hank Cohen. The Thun-
derbirds used a five run fifth
to ice the game.

Adnress’ Baca survived a
shaky start and then tamed 1-
AAAA power Burges on four
hits at the Mustangs’ field.
The Eagles scored twice after
two were out in the fifth when
shortstop Steve Burciaga
couldn’t find the handle ona
groundball. Doug Goodwin suf-
fered the loss.
