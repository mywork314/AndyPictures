Ags Hand Miners
Cut In Confidence

New Mexico State was certainly not
what the doctor prescribed for
building the University of Texas at El
Paso’s confidence for upcoming
Western Athletic Conference play.

The Aggies not only stopped the
Miners Tuesday in a doubleheader 9-1
and 6-5 at Dudley Field but left UTEP
wondering where its hitting and
fielding went suddenly.

.NMSU's Kevin Stegar hurled a
five-hitter in the opener, retiring 10
Miners in a row before UTEP’s Art
Bravo singled to lead off the sixth and
eventually scored on Bob Alsup’s
single to right.

By then. though, it was too late as
the Aggies had scored all nine of their
Tuns in handing UTEP its worse
defeat of the season.

“Every ball we tagged, they made a
good defensive job of getting to the
ball,'’ said Miner coach Andy Cohen.
“Stegar had good velocity on his pit-
ches.”

-NMSU's bats began to key off on
previously undefeated Wes Zotz in the
first inning when John Clark, one of
the top hitters in the nation with a 460
average, homered over the left-center
field fence after Elliott Chapman had
walked with two outs.

Four Aggie singles and a hit batter
produced three runs in the fourth and
New Mexico State added four more in
the sixth, two of which were unearn-
ed.

Bravo was the only UTEP batter to
get past second base the entire game.
