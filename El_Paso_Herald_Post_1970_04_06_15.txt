Lefthanders
Will Open
WAC Play

Lefthander Marc Bombard
will get the starting nod when
Andy Cohen’s U.T. El Paso
Miner baseball team opens
Western Athletic Conference
play against the University of
Arizona Friday at Dudley
Field.

On Friday the Miners have a
single game beginning at 2
p.m., and then doubleheader
Saturday starting at 1 p.m.
For the doubleheader Cohen
will go with his other two lef-
ties, Harry Milner and Jim
Greggerson.

COHEN hopes that the
team’s leading hitter Bob Pro-

pokowicz will be ready for
action, In Friday’s double-
header with Highlands Univer-
sity Propokowicz injured his
leg sliding into base and had to
sit out Saturday’s encounter.

In a single game Saturday
game the Miners again unload-
ed the wood on Highlands, tak-
ing a 10-3 decision behind the
pitching of Greggerson.

“T only hope we can keep
hitting the way we did last
weekend,” said Cohen. “The
boys are really hot with bat
now.”

THIS WEEK Cohen will
have the Miners working on
defensive play. ‘“‘We need to
iron out some defensive prob-
lems,”’ he said.

The Miners rapped out 18
hits against Highlands and
Greggerson held their bats
silent all except for one inning
where they hit the ball a few
times using some Miner errors
to score.
