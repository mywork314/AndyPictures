Win in Association
By THE ASSOCIATED PRESS

Manager Andy Cohen hasn't
gotten around to feeding his crew
tranquilizer pills yet, but the way
the Denver Bears have been play-
ing, it might not be a bad idea.

The American Assn’s second
place team has been involved, in
one-run or extra-inning affairs the
last five games, winning only two.

The Bears pulled one out Tues-
day night against Louisville, win-
ning 5-4 with three runs in the
last of the ninth, That kept them
one game behind front-running
Charleston, a 7-5 winner over
Omaha in 10 innings. Defending
champion Wichita moved within
a half-game of third place Minnea-
polis, downing the Millers 5-2.
Indianapolis whipped St. Paul by
the same score.
