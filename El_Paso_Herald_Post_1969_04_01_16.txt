Milner Pitches
Best UTEP Game

By MIKE PEMBERTON

Southpaw Harry Milner who
transfered from Odessa Jun-
ior College was beginning to
pay dividends today after the
best pitching performance in
his career at U.T. El Paso for
Andy Cohen.

Milner, who is one of the
few baseball players on full
UTEP. scholarship, came
through with superb control
and a curve ball that subdued
the Oklahoma Sooners 2-1 in

the second game of a twin bill
at Dudley Field. Oklahoma
won the opener 5-2.

AFTER TODAY’S game the
Miners will be idle until April
9 and Sooners will be back on
the road.

Milner’s performance can
be seen in the statistics as
well as in the comments of
both coaches. He limited the
Sooners to four hits and sent
12 cf them back to the dugout
on third strikes.

would come in with a fast
ball.”

Coach Enos Semore of Okla-
homa said, ‘‘Milner had good
control, used his curve when

down which made us hit on
the ground.”’

The Oklahoma ccach, who

some of the giants in colllege
baseball, Texas, Arizona, and
Arizona State, was asked to
make a comparison in the
pitching of these schools and
the Miners.

“THE BIG difference is
they are bigger and stronger.
The curve that Milner had
was not any better than some
of the bigger schools’ pitch-
ers, but he used it well. With
Milner and another pitcher
like him they could surprise
anybody they play.”

The maior play in the

Murphy hit to the second
