Visitors Cop Suspended Game

By JACK DOANE

Assistant

Sports Editor

The New Orleans Pelicans completed a 4-2 victory of
a suspended game over the Montgomery Rebels last night,
then rain washed out the scheduled regular game in the

bottom of the first inning.

The washout thus stacked up another doubleheader

on Manager Steve Souchock’s al-
ready overworked mound staff.
The Pels and Rebs will meet in
three straight twin bills starting
tonight. The first tilt tonight will
start at 7 p.m. at Paterson Field
instead of the usual 6:30 starting
time.

Manager Andy Cohen of the
Pelicans said last night that
Chuck Churn (5-6) and Hy Co-
hen (6-6) will be the probable
starters tonight for the invaders.
Manager Steve Souchock is ex-
pected to use Jim Russell, new
Reb hurler, in the first game to-
night for the Rebs and Chuck
Fowler in the second.

hander Bill Voiselle’s second pitch
last night gave the Pels an insur-
