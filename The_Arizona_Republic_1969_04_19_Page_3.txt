Dolinsek HRs

spark A-State

in 5-0 victory

By DAVE HICKS

MESA — John Dolinsek is an Arizona
State junior majoring in recreation.

One of his favorite methods of it must
be hitting home runs off Texans.

The Sun Devil right fielder smashed a
pair last night, driving in three runs as
A-State and southpaw Larry Gura
blanked Texas - El Paso, 5-0

GURA’'s LTH victory without defeat
handed ASU a one - game margin in the
Western Athletic Conference's southern
race.

Coach Bob Winkles’ club tries for a
series sweep today in contests at 1 p.m.
(Tempe) and 7:30 p.m. (Mesa’s Rendez-
vous).

Winkles tabbed Lerrin LaGrow (7-1)
and Ken Hansen (6-1), while the Miners’
Andy Cohen will go with Mare Bombard
(5-2) and Lynn Hunt (2-3).

“HE MUST BE trying to make up for
Tucson,” said Winkles of Dolinsek, the
6-2, 180-pounder from Novato, Calif.

Dolinsek was 1-for-14 in last weekend’s
ASU — Arizona series in Tucson, but he
amassed eight bases with two swings of
the bat last night.

His first wallop was a first - pitch
offering by loser Harry Milner (4-5),
who had walked leadoff man Lenny
Randle in the opening inning.

DOLINSEK HAD TO work harder for
No. 2, which also cleared the right field
barrier, in the third inning. He unloaded
that one on Milner's fifth pitch.

When it was over, Dolinsek had upped
his RBI mark to 30 and tied Paul Ray
Powell for the club homer leadership
with seven. '

Gura struck out 13 Miners and in one
stretch retired 13 in a row, but without
Dolinsek he might have experienced a
tighter squeeze.

ASU, outhit 5-4, had to scrape up its
last two runs on Randle’s sacrifice fly in
the seventh and Rick Valley's fielder’s
choice in the eighth.
