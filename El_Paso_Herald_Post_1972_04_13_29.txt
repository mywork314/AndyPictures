40th Season

Is Under Way

For Forti Teams

Andy Cohen and Sal Berro-
teran formed the batter for
the formal opening ceremonies
of Forti Baseball League’s
40th year at Hugo Meyer Field
Sunday.

Lara Vets of Las Cruces
beat El Paso 40-60 Club, 11-4
and La Mesa Bluejays nosed
Rebels Club 7-4.

Mrs. Carlos (Lorenza) Gon-
zales, widow of the late Carlos
Gonzalez, pitcher for Forti’s
was selected to reign as Queen
Of the “‘Old Pros’’.

_Max Lopez, Frank Campos,
Salo Sandoval, Rudy Carbajal,
Beto Mendez, Chulanga Gonza-
lez Sr. Manuel Villanueva and
Leo Forti were honored for
