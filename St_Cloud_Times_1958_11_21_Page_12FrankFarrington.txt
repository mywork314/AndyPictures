SORT OF SAD also is the announced retirement forever from
baseball of Andy Cohen, one-time Eau Claire manager. He is now
going into the suds business in his home town of El Paso—and
his friends, of course, hope bad hops do not shorten his new
career.

John Orsino, Rox catcher of 1958, who bids fair to becoming a
big leaguer, is the owner of a record which we will avoid men-
tioning at every future opportunity. John had 37 passed balls
last summer, according to final official Northern league statistics.
It ‘eis a new record, bettering a mark set in 1936 by Gus Hixon
of kargo-Moorhead. Good old Gus got charged with 34.

Ernie Christoff, an all-star pitcher with Eau Claire in the
Northern league last season, who descended all the way from
A to join the Braves, is now in that new Florida “instructional”
legaue. There he has won six and lost none. When he was here
last summer I didn’t thing he needed any more instruction. He
was awful tough.
