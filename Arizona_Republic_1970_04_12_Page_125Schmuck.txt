By BOB JACOBSON
Republic Sports Writer
MESA — Bobby Winkles
should have been an author.
His Arizona State Sun Dev-
ils, beaten 4-1 by New Mexico
Friday and performing like
mild-manner Dr, Jekyl,
turned Mr. Hyde yesterday
and stomped the Lobos in a
doubleheader, 7-2 and 10-3.

The sweep gave the unpre-
dictable Devils, now 21-13 on
the season, a 2-1 mark in the
Western Athletic Conference's
southern division and a share
of first place with Arizona.
New Mexico fell to 19-9 and
1-2 respectively.

Sophomore hurlers Craig
Swan (6-2) and Jim Crawford
(5-5) notched the victories,
but Sun Devil hitters, batting
-253 as a team before this se-
Ties, made the big splash.

A-State, held to three hits
Friday by UNM’s Jim Krem-
mel, broke loose for 22 hits —
12 in the first game (all sin-
les).

Roger Schmuck, the top
Devil hitter at .331, again led
ASU at the plate, smashing
four RBI singles along with a
sacrifice fly. Lenny Randle
also had four hits for the day,
including a triple in the sec-
ond game.

Jeff Osborn continued his
assault on WAC pitching,
going 3-for-9 to give him five
hits for the series, while Tom
Welton added a home run and
contributed several sparkling
plays afield.

Swan and Crawford, who
both went the distance, took
Opposite routes to their wins.

Swan started strongly, al-
lowing only two hits the first
four innings, but struggled
later as the Lobos got to him
for 10 hits.

“T really didn’t fecl that
good out here,” Swan said.
“My slider was nothing and
my fast ball wasn’t moving
but I could get it over for
strikes, I got a little tired too,
but of course it always helps
to have a five - run lead.”
