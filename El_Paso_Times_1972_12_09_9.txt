UNVEIL PLAQUES — United States and Mexican federal and local
authorities and representatives of civic and historical organizations of
the two countries Friday dedicated a Historical Park at International
Boundary Monument No. 1 located on the west bank of the Rio Grande.
Shown unveiling the bilingual plaques are, from left, Mayor Mario Jaquez of
Juarez; Prof. Armando Chavez, president of the Juarez Historical Society;
and Andy Cohen, president of Beautify El Paso Association. Leon Metz,
president of the El Paso County Historical Society, is behind Cohen.
