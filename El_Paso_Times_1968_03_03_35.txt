A Welcome Surprise Visitor

An extremely pleasant surprise this
past week was the appearance of George
Genovese at the annual Father-Son
Sportsmanship dinner of the Temple Mt.
Sinai Men’s Club. George is the little fel-
low who was first manager of the El
Paso Sun Kings when John Phelan and
Co. returned El Paso to organized base-
ball in 1961. That year the Kings were the
San Francisco Giant affiliate in the Soph-
omore League with Genovese moving his
club from Artesia, N.M., to E] Paso.

The following year when the Kings
advanced to the Texas League George
was again the manager and produced a
championship team. But it was his 1963
team that really sticks in memory, That
was the lineup of power sluggers which
shattered the Texas League home run
record.

George was the first man named
Sportsman of The Year by the Men's
Club, and when he received an invitation
to attend this year’s function he took time
off from his scouting duties with the
Giants to fly to El Paso. It was a happy
decision, because Andy Cohen, his former
assistant with the Sun Kings, was the sur-
prise honoree.

It was a great personal pleasure to
have the chance to visit with the scrappy
little guy who is a big part of baseball.
