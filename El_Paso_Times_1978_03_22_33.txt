Zotz Gets The Breaks
With Changeup Pitch

By MARK LEIBSON
Times Sports Write

For years, Wes Zotz never knew a
changeup existed in his arsenal of pit-
ches. He had a proven slider and a ‘‘pret-
ty good” fast ball and of course the
slider, but that was it

Toward the end of last year’s season,
the University of Texas at E] Paso
righthander was sitting in the dugout one
day with catcher Sal Flores. Flores, who
had originally had been an outfielder,
suggested to Zotz the possibility of
throwing a breaking pitch

Zotz went home to San Antonio and
worked on the pitch in a summer
baseball league. The results have been
outstanding

So far during the 1978 season, Zotz has
been a giant killer on the mound for the
Miners.

His secret has not been an overpower-
ing fast ball or sharp slicing curve. “It's
my changeup which has helped me this
year,” explained the 165-pound senior
who already has five victories to his
credit without defeat. ''I don’t have the
physica! size to finesse them so I really
started concentrating on my changeup."

So far, Zotz has been one of the few pit-
chers for Cohen who can doit all.

Last Friday night, knowing he was in
line to start Saturday's game against
Denver University, Zotz went up to
Cohen after the Miners had gotten
themselves in a serious jam and told the
UTEP coach he felt strong enough to go
in if Cohen needed him

After the Pioneers rallied from a 6-0
deficit to tie the game in the top of the
ninth with runners on second and third

and one out, Cohen went with his 5-for-9
starter in relief.

Zotz responded with back-to-back
strikeouts and then sat on the bench
while Rick Perez slugged a run-scoring
double in the bottom of the ninth to give
Zotz his fourth win of the season

Saturday, Zotz went to the mound and
threw nine innings of shutout ball His
fifth victory came during UTEP’s 15th
game of the year. As junior, Zotz’ fifth
win wasn’t recorded until the Miners’

““4 lot of my success has to do with the
team I have playing behind me,” said
Zotz. “Last year, | felt like I had to do it
all myself. We had outfielders dropping
balls but this year, we have guys out
there who have speed and can catch the
ball. This year, I just have to worry
about putting the ball over the plate. We
have outstanding defense and a bunch of
depth at all the infield positions and in
the outfield. Those guys in the outfield
are as quick as cats.’

Following Saturday's 9-0 win, Zotz’
