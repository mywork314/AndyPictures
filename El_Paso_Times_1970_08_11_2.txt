Three More
In Contest
For UF Title

Three more entries have
been received in the Miss
United Way contest; an annual
search by the United Fund for
a young miss to represent the
UF during its upcoming cam-
paign.

Bonnie Alene Stoepel with
American Airlines, Sandy
Stone with C. H. Leavell and
Co. and Mary Elizabeth Flores
with Southwest National!
Bank have submitted their
entry forms along with a black
and white picture to United
Fund office.

To -date, five entries have
registered for the contest to be
held August 28 at 7:30 p.m. in
the Festival Theater on Mon-
tana. Charlie Tupper, chairman
for the contest, said, “deadline
for entry in his contest is

August 16. Those planning on

representing their company in
this annual event should mail
their applications to Mrs. Fer-
guson at the UF office.”’
