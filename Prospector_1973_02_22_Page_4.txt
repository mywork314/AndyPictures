Doubleheader opens baseball season

With 11 returning lettermen
from last year’s squad, Andy
Cohen will be trying to produce a
successful UT El Paso baseball
team this eason. Coach Cohen is
looking to improve on last year’s
record.

The Miners will start the season
tomorrow afternoon at | p.m.
with a double header against
Hardin Simmons University at
Dudley Field. On Saturday the
two teams clash again in another
doubleheader at_|_ p.m.

THE 11 returnees who will head
this year’s team are Skip Baker,
Joe Pennies, Lali Maldonado,
Erie Hernandez, Tim Peden,
Barry Alburtis, Malcolm McLean,
Bobby Knox, Ray Greggers on,
Larry Alavarado and Joe
Kuentsler.

Cohen will also have some top
junior college transfers in Herbie
Von Haggen from Cochise and
Dave Wade from Ranger. Von
Haggen is an infielder and Wade is
a pitcher.

The Miners will also be counting
on Mark Miller, a transfer from
Regis College and Glen Villareal
ofEastwood High School in Fl
Paso. Both are infielders and
should help UT El Paso’s defense.

THERE ARE seven pitchers
competing for a spot on the
regular rotation. Along with Wade,
Cohen has Jeff Hogan, Mike
Miller, Tony Eaton, — Larry
Richardson, Bob Downing and
Mike Krute.
