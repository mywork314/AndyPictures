Colonel Quiz No. 6

O.K., Smarties!
Try This One

You baseball fans are too smart so we've toughened
up Colonel Quiz No. 6 for you. Here's the problem.

Louisville is leading 3-2 at the Stadium with one out in |
the ninth but Denver has Curt Roberts on third, Zeke Bella
on second and John Blanchard on first and Vern Rapp at bat.
Rapp lifts a high fly and umpire Bernie Deary immediately
yells “infield fly.” However, the wind carries the ball away
from Frank DiPrima and it hits Bella, who is standing off the
bag, on the head. Roberts tags up and scores,

Louisville manager Del Wilber charges that a double play
retired the side and the Colonels win; Denver skipper Andy~
Cohen maintains that there is only one out and that the score |
is tied. How would you call it:

Is Bella out? Is Rapp out? Does Roberts’ run count? Does
Louisville win or is the score tied?

The person submitting the best answer will be rewarded |
with two season box seats to Colonel games while the runner-up |
will get two box seats to a single game. Entries, which must
be post-marked after midnight, June 8th, must be submitted
on a post-card to Colonel Quiz, Courier-Journal Sports Dept.,
Louisville 2, Ky. In case of ties, the earlier postmark will deter-
mine the winner.

 

The winner of Colonel Quiz No. 5 was Ray Stiff, 1371 South
Preston Street, Louisville; the runner-up was William Water- |
stone, 3207 Cawein Way, Louisville.
