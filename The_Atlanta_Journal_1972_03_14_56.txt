From the time he was nine, Blomberg was stronger and
longer than anybody in his little league world. He is not now a
home run hitter, but one who strings his power out like a

the swing.”

They speak of him in whispers as the next Mickey Mantle,
who sat down on the bench a way, but this, too is a punch line
that Blomberg rolls with deftly. “I’m just going out to be the
best player I can. It if works out I’m the next Mantle, then I
guess I’m it.”

Oh, fame. Oh, immorality, seize me if you must.
