Lions Name
Outstanding
Young Men

Bill Latham, senior at El

Paso High School, and Juan
Salvador, Jefferson High senior,

have been selected as “Student

Lions of the Month” by tke El
Paso Lions Club.

This award is given to boys
who have been outstanding
in school, church, and social
activities and have been both
a good student and a good
citizen.

Bill is a member of Senior
National Honor Society, Langu-
age Honor Society, Slide Rule
Club, and Masque and Gavel
Club.

A football and track letter-
man, Bill is working as a stu-
dent assistant in charge of
Girls Poetry in the up-coming
El Paso High School debate
tournament.

Juan is president of the Jef-
ferson National Honor Society
and is an All-District baseball
player. He is a member of the
Number Sense Club, which is
an organization that works with
numbers for speed and accur-
acy.

Juan, who plans to attend
college and major in mathema-
tics, said, ‘““Through this award,
I have gotten to meet many
interesting people.” He listed
Judge Sam Blackham, Andy
Cohen, and John Phelan as
some of those he has met.
