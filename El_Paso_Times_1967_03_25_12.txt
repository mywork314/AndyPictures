Miss Marina Allen Cohen, daughter of Mr. and Mrs. An-
drew H. Cohen, 4341 N. Stanton, has been initiated into Phi
Theta Kappa, national honorary scholastic fraternity at

Stephens College, Columbia, Mo., where she is a freshman.
To be eligible for Phi Theta Kappa, a student must rank in
the upper seven per cent of the student body and be active

in campus events.
