Judges Study
Beautify EP

Contest Entries

Judges met Tuesday to decide the win-
ners in the recent ‘‘Beauty With Cacti”
contest which was jointly sponsored by
Beautify El Paso Association and The El
Paso Times.

Judges were Mrs. Gary Meyer of El
Paso Zoological Society, Mrs. Joseph C.
Baer, El Paso artist, and Mrs. H. W. Wil-
liams of the Convention and Tourist
Bureau of E] Paso Chamber of Com-
merce. They are deciding which El Paso
businesses will be awarded first, second,
and third prizes of $45, $35, and $20
respectively.

Southwestern landscaping was_ the

theme of the contest, and businessmen
were urged to use native plant material
which grows on our Southwestern desert,

or plants that do particularly well in
semi-arid climate with a minimum of
care and water.

Mrs. George Kistenmacher, vice-presi-
dent of Beautify El Paso Association and
chairman of the contest, said, ‘“‘We are
pleased with the interest that these busi-
nesses have shown in beautifying their
premises. I think these improvements
will prove to be an incentive for other
businesses in El] Paso to clean up and
beautify, too!”’

The two-month ‘‘Beautify With Cacti’’
campaign-contest began May 15 and
ended July 15. Names of the winning
businesses will be announced in the Sun-
day Times.

Andy Cohen is president of Beautify
EI] Paso Association.
