Ceremonies to open

Ysleta Boys League

Opening ceremonies for the Ysleta
Boys Baseball League will be held at 7
p.m. today at the Ysleta Cut-Off
Field, Zaragoza and I-10 East.

Master of ceremonies will be Sa!
Berroteran and the welcoming ad-
dress given by Janes L. Thrash. The
Invocation will be given by Rev. P.J.
Martinez and the guest speaker will
be former U.T. El Paso coach Andy
Cohen. Music will be provided by the
Pobres Band from 5-7 p.m.
