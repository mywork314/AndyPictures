Almost every present-day manager
in baseball can be traced back to two
men who were teaching the game in
weaning of this century — John
McGraw and Branch Rickey.
McGraw managed the Giants for
more than 30 years and Rickey built
two organizations, the Cardinals and
the Dodgers. McGraw begat Casey
Stengel, who tutored Billy Martin.
Gene Mauch, Dick Williams, Tom La-
sorda and Danny Ozark are products
of Rickey’s Dodger system. Jim Fre-
gosi played under Bill Rigney, who
played under Mel Ott, who played
under, yep, John J. McGraw.

sports writer for the New York Times,
