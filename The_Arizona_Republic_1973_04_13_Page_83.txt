Improved Miners challenge
A-State in 3-game series

By BOB JACOBSEN

At the College Baseball
Coaches convention in Chicago
this past winter, guest speak-
er and American League
Manager of the Year Chuck
Tanner gave much of the
credit for his (Tanner's) suc-
cess to one man — Andy Coh-
en.

It had to be good timing.

Cohen, who managed Tan-
ner in the minor leagues,
brings unquestionably the na-
tion's most improved collegi-
ale baseball team into town
tonight as his Texas-El Paso
Miners meet Arizona State in
the first of a three-game
Western Athletic Conference
Southern Division series,

The Sun Devils, 34-5 overall
and 3-0 in WAC play, will
send ace Eddie Bane (7-1) to
the mound against the Min-
ers, 17-11 and 2-1 in league
action after a pair of wins
over New Mexico last week.

Due to water being released
into the Salt River, causing
limited access to Municipal
Stadium, game times for to-
night and tomorrow night
have been moved back to 8
o'clock. The first game of to-
morrow’s twinbill is still 1
p.m. with Jim Umbarger (2-
1) set to hurl, and Jim Otten
(6-1) scheduledtomorrow
night. There will be no radio
broadcast of any of the three
games.

Most improved in the na-
tion, you say? They have to
be. The Miners were probably
the worst team (?) in the
country last year.

Examples: (1) ASU 30,
UTEPO; (2) ASU 16, UTEP 0;
(3) ASU 12, UTEP 1; (4) ASU
8, UTEP 0; and (5) ASU 10,
UTEP 4. Of course, losing to
last year's great Sun Devil
club was no disgrace, but the
Texans finished 12-39 overall
and 1-17 in WAC play, beating
the Lobos on the next-to-last
day,

“T just hope the players for-
get last year.” said ASU
coach. Jim Brock, “and keep
that out of their minds, We'll
have to beat this year’s
UTEP club on the field.

“The league is much more
even this year. There'll be
some upsets along the way.”

The Devils have won 26
straight in WAC regular sea-
son play, last losing to UTEP
in May, 1971, and have cap-
tured 36 of their last 39 in the
conference. Two of those
three defeats were to the
Miners, and, in fact, the Tex-
ans won two games in each of
1971 and 1972.
