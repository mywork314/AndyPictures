Boy Scout Troop
No. 99 Cleans
Landscaped Area

A group of 15 boys, members
of Boy Scout Troop No. 99,
sponsored by Our Lady’s
Youth Center, today cleaned
the area in the 4900 block of
Montana street, across from
Loretto Shopping Center.

This is an annual program of
the center. Tula Irrobali is
OLYC program director.

THE AREA was landscaped
with native plants some three
years ago. The workers
cleared paper, cans and bot-
tles and other debris away.
