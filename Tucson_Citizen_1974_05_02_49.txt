By STEVE WESTON
> Citizen Sportswriter

EL PASO — When coach Jerry Kindall’s University of Ari-
zona baseball team lost a game a few weeks ago in Tucson to
Texas-El Paso’s Miners, it was merely regrettable.

Another defeat could be disastrous.

And the Wildcats will go all out to avoid that tomorrow and
Saturday as they take on coach Andy Cohen’s Miners in a criti-
cal Western Athletic Conference Southern Division three-game
series. —

The set opens with an 11 a.m, (Tucson time) contest tomor-
row. Righthander Dave Breuker will go to the mound for Arizo
na in an attempt to win his 13th game of the season without
defeat.

Breuker probably will go up against UTEP’s spunky junior
righthander Roy Stringer (6-3, 3.75). Stringer was the winning
pitcher in the Miners’ 6-4 upset of the Cats and has hurled a win
over Arizona State. Despite a bad shoulder, Stringer held ASU
to a 3-2 victory just last weekend.

In Saturday’s 9 a.m, (Tucson time) twin bill, UA will pitch
righthanders Mark Schimpf (11-0) and Ron Bell (3-0) against
UTEP righthanders Jeff Hogan (3-4) and Dan Modlinski (3-5).

As the WAC Southern Division is approaching the finish,
Kindall’s Wildcats cannot afford to stumble.

UA, No, 1 rated in the country, goes into the game 50-4 over-
all and 10-2 in conference play. But they are being hotly pursued
by A-State (36-20, 9-3), which is at New Mexico tonight and
tomorrow.
