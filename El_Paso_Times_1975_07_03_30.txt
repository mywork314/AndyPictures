Sandoval New
Bowie Mentor

Juan Sandoval, mathematics
teacher at Bowie High School
has been appointed varsity
baseball coach at Bowie. He
succeeds Santos (Kayo) Perez.

Sandoval coached the Bowie
baseball team towards the end
of the season this year when
Perez had to leave the team
due to his health. He will coach
the team in addition to his math

assignment.

A graduate of Jefferson High
School, Perez received a B.S.

degree from the University of
Texas at El Paso. He came to
Fl Paso Independent School

District

in 1973 and was

assigned to Bowie where he has

taught math.

Sandoval played baseball at
Jefferson and also for the U.T.
El Paso Miners under the
coaching of Andy Cohen.

Armando Gutierrez,
assistant superintendent for
athletics. at EPISD, said,
“Sandoval did a commendable
job for the Bears in the absence
of Perez so we feel he will be a
real asset to the Bowie sports

program.”
