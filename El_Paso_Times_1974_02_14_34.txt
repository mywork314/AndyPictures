El Paso Times (El Paso, Texas) - Thu, Feb 14, 1974 - Page 34

Miner Diamond
Opener Nears

By FLIP MORIN

Times Sports Writer
The University of Texas at
El Paso’s baseballers contin-
ue inter-squad workouts at
Hugo Meyer Field in prepara-
tion for the 1974 exhibition
opener Feb. 22-23, hosting

Ranger Junior College.
Coach Andy Cohen has
some high hopes for this
year’s Miner baseball team in
his 12th season at the helm.
“The team spirit and hustle is
high. We have no problems in

this area,” Cohen said.

Coach Cohen said the daily
workouts have resulted in the
team’s being in much better
shape this far in the season
tha? any previous year. “My
brother Syd has the pitchers
especially on a special physi-
cal conditioning program and
it has paid off,” Cohen said.
“We have no worries about
sore arms or other petty inju-
Ties because of the exercises
they are going through.”

Last year the Miners looked
good in non-conference play
while compiling a 16-11 won
and lost slate. But when West-
ern Athletic Conference ac-
tion came it was another story
and they plummeted to a 2-16
league record.

A dozen returning letter-
men spark the current roster
of hopefuls, presently at 29,
including 15 El Pasoans. Four
seniors are in their final U_T.
El Paso season — infielder
Bob Downing, outfielder
Herb Von Hagen, catcher Jim
Brundage and pitcher Mike
Kruta.

Coach Cohen has given an
early nod for the Ranger ex-
hibition(2 p.m.) toa couple of
moundmen. Tentatively set
for pitching chores are River-
side High School's Sam Cox, a
freshman at 6-foot and 155
pounds and a righthander,
along with David Jannusch,
another frosh from Carpen-
tersville, Il]. Jannusch is 6-2
and 175 and also.throws and
bats right-handed.

Rounding out the pitching
staff are El Pasoan John
Franklin a sophomore; letter-
man sophomore Mike Hilton
from Andrews, Tex.; Pampa,
Texas’ Jeff Hogan, a sopho-
more letterman; Steve Klein,
a junior from Chicago plus
Chieagoans Dan Modlinski
and Bob Knox, both juniors.

Larry Richardson is a soph-
omore letterman from Odes-
sa, Tex., Kruta, a graduating
letterman is from Arlington,
Tex.
