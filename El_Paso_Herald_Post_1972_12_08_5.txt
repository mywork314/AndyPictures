Terming boundaries “unit-

lines, E] Paso and Juarez offi-
cials today dedicated a histori-
cal park at International
Boundary Monument No. 1, on
the west bank of the Rio
Grande near Smeltertown.

At the close of the short cer-
emony, youths identifying
themselves as ‘“‘La Raza stu-
dents’ from the University of
Texas at El Paso took over the
speaker's podium and asked
the monument be named after
Ricardo Falcon, Chicano lead-

to death in Orogrande in

August.

ignored by the crowd of some

taries from El Paso-Juarez.
