El Paso Herald-Post (El Paso, Texas) - Sat, Mar 8, 1975 - Page 11

Miners’ Pitching
Good; Split Bill,
Errors are Costly

LUBBOCK — Andy Cohen
found out that good pitching
can’t do it all.

The veteran U.T.-El Paso~
coach has been counting on his*
mound staff to carry the
Miners’ baseball team in 1975.

George Landreth and Jeff
Hogan tried to do just that in
yesterday's doubleheader
against Texas Tech. They
permitted just one earned run
in 21 innings, but the Miners
were only able to get a split
against the Red Raiders.

Texas Tech took the opener 1-
0 in 10 innings and UTEP
grabbed the nightcap 4-3 in 11
innings.

“THE ONLY earned run they
scored,”’ Cohen said, “came in
that first game. Texas Tech
had runners on first and third
with two outs. The batter hit a
swinging bunt down third base.

Glenn Villareal fielded the ball
cleanly and threw low to first
base, but the first baseman
couldn’t hold on to the ball.”

Roy Stringer drove in the
Miners’ winning run in the
second game with a one-out
single in the top of the 11th to
plate Terry Dillingham.

“We have to improve our
fielding,”’ Cohen added. The
Miners made six errors in the
twinbill.

THE MINERS had a chance
to win the opener when they
loaded the bases with only one
out in the 10th inning against
Texas Tech starter Steve
Runkel. Kim Hunter was
brought in relief and he struck
out the next two batters.

Art Bravo collected four hits,
including two doubles, in the
two games. He was 3-4 the
second game. Joe Pennies was
2-9 for both games while Alan
Morris was 2-5, with one double
and one rbi, in the second
game.

UTEP has a 6-3 season
record.

The two teams were to play
another doubleheader today,
but Cohen reported freezing
temperatures that might force
cancellation.
