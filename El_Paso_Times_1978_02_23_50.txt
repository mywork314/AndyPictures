UTEP '78 Opener
Hosts NM Highlands

The University of Texas at El Paso
Opens its 60-game 1978 baseball
Season Friday with a doubleheader
against New Mexico Highlands.

Game time is set for noon at the El
Paso Technical High Schoo! field.
Tech is one of three fields the Miner
team will call its home this season.

Coach Andy Cohen's squad will play
all Western Athletic Conference
schools and a few others at Dudley
Field with the remainder of the
baseball action going across the
border to Cruz Blanca baseball park
in Juarez.
