WEST ORANGE, NJ. —

Thomas A. Edison died peace-
fully before dawn at the hilltop
estate where he labored to
give light, work and recreation
to millions.

Andy Cohen’s fine playing in
the field and Jodie
McCormick’s excellent work
on the mound enabled the El
Paso All-Stars to defeat the
Lion Shoemen, 10 to 5, yester-
day in Juarez.

““One-Eyed’’ Connelly came
to town yesterday, sore at
heart because the motorist
from whom he bummed a ride
arrived too late for him to
crash the bullfight in Juarez.
