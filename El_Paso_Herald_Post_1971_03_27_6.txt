El Paso Herald-Post (El Paso, Texas) - Sat, Mar 27, 1971 - Page 6

Miners Win—

Bombard’s
Clutch Work

Is Supreme

Marc Bombard sure makes
things exciting — much to the
dismay of Miner baseball
coach Andy Cohen’s ulcers.

Bombard, who completed his
fifth game of the season, yes-
terday at Dudley Field, was in
trouble in just about every
other inning. He worked out of
every tight situation except for
the second inning when East-
ern New Mexico scored three
of its five runs in a 7-5 deci-
sion.

“BOY I TELL you, he
sure pitched himself out of
some kind of jams,” said
Cohen, “‘Bombard really stuck
with it and bore down to doa
fine job.”

Cohen had praise for the hit-
ting of Bob Prokopowicz, Gary
Molberg and Bobby Morales,
who all tagged key hits as
U.T.-El Paso overcame a 4-0
deficit to win.

Molberg and Prokopowicz
highlighted a four-run sixth
which put the Miners ahead to
stay. Molberg put the Miners
out in front 5-4 with a two-run
single and Prokopowicz added
a double to knock in the sixth
and seventh runs.
