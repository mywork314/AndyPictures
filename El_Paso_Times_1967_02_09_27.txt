Sun King
Boosters
Completed

Formation of an El Paso Sun
King booster club was completed
at a luncheon meeting Wednes-
day of 12 El Pasoans who will
co-captain teams in a pre-season
ticket drive.

The ticket campaign, to be
called “Operation Sun
Kings—67” is scheduled March|
6-10.

The booster club will be made
}up of 60 prominent El Paso busi-

nessmen to be known as the
“King’s Club.”

Gene Autrey, president of the
Sun Kings and chairman of the
board of the parent California
Angels is honorary chairman of
Operation Sun Kings.

Members of the Kings Club, if
it is able to reach its $60,000-goal
in the drive, will be guests of the
Angels for a day at Disneyland
and an Angel-Yankee baseball
game later in the season.

Founders of the organization
are John Phelan, Harry Elliot,
Dorr Miller, H. D. Fulwiler,
Andy Cohen, Jim Murphy, Saul
Kleinfield, Ed Saucedo, Ira Batt,
Irv Lambka, Neil McKenzie and
Allan Tompson.
