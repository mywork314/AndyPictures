Alsup delivers key
hits in Miner wins

Bob Alsup certainly rates as ‘Mr, Clutch’ for
U.T,-El Paso's baseball team.

Alsup’s late-inning performances provided the
Miners with a doubleheader sweep, 7-5 and 3-2, over
Southern Colorado, UTEP upped its season record
to 21-8.

The first game found Alsup breaking up a 5-5 tie
with a run-producing single. Head coach Andy
Cohen brought Alsup from the outfield to the pit-
cher's mound to choke off a Southern Colorado ral-
ly. Alsup drove in two runs in the second game, in-
cluding the final one on a two-out triple in the bot-
tom of the eighth,
