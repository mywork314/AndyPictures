Ted’s tenure with Indianap-
olis actually started in 1947.
He was with the club for the
first month of the season but
made only one official trip to
the plate. After his promotion
in '48, Beard came back to
the Indians for most of the
1949 season

He never has spent an entire
season in the majors, being
with Pittsburgh part-time from
1948-52. In 1953 he was with
Hollywood and the next two
years with San Francisco, the
club from which the Indians
bought him in the winter of
1955,

Ted’s farewell contribution
with the Indians was three hits
in five trips, to boost his sea-
son average to .347. He will
miss the opportunity to win
the league batting champion-
ship, but a contract with the
White Sox means much more.
In Ted's greatest season he
was the bulwark of the Indian
apolis club and many might
agree that he was even better
than in 1948.

The indelible record thot

addition to the top batting mark
in the league, a total of 121
hits, 20 doubles, 12 triples, 10
homers and 11 stolen bases. All
hoil to Beard, and may Manager
