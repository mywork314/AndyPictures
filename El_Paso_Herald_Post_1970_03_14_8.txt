Early Lead
Doesn't Hold

For Miners

LUBBOCK — “Tighten up
the infield and don’t let any-
thing through” was the strate-
gy coach Andy Cohen and U.T.
El Paso Miner baseball team
used today as they met Texas
Tech in a double header.

The Miners dropped an
opener to the Red Raiders 4-3
yesterday after losing a 3-0
lead late in the game.

Harry Milner, who started
for the Miners, held Tech
scoreless for 6 2-3 innings

before getting himself into
trouble. He was relieved by
Ernie Carreon, who was
charged with the loss after an
infield error made possible the
tying run.

THE RED RAIDERS came
back in the eighth to add the
winning run. The Miners tried
a comeback in the ninth, but
couldn’t plate a run.

Scoring for the Miners came
in the first and third innings.
Milner got on with a double
and was driven in by Sandov-
al.

In the third Sandoval and
Johnny Miller both had singles
and scored on a blast by Bob
Popokowitz.

Marc Bombard and Jim
Greggerson were slated to get
the starting nods in today’s
double outing.
