Toughest Foe |

This Season —

Listen to Andy Cohen talk
and one comes to the conclu-
sion that his Texas-El Paso
baseballers are now leaving
their honeymoon cottage and
preparing for a rough sea
voyage. ;

The Miners have run up six
straight victories, after drop-
ping their first two games of
the season, but Cohen
believes his team will start
into the meat of their sched-
ule Friday and Saturday
against Northern Arizona.

Friday’s single affair at
Dudley Field is scheduled for
a 2:30 p.m. starting time
while the double feature Sat-
urday, at the same time, will
get underway at 1:30 p.m.

The Miners made New
Mexico State their sixth vic-
tim last Tuesday, winning 9-1
and continued their long
string of innings without
allowing an earned run.

The Miners pitching staff
has not allowed an earned
run in 32 innings although
they have been nicked for
seven runs during this period.
Errors have enabled the
opponents to plate runs which
were not charged to the pitch-
ers.

Cohen finally started a
righthander against New
Mexico State, Ron Peterson,
who toiled seven innings,
before being spelled. Peter-
son, used in relief on other
occasions, was the first right-
hander Cohen had nominated
to start a game.

Previously, Cohen has
employed four lefthanders,
Lynn Hunt, Marc Bombard,
Harry Milner and Terry Witt,
as his starting hurlers.

The Lumberjacks can
expect some lefthanded
throwing this weekend inas-
much as the Hunt-Bombard-
Milner-Witt combo haven’t
seen action since the Texas
Tech series last weekend.

Milner has won both of his
starts with route-going per-
formances, taming University
of Albuquerque and Texas
Tech. Hunt and Bom bard,
after shaky starts in their
first outing, have evened
their record at 1-1. Witt
stands 1-0 in his only appear-
ance.
