Miner Nine Comes Close

The University of Texas at El
Paso won two moral victories
Saturday afternoon against
Arizona while extending its
losing streak to 12 games.

“We gave Arizona a couple of
good battles. We could have
won both games and I thought
we had that last one,” said
dejected Andy Cohen after his
Miners fell to the Wildcats 3-1
and 10-3 at Dudley Field in
Western Athletic Conference
baseball action.

UTEP was leading Arizona 3-
2 in the second game going into
the ninth inning before the
Wildcats exploded for eight
runs to improve their WAC
record to 9-3 while the Miners
dropped to 1-11 in conference
play.

Dave Stegman began the late
Arizona uproar with a leadoff
double. After one out, Miner
starter Dave Jannusch gave up
an infield single to Bill Simpson
which scored Stegman from
third.

Another single by Don
Zimmerman and a hit batter
loaded up the bases with the

score tied at 3-3 and Arizona
went ahead for good when
Jannusch uncorked a wild
pitch, allowing Simpson to slide
under Janrusch's glove after
catcher Terry Goodro had
retrieved the ball and thrown it
back to home.

After that, the game went
downhill for the underdog
Miners. Les Pearsey walked to
reload the sacks and Ron
Hassey rapped an opposite
field single to left which sent
Arizona on its way back to
Tucson with a 41-13 overall
record

“‘Jannusch pitched real well
for us but he just ran out of gas
there in the ninth inning," said
Cohen. ‘When Arizona started
off the ninth with that double, it
took our momentum away from
us.””

Arizona opened the scoring in
the nightcap with a run in the
third only to have Keith
Dwinell, Glenn Villarreal and
John Hriniak knot the score in
the bottom of the fourth with
singles.
