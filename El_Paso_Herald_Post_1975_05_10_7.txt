ogan

Is Rewarded

It’s ‘only justice that
University of Texas at El Paso
hurler Jeff Hogan strolled out
of Dudley Field yesterday
afternoon with a big ‘W’’ strung
from his belt.

Hogan stymied New
Mexico’s Lobos on seven
scattered hits and an eighth
inning run Friday but appeared
a loser until two were gone in
the bottom of the ninth and his
mates rallied for a much-
celebrated 2-1 triumph.

Still it was Hogan's bulldog
effort in the top of the ninth that
left Miner coach Andy Cohen
beaming the brightest.

“JEFF pitched an out-
standing game,’’ Andy com-

mented. “But in the ninth he
was trailing 1-0 and we weren’t
doing anything against New
Mexico’s pitcher (southpaw
Jim Weber). Jeff got into a jam
and could have easily folded up
