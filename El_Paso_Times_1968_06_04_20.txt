Special to EL PASO TIMES
AMARILLO, Tex. — With a new manager at the helm, the
EI Paso Sun Kings bring their five-game winning streak to
Potter County Stadium here Tuesday to open a three-game
Texas League series with the Amarillo Giants. Gametime is

7:30 p.m. Tuesday.

Andy Cohen, former major league player and manager who

has acted as coach for the El
Paso team for the past several
years, will guide the club
through its Amarillo set while
manager Chuck Tanner takes
a brief break to attend the
graduation of his oldest son in
New Castle, Pa.

Tanner left the team Mon-
day afternoon in San Antonio,
after the final doubleheader of
the series in the Alamo City
was rained out. He will rejoin
the Kings here Thursday.

Tall Tommy Murphy, who
rejoined the team in San Anto-
nio after a stint of military
reserve duty, will take the hill
in the opening game here
Tuesday. A doubleheader
Wednesday night — picking up
arained-out game on El
Paso’s last visit — will find
Veru Geishert and Rudy May
drawing the hill chores.

The Kings have won five
games in a row since their
last loss, which was at the
hands of Amarillo in the ope-
ner of a four-game set in El
Paso. The Kings won the last
three games in El Paso and
then won both games in San
Antonio.

Monday’s rainout, which left Wood

the Kings victors in 17 of their
last 20 games, actually boost-
ed the El Paso club half a
game in the western division

Standings, since the Albuqu- Chicago

erque Dodgers, the division
leaders, lost to Amarillo.

The Kings will be playing
without the services of Don
‘Wilkinson, slugging outfielder
who cut his foot Saturday in
San Antonio and missed Sun-
day’s game.

Pitcher Rick Evans has
been sent to San Jose, and
pitcher Jon Lervig has been
reactivated from the disabled
list.
