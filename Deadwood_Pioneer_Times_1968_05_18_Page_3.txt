Texas Hurler
Inks Contract
With Winner

WINNER — Richard Moore,
a big right handed pitcher from
the University of Texas at El
Paso, has signed with the Win-
ner Pheasants for the 1968 Ba-
sin League season. Moore had
a 4-0 record in summer ball last
year pitching in Honolulu, Ha-
waii.

Richard was born in Fort
Smith, Ark., but graduated from

the Burges High School in El
Paso, Texas, He won football,
baseball and basketball letters
and was captain of the baseball
team.

“Spinner” as he is called,
played in Little League, Pony
League, Colt League, Babe Ruth
League and Connie Mack
Leagues as well as American
Legion ball. His Connie Mack
and American Legion teams
won state and regional cham-
pionships.

For Coach Andy Cohen at
Texas El] Paso, he plays first
bases and pitches. He is a pre-
dental major at the university.

Moore is 6'2", weighs 214

pounds, and is 19 years old.
