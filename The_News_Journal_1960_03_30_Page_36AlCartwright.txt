NTEGRATION IN BASEBALL HAS REACHED THE
point where they even kid about racial prejudices.
Joe Garagiola’s first crack in that department still gets
a giggle in all the camps. The Cardinals’ clowning sports-
caster Saw a glum Bill White standing in the clubhouse
doorway during a game-postponing rainstorm and cracked:
“Hey Bill, let's you and me go down town and shake up
Kresge’s.” The broadest grin in the clubhouse came from
the Negro outfielder.

More recently, the Cardinals were giving their annual
alleged talent show in their St. Pete hotel. Garagiola was
M. C. and stood by while Vinegar Bend Mizell did a mono-
logue. Some heckler among the hotel guests, Who threw
in a few cheers for Alabama, kept butting in on Mizell
until Garagiola came forward and said, “Dad, if you
don’t cut that out we'll all come down and build a lunch |
counter around you.”
