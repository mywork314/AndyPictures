Cohen is
recipient
of citation

Andy Cohen, who retired last
season after 16 years as UTEP’s
only baseball coach, was hon-
ored with a special citation
Tuesday night at the Lions Club
fifth annual ‘Dinner with the
Miners” at the Civic Center.

Cohen was honored in place of
naming an outstanding athlete
in baseball at the awards ban-
quet, the proceeds of which go
into the Miner athletic scholar-
ship fund.

Outstanding athletes in the
other men’s sports were: Jerry
Walker, football; James Munya-
la, indoor track; Peter Lema-
shon, outdoor track; Michael
Musyoki, cross country, and
Kevin Hill, basketball.

Honored in the women's
sports were: Endi Gil, basket-
ball; Ruth Jaramillo, gymnas-
ties; Diane Bohling, volleyball
and Jenifer Smit, track.
