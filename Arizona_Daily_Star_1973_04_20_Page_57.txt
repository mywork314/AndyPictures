Knox is the Miner’s leading hurler with a

giving up 14 hits and fanning 22 batters. Greg-

Texas coach Andy Cohen contends
his team is “raring to go” against the Wildcats
despite being “annihilated by Arizona State”’
last week.

“We hope to make a respectable showing
against Arizona’s fine team,” Cohen said.
“Our pitching is not quite as strong as I
thought it would be at season’s start, but we
hope to give Arizona a good game.”

Cohen’s mound woes are attested not only
by their 17-14 overall record, but also by the
fact the Miner pitching staff has given up 225
hits for 141 runs (92 earned) for a 3.33 team
ERA.

By comparison, UTEP collected only 222
hits for 144 runs (102 earned) for a 3.84 ERA.

Pitching problems have also plagued the
Wildeats, in what Kindall describes as “‘incon-
sistent performances.”

Arizona's staff has given up 309 hits for 160
runs (137 earned) for a 3.60 team ERA. The 26-
14 overall record can be attributed to Wildcat
hitting which has amassed 252 runs (196
earned) on 384 hits for a 5.31 ERA.

Adding to Kindall’s concern for this series
is Joel Godfrey (7-1), Saturday’s scheduled
first-game starter, will not make the trip be-
cause of illness. Ed Anderson (3-1) will pitch
instead with Mark Schimpf. (4-1) throwing in
the final contest of the series.

Although Kindall did not name his starting
lineup, it is expected that he will start Marty
Reid (.311) behind the plate, Bob Starke (.318)
at first; Rudy Mendoza (.309) at second; Ben
Heise (.319) at shortstop; either Bob Scze-
cinski (.273) or Bob Allen (.237) at third; Jim
Filippelli (.294) in left field; Marv Thompson
(.322) in center; Scott Norris (.226) in right;
and either Allen or Rod Fleming (.362) as the
designated hitter.

Arizona holds a 21-3 advantage over UTEP
since the two started playing WAC games in
1969. The Wildcats swept both three-games
series last year.
