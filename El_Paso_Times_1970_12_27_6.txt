earnest prayer that Thou wilt keep
the United States in Thy holy pro-
tection; that Thou wilt incline the
hearts of the citizens to cultivate
a@ spirit of subordination and obe-
dience to government, and to en-

love for one another and for tne
citizens of the United States at
large; and finally, that Thou wilt
most graciously be pleased to dis-
pose us all to do justice—to love
mercy and to demean ourselves

EL PASO TAKES LEAD

El Pasoans brought a staggering
97,180 pounds of aluminum during August
and September to Dickshire, Inc. to boost
that firm into first place among all Coors
distributors for aluminum recovery dur-
ing the first nine months of 1970.

Dickshire’s total for 1970 then stood at
162,540 pounds of aluminum . . . some 3.7
million empty cans.

In second place, and not far behind,
was a Midland, Tex., firm, DKT Co., with
145,508 pounds.

Dickshire has another honor, too.
Andy Cohen, former major league base-
ball player and a salesman for Dickshire,
is the president of Beautify El Paso Asso-
ciation. Andy certainly seems to work at
his job — rather at both jobs!
