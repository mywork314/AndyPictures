El Paso Herald-Post (El Paso, Texas) - Mon, Jun 21, 1976 - Page 2

Roast set
for retiring

Times Chief

R, W. “Pete’’ Lee, editor
of The Herald-Post, will be
master of ceremonies at the
William I. Latham Roast, to
be held June 29, at noon, at
El! Paso Country Club.

Participants will include
Andy Cohen, Frank Feuille,
Art Leibson, Louise Rea
and Bessie Simpson.

OTHERS ON the
program include Rev.
Herman Green, associate
pastor of First Baptist
Church, who will give the
invocation; County Judge
T. Udell Moore, Mrs.
Wayne Windle, Dorr Miller,
of the Chamber of Com-
merce, Mrs. Arlene
Quenon, representing the
Mayor, and Freeman
Harris, who will conclude
the program with ‘God
Bless America.’’ Mrs.
Latham also will be seated
at the head table.

The luncheon is sponsored
by civic organizations of El
Paso. All members and
other friends of Mr.
Latham, who retires July 4,
as editor of The Times, are
invited. Reservations may
be made with Mrs. Joe
Battle, 10121 Honolulu
drive, by June 24, at $5 per
person,
