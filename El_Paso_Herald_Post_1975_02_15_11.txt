Miners’ 1975 opener.

Ron Wrokel gave UTEP its
second win with a 3-1 decision
in the nightcap.

RONNIE MATTSON was the
only Red Raider to reach base
in the opener when he lofted a
fly ball to centerfield, which
was dropped by Dau. That fly
ball was only to be one of three
hit out of the infield all day off
of Modlinski, who struck out
five.

Dau made up for his mistake
two innings later.

Terry Dillingham led off the
Miners’ third with a walk.
Keith Rushing singled to left
and David Nance filled the
bases with an infield single. Art
Bravo popped out. Dau deliv-
ered a two-run base hit to left
center. A wild pitch by Tech’s
John Davidson allowed Nance
to score the third run of the in-
ning.

Wrobel scattered six hits in
the night cap and fanned nine
batters. Wayne Larson drove in
two runs with a double and a
