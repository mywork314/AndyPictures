Andy Cohen
Will Speak

At Kiwanis

Andy Cohen will be featured
speaker at the Friday noon
meeting of the Mt. Franklin
Kiwanis Club. Honored guests
will be 32 Little League ball
players from El Paso. Coach
Juan Gonzales and six of his
coaches will be at the annual
event.

Cohen played shortstop for
the National League New York
Giants under Manager John
McGraw.

Cohen coached with the Phil-
adelphia Phillies after his
playing days. He has been
coach of the U.T. El Paso
Miners for eight years and un-
til recently was the first base
coach of the El Paso Sun
Kings. He is past commander
of the American Legion. he
will speak on the ““‘Democracy
of Baseball.”’
