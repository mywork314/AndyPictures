Baseball Clinic Set
At Northeast YACA

A baseball clinic for 914
year olds will be held March 22
at 8:30 a.m. at the Northeast
Family ymca ball diamond,
5509 Will Ruth road. Andy
Cohen and some of his U.T. El

Paso Miners baseball team will
be on hand to help Bob Howell,
an ex-pro player, and his
associates start the first of
eight sessions aimed at
teaching the fundamentals and
features of playing baseball.
For more information call the
Northeast YMCA.
