Can Pickup

Program

Going Well

Members of the Beautify El |

Paso Association and their
guests were shown results of
the Dickshire program to rid
the City’s streets and scenic
views of aluminum beer cans.

Dickshire, distributor of
Coors beer, is paying one-half
cent a can for any aluminum
beer can turned in. Thus far,
the El Paso firm has paid out
$8,118.60 for the return of
1,867,278 cans.

Cans may be turned in to
Dickshire, 203 Chelsea, any-
time between noon and 3 p.m.
on Tuesdays end Thursdays.

Richard Azar, Dickshire
president, said that currently
about 30 per cent of the total
number of cans handled by his
firm are being returned. He
said that the history of the pro-
gram shows that by Septem-
ber about 50 per cent of the
cans will be brought in.

The cans are baled and then
re-processed for future use.

Presiding over the meeting
was Andy Cohen, Dickshire
sales manager and chairman
of the Beautify El Paso Asso-
ciation. Special guests includ-
ed State Rep.-Elect Charlie
Tupper Jr., Democratic nomi-
nee to the State House of Rep-
resentatives Jim Kaster IJr.,
Robert W. ‘‘Pete” Lee, editor
of the Herald-Post, and Dor-
rance H. Guy, executive edi-
tor of The El Paso Times.

The can project, sponsored

throughout the 11 Western |

states in which Coors is dis-
tributed, is a continuing one.
