Barber Trims Miners, 9-4

Albuquerque Journal (Albuquerque, New Mexico) - Sat, Apr 22, 1978 -
Page 17
Downloaded on May 31, 2025

By BART RIPP

New Mexico right-hander Bruce
Barber likes to walk up to people and
ask in a mock-Leslie Caron accent:
“Are my feathers green? Doesmy
gown look purple?”

The Lobo and Texas-El Paso baseball
teams locked in mortal combat Friday
and when the dust had cleared, or at
least stopped swirling at the Sports
Stadium, Barber turned the Miners a
paler shade of orange.

Barber, who may have lost if he'd
seen Arizona or Arizona State uni-

forms on the Miners, undressed UTEP,
9-4 in the opener of the three-game
series that could decide —ta-DA!
trumpets blare — third place in the
Western Athletic Conference Southern
Division.

A six-run uprising in the second in-
ning won the game for New Mexico,
which capt its first league game
in seven tries. Aaron Cain, Steve Muc-
cio and Mike Brown each drove in two
runs during the brisk game. Shortstop
Mike Foote, despite some unheady
baserunning, clubbed three singles
and knocked home three runs.

But it was the senior Barber, likened

Andy Cohen, the 73-year-old Miner
coach, didn't make the trip because his
wife is ill in El Paso, Andy's equally-
spry brother Syd, a mere 70, ran the
ballclub.

Trivia buffs will be eager to know
that Syd, while employed by the Wash-
ington Senators in 1934, was the last
American League pitcher to strike out
Babe Ruth. Syd also was the last AL
Pitcher to serve a home run to Ruth.

With those facts in mind, the Lobos
and Miners reconvene this afternoon
at the Sports Stadium. They'll play a
