Huntsman: Job
Scary, Vols
Expectedto Win

By DERRY EADS

Stan Huntsman has known success in coaching track
from two angles, and he admits starting at the top is kind of
scary.

Huntsman, who won Little All-American honors as a
fullback at Wabash College and laurels in track by establish-
ing school records in the shot put, discus and javelin, started
coaching at Ohio University.

“The year before I came to Ohio.” Huntsman said, “the
school had scored only four points in the conference meet
had a chance to develop the school’s track program by
myself.”

OHIO, IN Huntsman’s 14-year tenure, had 20 All-Ameri-
cans and finished high in the NCAA rankings.

Huntsman replaced Chuck Rohe as head coach at the
University of Tennessee in 1971. Rohe had won seven
straight Southeastern Conference outdoor championships
and 21 track and cross-country titles in the previous nine
year.

“The pressure is really tough.” Huntsman pointed out,
“because they expect you to win at Tennessee in track. It's
good in a way because it keeps me going, but it is also scarey
at times.”

HE ADDED that it is getting increasingly difficult to win
in the SEC

“SEC is getting better by leaps and bounds. The
University of Florida is always there. LSU is up and coming.
Alabama hired two new coaches to concentrate on building
up its track.”

ENMU’S First Track

Bill Silverberg, Eastern New Mexico University's track
coach, hasn't exactly had the ideal conditions at Portales the
last couple of years.

‘THIS IS OUR first year with a
Silverberd. “We had been running on dirt and rocks. It
makes a big difference for the athletes to run on a smooth
surface.”

Although Silverberg has not been able to get great depth
for his team, he has been able to recruit several outstanding
individuals like Mike Boit, the Olympian middle-distance
runner. Silverberg is hoping that Boit will help ENMU in
further recruiting in the next three years

The Greyhounds are taking their lumps this year but
Silverberg is optimistic

“MOST OF THE kids are freshmen,” he said. “and | am

looking forward to them developing strong in the next
of years

‘We are glad now that schools like UTEP and Arizona

are putting us on their schedule so that we can go against

good competition. We may lose, but we are gaining a lot of

experience.”
