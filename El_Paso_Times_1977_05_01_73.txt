Against UTEP 8-3, 11-6

By MARK LEIBSON
Times Sports Writer

Guess who defeated defending
National Collegiate Athletic
Association champion Arizona by
scores of 8-3 and 11-6 Saturday in a
college doubleheader?

Here are a few hints. First off, this
team had lost 19 straight games to the
powerful Wildcats and had previously
won only seven of 66 games in their
series which dates back to 1920.

Second, the last time the two teams
met, Arizona prevailed by em-
barrassing scores in Tucson of 27-5,
10-8 and 15-5,

If your answer was the University
of Texas at El Paso, chalk up a few
points in your favor. If you failed to
answer the question correctly, don’t
be too ashamed. Not too many other
people would have thought UTEP
could ever beat Arizona either, much
less twice in the same afternoon.

It happened though, in plain view of
one of the largest crowds to watch the
Miners this year — an estimated 150
people at Dudley Field.

But the noise generated from the
stands and UTEP’s dugout made up
for all the people who missed the
game.

“Enthusiasm supreme,” said
Miner Coach Andy Cohen as pan-
demonium overtook UTEP’s side of
the field. ‘I told you they'd surprise
some people and they showed it this
series that we could play against any
