Pels Better Team Than Now Showing

New Orleans had lost to Nashville Friday night, but
Andy Cohen, the popular Pelican pilot, wasn’t down,
like so many managers get when misfortune takes up
with them... “We've got a better team than our record
shows,” Andy commented as he hitched a ride to his
hotel. “Our pitching has been erratic, We've got some

very good youngsters that will make fine pitchers in
time. We've been making too many mistakes...”

The more Ernie White sees of Bob Schultz, the
more he likes the big lefthander who was signed a
week ago today as a free agent... Bob appeared in
four games during the week and now he gets a chance
as a starter... ‘He's a fine competitor,” remarked
the Vol manager after Friday night’s victory.
