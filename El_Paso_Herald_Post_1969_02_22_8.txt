Miners to Open
50-Game Slate

By MIKE PEMBERTON

Andy Cohen's U.T. El Paso
baseball team gets off to an
early start om a rugged 50-
game slate next Friday.

The Miners open against
Albuquerque University fora
three game home stand at
Dudley Field. The opener on
Friday will be followed by a
twin bil! on Saturday.

THIS IS THE TEAM’S first
year fn the Western Athletic
Conference and the Miners
will be in a division with Ari-
zona State, University of Ari-
zona and New Mexico. Ex-na-
tional champs Arizona and
Arizona State are always a
tough bunch to crack.

Cohen, who in previous
years, has relied on a strong
hitting team, may have his
Strategy changed. He will go
to what the legendary Connie
Mack called the most impor-
tant part of the game—pitch-
ing.

COHEN’S ACE will be sen-
for lefty Lynn Hunt. Hunt
dipped to a 7-4 mark a year
ago after posting an 8-3
record with a 1.73 earned run
average the previous season.

In support of Hunt will be
Jess Lujan, Bill Diesselhorst,
Don Wilson and imports
Harry Milner and Marc Bom-
ard.

COHEN feels the big sur-
prise on the hill could come
from Ron Jones. Jones was
the tight end on the Miner
football team this year and
was also drafted by the Green
Bay Packers.

“This Quy could be a real
Surprise to us when he gets his
arm into shape. The kid has
lots of raw talent and I like the
way he looks,” Cohen said.

LUJAN FELL off last year

to a poor 1-5 season after post-
ing an 83 mark the year
before. Adding power on the
hill will be transfers Bombard
and Milner. Bombard, an ex-
Eastwood hurler, came to
UTEP after a stretch at San
Angelo State. Milner is a
Odessa transfer.

Cohen faces two problems
in the hitting department but
hopes this won't affect his
game too much. The first is
the fact he is staffing only two
returning .300 hitters and the
second is he has only one left-
handed swinger in the lineup.

RETURNING with .300 plus
averages are John Miller and
Juan Sandoval. Milner swat-
ted .348 and Sandoval hada
311 pace.

Adding to the bombing
power will be hard-hitting
Danny Blanco and catcher
Ron Mix. Blanco, who usually
stays around the .300 mark
dropped to .276 last year and
Hix came home with .267
average.

ONE ONE MAJOR shift is
seen in the fielding position.
With appearance of Larry
Currie, who is that only lefty
in the lineup, at first, Miller
was moved to the outfield.
Hopefully the move won't
bother Miller, whom Cohen
feels is the most ‘versatile’
plaver on the field.

The infield will be staffed
with experience. At second
base will be Sandoval, at
short Ron Rush and at the hot
box Blanco. Rush can also
play in the outfield, but it is
very doubtful with the lineup
Cohen has in the pastures.
