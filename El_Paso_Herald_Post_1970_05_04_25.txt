Coach Andy Cohen's U.T. El
Paso baseball team gets to
help New Mexico State finish

_ its season with a doubleheader

| beginning at 1 p.m. tomorrow
at either Dudley or Hugo
Meyer Field

| Cohen's Miners will be look-
ing for their fourth and fifth

| straight wins after coming off

a series sweep over the New

Mexico Lobos 10-8, 7-2 and 5-3.

THIS IS THE FIRST West-
ern Athletic Conference sweep
for the Miners and also the
| first doubleheader sweep in
the WAC.

The series sweep was a big
blow to New Mexico's chances
of winning the WAC. The
Lobos had strong title hopes
before the series here.

In the first game of Satur-
day’s twinbill, Harry Milner
recorded what Cohen termed a
masterpiece. “His fastball,
curve and screwball were
working exceptionall,”’ said
the Miner mentor.

“He also got good fielding
and hitting behind him,”
Cohen added.

Jim Greggerson pitched the
second game and was cool
despite wildness at times.
“He got into trouble, but was
able pitch himself out of it,”
said Cohen,

COHEN PRAISED the work
of Rick Butler through the
series and especially the last
game.

Leading the Miners in hit-
ting were Bob Prokopowicz
who went 6-11 for the three
games and Juan Sandoval who
was 7-13 in the sweep.

One big thing that was
favorable to the Miners in the
| last game was two costly
| errors by the Lobos when the
Miners had their big inning.

WHEN THE MINERS meet
| the Aggies tomorrow they will
face some young pitching.

New Mexico State has been
hampered by the loss of Mitch
Seales to injuries, Sophomore
Weldon Langley has had to
pick up much of the slack.

Leading the Aggie hitters is
center fielder Rex Gates who
is hitting right at the .400
mark. He also has nine
homers.
