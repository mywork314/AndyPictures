ANDY CAN‘T
BEAR TO LOSE

A bear, valued at $1,000
will be presented to
Manager Andy Cohen to-
morrow night if his Den-
ver Bears defeat the In-
dianapolis Indians in the
American Association
baseball opener starting
at 7:45.

Cohen, who managed
the Indians last year, has
agreed to accept the bear,
which will be presented
immediately after the
game if Denver wins.

“We'll just ship him to
Denver and use him as a
mascot,” smiled Cohen,
who arrived yesterday
with the Denver team.
The bear was presented
to the Indianapolis club
by Dr. Marvin Sandorf,
who had the trained ani-
mal at his Twin drive-in
theater

If the Indians defeat
Denver, the bear will be
kept in Indianapolis as a
“teaser” to Cohen in
future Denver-Indianapo-
lis games.
