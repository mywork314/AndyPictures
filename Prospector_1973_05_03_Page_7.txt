“The Way I See it.

By Bob MacDonald

The circus was at Dudley Field last weekend. The Ringling Brothers:
Barnum and Cohen fiasco starred the Arizona State baseball team in all
its glory.

What else could you have called it? The Miners were completely,
out-classed by the second-ranked Sun Devils. ASU scored 50 runs in the
three game series and had 24 extra base hits.

The three games the two teams played in no way resembled the sport
of baseball, It was batting practice for the Devils and an utter
embarassment for UT El Paso. And so ends the case against Miner coach: «
Andy Cohen, May the baseball program at UT El Paso rest in peace.

On the brighter side, Miner track men Hans Hoglund and Peter
Farmer extended their unbeaten streaks with wins at the Mt. SAC and -
Penn Relays.

Hoglund, the giant from Mindal, Sweden, is one of the top collegiate
shot-putters in the nation. He is consistently over the 62 feet mark and

has a best this year of 65-%.. Hoglund is just a sophomore: and a mark of
68 or better is in the not too distant future.

Farmer performed a superman feat last weekend by winning the
hammer throw competition at the Pen Relays on Friday and then
jetting across the country to win his speciality at the Mt. SAC Relays in
California on Saturday. The two wins were his fifth and sixth of the
year. Farmer has been in dire need for competition, as his specialty has
not been counted in Miner dual meets this year. The Miner strong man
responded well to the test and is expected to easily win the WAC
championship.
