Father Yermo Youth
Honored At Banquet

Father Yermo Elementary
School sponsored its third
annual awards banquet on
Sunday, April 27, at the Mid-
town Holiday Inn.

Master of Ceremonies,
Ismael Tercero, introduced
guests of honor who were as
follows: Sister Rose, principal
of Father Yermo; Mr. and
Mrs. Robert Fitzgerald, repre-
senting the Catholic Office of
Education; Pat Velarde, head
coach of St. Pius X School;
Andy Cohen, University of
Texas at El Paso baseball
coach; Don Maynard, flanker-
back for the New York Jets
and Mrs. Manuel Martinez,
chairman of the banquet.

Maynard presented a film
featuring the highlight of the
Jets’ 1967 football season. He
also awarded the Most Valua-
ble Player Trophies in the var-
sity and pee-wee divisions.
Recipients of those awards
were as follows: varsity divi-
sion, Richard Martinez and
Sylvia de la Rosa, and pee-wee
division, Gilbert Gutierrez and
Lilly Ortega.

Cohen spoke on the import-
ance of being good sports as
well as being good players. He
presented the Sportsmanship
Trophies to Donn Gutierrez
and Gloria Munoz.

Fitzgerald presented Jesus

Award for having attained
high grade averages through-
out eight years attendance at

Father Yermo and for being
good athletes as well.

Coaches John Maya, Miss
Gloria Mena, Tom Samaniego
and Al Hernandez awarded
medals to the outstanding var-
sity and pee-wee players in
each sport.

Medal winners were for vol-
leyball, varsity division, Lupi-
ta Corona and pee-wee divi-
sion, Patricia Marquez; foot-
ball, varsity division, Robert
Barajas and pee-wee division,
Johnny Maya and Ruben
Munoz; basketball, varsity
division, Juan Olmos and
Diana Duarte and pee-wee
division, Rudy Armendariz
and Edith Quiroz, and softball,
varsity division, Juan Castillo
and Lucy Rojas and pee-wee
division, Sergio Castillo.

Coach Maya and Coach
Mena also presented Certifi-
cates of Achievement to all
students who participated in
sports throughout the year.
