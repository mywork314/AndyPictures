2 Brothers
Win Games

Two brothers, pitching on
different teams, won games in
the Industrial Softball League
last night.

Pete Frausto hurled Local
1256 to an 8-2 win over Border
Machinery while Jaime Fraus-

ing to a $1 victory over Local
301. Carlos Dominguez hit
three for four for Farah and
Pete DeLeon homered.

theon, and, 9, Hicks vs.
Rudolph Chevrolet.
