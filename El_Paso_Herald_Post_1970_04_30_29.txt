Play Miners—

Title Piece

University of New Mexico
brings its Wolfpack to Dudley
Field tomorrow for a three
game Western Athletic Confer-
ence stand with the U.T. El
Paso Miner baseball team.

This is the last trip of the
season for the Lobos and it
could mean a piece of the
WAC title for them. New Mexi-
co dropped into a second place
tie with Arizona last week
after losing the series to Arizo-
na State.

THE MINERS on the other
hand, are hot under the collar
from a three game shellacking
they took from the Wildcats.
Coach Andy Cohen has had his
Miner team working on funda-
mentals all week.

One thing both head coaches
have in common is neither
knows who will pitch which
games. But both know who
their starters will be. For
UTEP Cohen will go with his
lefties Marc Bombard, Harry
Milner and Jim Greggerson.
Lobo coach Bob Leigh will go
with Jim Kremmel, Gary
Jacobs and Dan Ketcherside.

THIS IS THE SECOND time
the two have met this year,
although it’s only the first in
WAC competition. In the first
the Miners took the best two
out of three series, winning the
first two and dropping the
finale.

Leading the Lobo hitters is
Larry Minarsich, who is
swinging at a .398 clip. Minar-
sich also leads the team in
RBI’s with 27. He followed by
Bob Faford at .348 and Ken
Johnson at .333.

Game time for the opener
tomorrow will be 2 p.m. and
for twin bill Saturday 1 p.m.

The Miners are currently 2-7
in the WAC while the Lobos
are 5-4.
