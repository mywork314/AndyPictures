UA Faces Miners In
Not-So-Minor Series

By JAN PETRANEK
Citizen Sportswriter

Arizona’s baseball team will
depart for El Paso tomorrow
morning — and coach Frank
Sancet hopes to bring ’em back
alive.

The Wildcats, in deep trouble
in the WAC Southern Division
championship race, will open a
critical three-game series with
Texas-El Paso tomorrow at 2:30
p.m. at Dudley Field. The Cats
will conclude their UTEP tour
with a 1:30 p.m. doubleheader
Saturday.

Anything less than a three-
game sweep at UTEP could
mean premature death for Ari-
zona in the Southern Division.
UA has a 3-3 record and can’t

afford to fall any farther behind
Arizona State (5-1).

UA right-hander Leon Hooten
(9-1) has been nominated to
open against Miner lefty Harry
Milner (4-5) in tomorfow’s cur-
tain-raiser. The Wildcats will
follow with lefty Rich Hinton
(10-1) and righthander Fred
Burns (4-2) in the twinbill.
UTEP will counter with two
more southpaws, Mare Bomb-
ard (5-3) and Lynn Hunt (2-4).

Coach Andy Cohen, the old
pro, has been surprised by his
UTEP club. Cohen expected to
have good pitching and light hit-
ting this season.

He was wrong.

Instead, the Miners have had
solid hitting and unpredictable
