BEAUTY AND THE MARINES—Marines are famous for their
ability to take on and finish tough jobs. The Young Marines
Organization, with the backing of the Beautify E] Paso Assn.,
decided to clean up Concordia Cemetery. This past Saturday
a-large group set to work and after working almost all day,
they had cleared an area about the size of a football field
and accumulated a very large pile of weeds and trash. Above
are from left: Mrs. W. W. Lake and Andy Cohen of the
Beautify El Paso League; Richard Bouche, Victor Perez,
Ralph Orner, Elizeo Delgado and Ray Gillespie. Mr. Orner
is the group leader.
