In Sweep

University of Texas at Ei
Paso coach Andy Cohen has
seen a few baseball games at
Dudley Field so when the
veteran skipper talks . . .
people listen.

“T'll tell you that’s as fine a
pair of well-pitched games as
people have ever seen here,’’
Cohen remarked after his
Miners swept a doubleheader
from New Mexico Highlands >
0 and 2-1 yesterday. “I’ve never
seen two games like that on the
same day. And let me tell you
something else, that second
game was a Standout pitching
duel. Both those kids threw
great games.”

COHEN GOT route-going
efforts from righty Bob Knox
and southpaw Ron Wrobel to
extend UTEP’s season mark to
15-9. Knox, a _pencil-thin
sidewinder, tossed a two-hitter
in the opener while second
baseman Dave Patt sparked
two rallies with singles.

In the 10inning nightcap
Wrobel locked up with fellow
southpaw Charlie Schlosser for
the duration, emerging a
winner only when the Highland
rightfielder misplayed a
windblown flyball in the last of
the 10th.

WROBEL PICKED up his
fourth win in six outings,
permitting only three singles,
an unearned run and striking
out five. Schlosser worked out
of a little more difficulty,
scattering 11 Miner hits and
walking seven. The NHM
pitcher stranded 15 UTEP
baserunners.

Centerfielder Joe Pennies
went 4-for-4 in the nightcap and
had five hits on the day, topping
all Orange bats.
