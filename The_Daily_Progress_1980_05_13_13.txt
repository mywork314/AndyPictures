UTEP Coach Resigns

EL PASO, Texas (UPI) — University of
Texas at El Paso baseball coach Larry Mc-
Farlin resigned Monday for ‘“‘personal and
family reasons,”’ effective June 1.

McFarlin last week had completed his sec-
ond year as head baseball coach.

A former UTEP baseball player in the
1960s. McFarlin was named head coach in
September 1978 to replace longtime coach
Andy Cohen.

“During the two years it has been my
pleasure to work with a fine administration
and staff, but due to personal and family rea-
sons. I feel it is necessary to resign at this
time.” McFarlin said.
