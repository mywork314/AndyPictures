Cohen Batters
In Albuquerque

For Four Games

Coach Andy Cohen’s Universi-
ty of Texas at El Paso baseball
squad left today to play a four-
game series in Albuquerque
against both the University of
Albuquerque and University of
South Dakota.

Lynn Hunt was scheduled for
the opener tomorrow with the
Miners playing both the Albuqu-
erque and South Dakota squads
while games were slated for Fri-
day and Saturday.

OTHERS SELECTED as pos-
sible starters after a 10-day lay-
off from competition include
Jesse Lujan, Bill Onick, Terry
Witt, Ken McAnally and Don
Wilson. Howard MacDonald, the
tall cager, was left behind due
to a sore elbow which may be
operated on when school lets
out.

“We will try to maintain our
pace,” Cohen said today. ‘We
have an 11-10 record and want to
play over .500 ball this year. I
hope our long layoff won’t hurt
us because we have been prac-
ticing daily.”
