TEMPE, ARIZ. — Andy
Cohen's U.T. El Paso Miners
had tasted play beneath the
watchful eyes of 3000 baseball
fans today when they dropped
their second shutout of the
season. They lost to the Arizo-
na State Sun Devils 5-0, last
night.

But nevertheless, Cohen's
Miners set their sights on sole
possession of first place in the
Southern Division of the WAC.
If Cohen could sweep today’s
twin-bill they would hold a
one game lead over the Sun
Devils, who took first with the
win over the Miners.

ASU IS SITTING on top of a
3-1 record while the Miners
are tied for second with New
Mexico University at 2-2.

Cohen called the difference
in the game the mound per-
formance of ASU _ pitcher
Larry Gura. “He held us to
four hits and struck out 13 of
our batters,’ Cohen said. He
added, ‘this is the best pitch-
er we have faced so far this
season.”

The Miners looked as
though they were going to get
off the ground in grand fash-
ion in the first inning when
they had men on first and sec-
ond with no one out. But Gura
hurled his way out of the jam
letting them die there.

“WE COULDN'T get a man
past second after that,’’
Cohen said.

Harry Milner started for
the Miners and hurled what
Cohen called a game that
would have won any other
time, ‘He was working well,”
Cohen said. “But he let a cou-
ple of bad pitches in there for
homers.”*

John Dolinsek collected two
Arizona State homers.

The crowd last night was
the largest ever to se the Min-
ers play a baseball game.
