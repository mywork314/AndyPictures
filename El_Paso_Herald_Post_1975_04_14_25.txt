Andy, Miners
Turn Toward
Lobo Series

Andy Cohen’s U.T. El Pas.
baseball squad will try to pack
up the pieces this week and get
ready for a weekend journey
to Albuquerque and a three
game Western Athletic
Conference baseball series
with the University of New
Mexico.

The Miners were buzzed
twice last Saturday by
powerful Arizona State, 12-0
and 18-6. The visitors amassed
35 hits in the Dudley Field
twinbill while UTEP was held
to 11, only two in the opener by
Sun Devil righthander Greg
Cochran who fanned 12 and
didn’t walk a batter in picking
up his ninth victory without a
loss on the 1975 season.

“‘ASU HAS some excellent
pitching,’’ Cohen lamented,
“Cand their hitters handled our
staff pretty well. That’s not
much I can say after that.
Yes, I’m awfully disappointed
but now we've just got to get
ready to New Mexico.”
