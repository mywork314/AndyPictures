University of Texas at El
Paso Miner baseball coach
Andy Cohen is expected to
go with Mare Bombard on
the mound against the Arizo-
na State Sun Devils as the
two teams meet in the first
of a three-game series at
1:30 p.m. Friday at Dudley
Field.

Arizona State is leading
the southern division of the
Western Athletic Conference
with an 11-1 mark and needs

to win four of its six remain-
ing games to clinch the title.

The Sun Devils are expect-
ed to put Larry Gura on the
mound for the opening
game. Gura has a 13-1 sea-
son mark with his only
defeat coming at the hands
of rival Arizona. Gura was
named the outstanding
pitcher in the National Base-
ball Congress world tourna-
ment last summer in Wichi-
ta, Kan.

The two teams will square
off Saturday in a double-
header set to start at 12
noon. Harry Milner is
expected to get the nod from
Cohen for the first game
with lefty Lynn Hunt expect-
ed to start on the mound in
the nightcap.
