Miners Face
Big Series
With Aggies

University of Texas at El Paso, off to

a slow start in its baseball season, picked
up some momentum last week and will
try to keep it alive Friday and Saturday
when the Miners meet New Mexico State
University in a four-game series.

A doubleheader is set for 1 p.m. Fri-
day at Las Cruces with the two teams
moving to E] Paso’s Dudley Field Satur-
day for another 1 p.m. twin bill.

The Miners, with all but one player
returning from last year’s team, com-
piled a 3-9 mark through their first dozen
games but in the last five contests
showed signs of coming around as they
won four of those five encounters for a 7-
10 record to this point.

The last five games included a three-
game series with New Mexico Highlands,
last year’s NAIA champion, in which the
Miners took a 2-1 edge.

New Mexico State has had its troubles
also. The Aggies are winless in 10 games.

For New Mexico State, shortstop-pitch-
er Steve Loe and third baseman Clyde
Ziegler are the top hitters.

The Miners have three hitters batting
more than .300, led by outfielder Mike
Ward at .327. Ward also leads the team in
doubles with nine, has two home runs and
has hit safely in the last 10 games.

Johnny Miller at .308, Bobby Aguirre
-306 and Ron Hix at .302 are the Miners’
other .300 hitters. Hix leads the team in
RBIs with 14 followed by Charlie West
with 11.

Coach Andy Cohen has named lefthan-
der Lynn Hunt and righthander Jess
Lujan as starting pitchers for Friday’s
doubleheader.

Hunt, a junior, has a 3-2 record with a
3.60 earned run average. Lujan has a 1-3
mark with a 5,22 era.

Cohen said Howard MacDonald, a 6-
foot-8 basketball player who came out for
baseball at the end of the cage season,
would start one of the Saturday games.

New Mexico State's first three starters
are expected to be Fred Eyhrabide, Curt
Montmann and Lee Leonard.
