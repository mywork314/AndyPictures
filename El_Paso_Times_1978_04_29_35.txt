By MARK LEIBSON
Times Sports Writer

Craig Chamberlain hurled a masterful six-hitter
Friday while striking out 11 batters as the Universi-
ty of Arizona bombed the University of Texas at El
Paso 11-0 at Dudley Field.

Chamberlain, a 190-pound junior college transfer
from Los Alamos, Cal., upped his record for the
season to +1 and 2-0 over UTEP. The Wildcats are
now 6-4 in Western Athletic Conference play and 32-
10 for the year while UTEP dropped to 2-8 in WAC
and 26-19 overall.

“He’s a good pitcher, there’s no doubt about
that,” praised Miner coach Andy Cohen. “I also
thought our man (Jim Socco) did pretty good until
he gave up that homer to (Bob) Woodside.””

For four innings, the Miners stayed within one
run of the Wildcats until a disastrous four-run fifth
inning and an equally harmful sixth where four
more Arizona runners crossed home plate.

“This is the worst day of my baseball career,”
moaned substatite left fielder Bill Roberts who was
unable to catch up with a short fly to left in the fifth
which would have ended the inning before Wood-
side’s sixth homer of the year drove in three of the
four runs.

The Miners trailed by only one run when
Arizona's Jim Morley was on second base with two
outs in the fifth. Les Pearsey looped a short fly
toward Roberts who was playing deep although a
strong wind was blowing in from left.

Pearsey’s blooper feel at Roberts’ feet, scoring
Morley and prolonging the inning which eventually
led to another Wildcat taking four balls from Sacco
who is now 5-4 for the year and Woodside’s blast
over the fence in left-center which sent Sacco to the
showers.

The sixth inning was more embarrassing for
Roberts who was filling in for Danny Haygood, a
victim of a separated shoulder which has sidelined
the speedster for the remainder of the season.

After Tony Incavigiia reached third base with one
out, Scott Overlund slapped a single to left which
went through Roberts for a two-base error. “‘I look-
ed up to see where the runner was,” explained
Roberts, “‘and the ball went right through me.”

Morely followed with a run-scoring double before
Pearsey slammed his 13th home run of the year in
the same spot that Woodside hit his the inning
before.

Arizona added an unearned run in the seventh
and one in the ninth when Incaviglia socked his se-
cond four-bagger.

UTEP had several scoring opportunities but
could never make Chamberlain bend. Arizona turn-
ed a double play in the second inning, wiping out
Jerry Walker's crossing of home plate.

In the fifth and sixth innings, UTEP’s first two
batters reached base with no outs only to be strand-
ed.

Arizona and UTEP conclude their three-game
series at 11 a.m, Saturday at Dudley Field.
