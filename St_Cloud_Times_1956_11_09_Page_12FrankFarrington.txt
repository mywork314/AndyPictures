AMONG THE most popular of all in the long parade of Northern
league baseball managers was Andy Cohen of the Eau Claire
Braves. He was popular with the people, the players and with the
press.

Besides, he was good. He had a faculty of making his players
put out, without suffering any agony while doing it. He made the

game a pleasure for all and it was a pleasure
for all to make a contact with Andy.
He won pennants with Eau Claire in the
Northern, Denver in the Western before mov-
ing on to manage the New Orleans Pelicans
in the Southern these past two seasons.
President Donie Bush of the Indianapolis
Indians, who just announced the hiring of
Cohen for that Triple A club, said that the
Chicago White Sox have been trying to get
Cohen into their organization for several
years.
And they will not be sorry—even though
Cohen is stepping into the shoes of Kerby
Ferrell, who won pennants at Indianapoils the last two years and
the Little World Series this fall.

Andy’s got it—and his old Polar pals are happy to hear he’s

on his way up. He’s a nice guy who never finished last.
