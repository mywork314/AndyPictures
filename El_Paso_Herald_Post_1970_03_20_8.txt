Cohen's Nine
Adds Shutout
Win to List

Coach Andy Cohen’s U.T. El
Paso Miner team has another
shutout in its collection today.

The latest came over the
University of Colorado Buffal-
oes, 3-0, at Dudley Field yes-
terday behind the strong left
arm of Jim Greggerson.

Greggerson going the dist-
ance for the Miners, collected
his first college win and scat-
tered only seven Buff hits.

“Greggerson was the whole
game,’’ Cohen said. ‘‘His fast
ball and curve were working
well,.”’

The Miners scored in the
eighth via a squeeze play set
up on a perfect bunt by Tim
Pedin.

Cohen had no lineup changes
for Colorado State College
today and righthander Bill
Disselhorst got the nod.
