NL owners set

Chub Feeney is under fire from one
segment of owners and faces a fight
next month at the major league win-

League. The decision must be made
this year under the terms of his con-

contract runs out on Dec. 31, 1977, will
be re-hired for at least three more
years, but it’s almost certain he will
have to agree to move the league of-
