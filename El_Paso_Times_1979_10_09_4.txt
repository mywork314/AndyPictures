Play ball

EL PASO’S Texas League Diablos may not be
in the World Series, which begins tonight
in Baltimore. Yet there’ll be a Southwest-

ern flavor to the series, anyhow.

One Baltimore Orioles’ player — infielder
Billy Smith — was a Diablo several years ago
when he was in the California Angels’ farm
system. And manager Chuck Tanner of the
opposing Pittsburgh Pirates managed the old
El Paso Sun Kings three different times.

Even veteran Pittsburgh slugger Willie
Stargell played close by in his second year of
professional baseball back in the 1950s as a
member of the Roswell-San Angelo club in the
old Sophomore League.

And, as Times Sports Editor John Laird
notes in his column this morning, E] Paso’s
“Mr. Baseball,’’ Andy Cohen, managed both
Tanner and Baltimore skipper Earl Weaver
when the latter two were in the minor
leagues.

So, even though El Paso is a long way from
Baltimore or Pittsburgh, we may be closer to
the action than the miles indicate.
