Is New E.P. Boss

By BOB INGRAM

Monty Basgall today
resigned as manager of El
Paso Sun Dodgers, saying the
emotional strain of running the
club was too big a burden on
him.

Stan Wasiak, manager of the
Daytona Beach club in the
Florida State League, was
named to succeed Basgall.

He will take over the Sun

Dodgers tomorrow night.
’ Andy Cohen, the first base
coach of the Sun Dodgers, will
direct the team tonight in its
third game of the series with
the Alexandria, La., Aces.
