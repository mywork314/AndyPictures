Members of El Paso Sports Association Inc., are keeping
their fingers crossed this year. This is El Paso’s sixth year
back in organized baseball and the club underwriters haven’t
had to bail out the ball club yet. .

El Paso is one of the few remaining clubs in the country
owned and operated. “by the community on a non-profit basis.

The unique El Paso Sports

~ Association consists of some 40

local business and professional
men who underwrite the base-
ball operation.

A run down of the Texas
League roster shows the cur-

rent prevailing trend in base--

ball. The Amarillo Sonics are
owned outright by the Houston

Two Texas millionaires,
