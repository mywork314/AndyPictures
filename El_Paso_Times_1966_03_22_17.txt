After a national champion in

behold, the Miner  baseballers

year in the school’ shistory.

The Miners opened the season
with a four game sweep over
the College of Albuquerque and
took three of four from New
Mexico Highlands to run their
season record to seven victories
and one defeat.

The Miners will play the Uni-
versity of Denver Wednesday and
Thursday at Dudley Field with
both encounters scheduled for 2

 

p.m.

Miner Coach Andy Cohen is
hopeful that Bill Sanders will be
ready to go in on one of the games
during the two game set. Sanders
is recovering from the flu and
had to miss the series with High-
lands. Sanders pitched a four-
hit shutout in the opening game
of the season for TWC.

Tom Jennings pushed his rec-
ord to 2-0 for the 1966 season
with a victory over Highlands
last Friday and should be a
starter in one of the games
against Denver this week.
