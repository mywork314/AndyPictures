Tribesmen Play
Rain-Called Tie

By MAX GREENWALD

Indianapolis was within one
out of defeat and within one
hit of victory in the eighth in-
ning last night when play was
stopped because of rain.

After about a half-hour
wait, the game with Minneap-
olis was called. And the score

| reverted back to the seventh

inning for a 2-2 tie. It will be
replayed at Minneapolis later
this month,

For the official seven
frames, the tilt was a mound
duel between the Tribe's Jim
McDonald and Southpaw Pete
Burnside, each allowing seven
hits.

BURNSIDE, who had eight
strikeouts, retired the first 11
batters. Then, with two out,
in the fourth, singles by Ron
Jackson, Sam Mele and John
Romano provided an Indian-
apolis run,

|
In the fifth, Jose Valdivielso

tripled off the right-field wall
and stole home. The theft was
a dandy as it caught the Mil-
lers asleep and Catcher Bob
Schmidt sheepishly made a
half-hearted try for the tag

Minneapolis had scoring
chances in the first and second
innings but McDonald bore
down to prevent any scoring.
A running catch by Mele in
the second was helpful

The Millers registered twice
in the fifth, the tallies being
set up by Bob Prescott’s single
and a double off the score-
board by Wayne Terwilliger.
Prescott tallied after Don
Grate’s sacrifice fly and Ter-
williger came home on Bill
Taylor's wrongway double to
left.

Most of the excitement was
in the erased eighth. Minne-
apolis scored three times, two
of the runs being chalked up
on Prescott’s triple off the wall
in right.

The rain, which started
in the sixth, turned into a_
deluge as the Indians came
to bat. There were two out
before Mele doubled. Ro-
mano’s single provided a

Fernando Rodriguez relieved
Burnside and Jim Delsing en-
tered the tilt as a pinchhitter.
The count was 2-2 as Man-
ager Andy Cohen and Coach
Johnny Hutchings of the’ In-
dians were arguing a called
strike with Umpire John
Mullen.

Mullen suddenly raised his
arms, signifying play was
halted. And it never resumed,
forcing elimination of Rod-
riguez and Delsing from the
box score.
