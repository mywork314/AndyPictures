Downing Blasts Key

Miner Twinbill Sweep

Bob Downing smashed a pair
of homers and Coach Andy
Cohen got heip from a 10th man
(Cowboys) as University of
Texas at El Paso’s Miners
swept a pair of collegiate
baseball sets from New Mexico
Highlands Thursday in Dudley
Field.

The Miners overcame a 4-1
deficit in the first game and
roared back for a 6-5 victory.
then bombed the Cowboys in all
but two innings for a 10-4
twilight win behind the superb
hurling of Mike Kruta.

The wins pushed U.T. El Paso
to a 5-3 mark on the 1974 season
in preparation for Saturday’s
noon encounter hosting

Oklahoma’s strong Sooners in a
single.

Downing poled his round-

trippers in the second and third
innings, scoring two off the
second blast setting the tone for
U.T. El Paso’s best _ per-
formance. run-wise, all year
(11).

Coach Cohen singled out
Downing’s heavy stick and the
relief pitching of Larry
Richardson (starter Dave
Jannusch went five) in the first
game as outstanding efforts.
“Dave really looked good in

there, but sort of ran out of
gas,’ he said. Jannusch was
tagged with five hits and four

runs while sidelining two

Cowboys.

Bob Whitney. tagged with the
second-game loss for
Highlands, gave the Miners all
the help they needed in the big
fifth inning. Mike Miller sailed
one over the right field wall
followed by Glenn Villarreal’s
triple. Downing followed with a
single (scoring Villarreal) and
Roy Stringer slammed one that
almost reached the wall. Joe
Pennies caught one of Whit-
ney’s first offers and sent it
over the right field wall for the
fourth run.

Cohen pointed out the barrage
as outstanding. in that the trio
are all switch-hitters.

Kruta went the route on the

Miner-mound and earned
Coach Cohen’s praise for “‘real.
strong pitching. Kruta gave up
the four tallies on nine hits

while striking out two facing 29
batters.

Both clubs only committed
one error each in the game.

Saturday’s contest should -
provide a test for the Miners
now that their team spirit is up
after two wins.

The Sooners were fifth last
year in the College World
Series of Baseball and captured
the Big Eight Conference. It’s
only the second game of the
year for Oklahoma, with no

reports on their progress thus
far.
