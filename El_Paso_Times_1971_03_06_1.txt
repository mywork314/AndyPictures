Q. On all Coors beer cans there is a
note that states that all aluminum beer
cans may be recycled. I feel that this is
to cut down on garbage, but where in El

Paso can a person send these beer cans
or have them picked up?—H.E.

A. Andrew Cohen, public relations and
sales manager for Dickshire Coors, said
that Dickshire accepts Coors cans and
any aluminum beer cans from 12 to 3
p.m. Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday at
the company, 203 N. Chelsea, and pays 10
cents per pound. Last week Cohen said,
Dickshire took in 30,000 pounds of cans,
and during the year has taken in more
than 300,000 pounds, representing
6,900,000 cans which Dickshire has helped
to keep off the roads and highways. After
crushing and baling in El Paso, the cans
are sent to the Coors brewery in Denver
for recycling, he said.
