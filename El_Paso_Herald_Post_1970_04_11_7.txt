Minermen
Find It’s
Hl Wind

By MIKE PEMBERTON

A little gust of wind may
have kept Andy Cohen’s U.T.
Miners from beating the Uni-
versity of Arizona Wildcats in
the opening game of the West-
ern Athletic Conference series
at Dudley Field.

Slugging Tim Peden, Miner
catcher, was up to bat in the
ninth inning and there were
two men on base. He hit what

looked like a homer. A sud-
den breeze, however, blowing

from the East, held the ball up
and Keith Rhodes of Arizona
caught the ball up against the
left field fence.

“IF THAT wind hadn't been
blowing straight in, the ball
would have gone out,’ said
Cohen.

The Wildcats won 5-3. The
teams played a doubleheader
at Dudley Field today.

The Cats, out-hitting the
Miners 13-8, got a route job
from Leon Hooten. It gave him
a 7-2 season’s record. Marc
Bombard also went the route
for the Miners. The Cats
scored two tainted runs off
him in the second.
