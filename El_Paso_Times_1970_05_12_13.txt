BEAUTIFY EL PASO — Beer cans
littering city streets and yards do
little toward projecting a favorable
image of El Paso, 30 Memorial Park
School and Adolph Coors Co. are
doing their part with Beautify El
Paso Association to gather up the
offending metal. John de Frank,
who is official can crusher at the
school, helps keep El Paso clear,

with encouragement from Mrs.

Edith Zanker, left, of Memorial
Park; Mrs. Shirley Christensen,
secretary, Beautify El Paso As-
sociation, and Andy Cohen, El Paso
sports and civic figure, and presi-
dent, Beautify El Paso Association.
Coors offers the school 10 cents a
pound for crushed cans.
