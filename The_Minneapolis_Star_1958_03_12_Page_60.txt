VERSATILE MILLERS DON’T
LACK FOR UTILITY PLAYER

sales

Special to the Minneapolis Star

DELAND, Fla.—With versatility as the keynote of
the 42-man Miller squad in spring training here, Minne-
apolis won't lack for a utility man this season.

Jack Phillips has been working at first base, but he
can play any position in the infield, and played third
base in Tuesday’s intra-squad game,

Harry Malmberg is a fielding genius at any infield
position, as is Red Robbins, 30-year-old rookie from Okla-
homa City. Robbins has the inside track for a utility
post and stroked .273 at Oklahoma City last year with 97
RBIs and 27 homers.

Pumpsie Green, a switch hitter, can play second,
short or third, and the same is true of rookie Yogi Her-
genrader. Art Schult can be used in the outfield or at
first. Don Gile has experience at first, behind the plate
and in the outfield.
