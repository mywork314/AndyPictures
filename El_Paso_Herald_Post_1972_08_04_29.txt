El Paso Herald-Post (El Paso, Texas) - Fri, Aug 4, 1972 - Page 29

 

EL PASO HOMER—It was a futile blow as far as winning
was concerned but it was a big event for Wayne Burney in
the second inning of last night's Texas League game with
Shreveport at Dudley Field. Burney’s singleton homer tied
the score at the time 2-2. Shreveport won 12-2. Burney is
shown rounding third base and being welcomed by Andy
Cohen who was acting manager in the absence of Stan
Wasiak. (Herald-Post Photo by Bill Thompson).

 

Signs With Phoenix

Multi Year Pact
For Scott English

PHOENIX, (UPI) —Scott English of Texas-El Paso, the
third round draft pick of the National Basketball Assn.
Phoenix Suns, has signed a multi-year contract with the club,
General Manager Jerry Colangelo announced.

English, a 6-foot 6-inch, 220 pound forward, led the Miners
in scoring with 16.4 points per game last season and was the
second leading rebounder on the team.

This summer English was a member of an amateur
Athletic Union team that toured Europe, where he averaged
30 points per game.
