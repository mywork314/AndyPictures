disappointment to the
New York Giants in 1926
and again in 1928-29.

They were hoping he
would be the club’s first
Jewish gate attraction.
But Cohen, both a sec-
ond baseman and short-
stop, had a tough act to

follow. First time up,
he was replacing Ro-
gers Hornsby, next time
Frankje Frisch. Cohen
completed his playing
career in the minors,
later became a pennant
winning manager in the
Pittsburgh system, and
