Miner Hardballers
Open Second Half

Injury-riddled University of Texas at El Paso begins the second
half of conference play Friday against Arizona needing only one
victory to set the school record for most baseball victories in a sea-

son.

The Miners are 26-18 for the season and 2-7 in Western Athletic
Conference play following last weekend's three-game series in Al-
we where UTEP won one and dropped two games to New

lexico.

Finding that one victory in the 1 p.m. contest at Dudley Field may
be somewhat of a task for the Miners who have recently lost the
services of regular leftfielder Danny Haygood and backup out-
fielder Art Flores with shoulder seperations.

Second baseman Art Bravo's services are also questionable. The
UTEP captain pulled a hamstring several weeks ago and his avail-
ability is a day-to-day question mark for Coach Andy Cohen.

“We're really hurting in the outfield. Losing Haygood really hurts
us because of his speed and his bat,” said Cohen Thursday.

Haygood’s departure for the remainder of the season leaves
UTEP with four outfielders and utility infielder Orlando Cordova
who has had outfield experience in the past.

“We'll probably put Bill Roberts in right and move Dan Feagler to
left,” said Cohen.

One of UTEP’s two WAC victories was against Arizona in Tucson
two weeks ago. Since then, the Wildcats hit a torrid slump by losing
three straight games to rival Arizona State, thus dropping Arizona's
ae record to 54 behind the Southern Division leading Sun

evils.

The last time Arizona came to town, the Miners pulled off a
double upset by sweeping a twinbill in 1977.

“We're hurting a little but by no means are we giving up,” added
Cohen. “When we beat Arizona over in Tucsgn, our boys acted like
we had just won the World Series. They know what it takes to beat a
good team like Arizona.”

Jim Sacco, supporting a 58 record and a strikeout ratio of little
more than one per inning is scheduled to start on the mound for the
Miners

Both teams will return to Dudley Field for a Saturday double-
header beginning at 11 a.m.
