El Paso Herald-Post (El Paso, Texas) - Mon, Feb 28, 1972 - Page 23

Andy Likes
Miner 9’s
Performance

Texas-El] Paso baseball men-
tor Andy Cohen was pleased
and proud of the performance
his Miner nine put as it swept
a twin-bill from Ranger Junior
College at Dudley Field.

The Miners edged a 2-1 vic-
tory behind the pitching of
converted hurler Tim Peden in
the first game and then scored
three early runs in the second
game and hung on for a 32
win.

“TI felt proud because Rang-
er is one of the finest junior
colleges in the country. They
were ranked fourth in the
nation last year,”’ said Cohen.

COHEN SAID Peden had
good stuff on the ball the first
game and was using a fast ball
and slider as his Sunday pitch-
es.

The Miners came from
behind to win the first game as
Joel Kunster rapped an inside
the park homer to tie it up.
Lali Maldonado then drove in
the winning run.

MIKE BELTRAN hurled the
second game and kept Ranger
intact until the late innings
when the big Ranger first
baseman tagged a high curve
and sent it on a ride.
