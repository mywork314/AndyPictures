UTEP Miners

El Paso Diablo General Man-
ager Jim Paul completed his
team’s 1975 baseball schedule
today with the announcement
of an April 9th exhibition game
with Andy Cohen's University
of Texas at El Paso diamond
squad.

The revelation came on the
heels of the Diablos’ agreement
with the Mexican League
Juarez Indios to meet in a home
and home exhibition series
May 11 (Juarez) and July 21
(El Paso).

EL PASO’S other exhibition
game will be against its older
brother Salt Lake City club,
Class AAA affiliates of the Cali-
fornia Angels, on Aug. 14 in El
Paso.

Paul had also attempted to
negotiate a home and home ex-
hibition series with the AAA
Pacific Coast League Albu-
querque Dukes, AAA ballclup
of the Los Angeles Dodgers.
The Duke City roster contains
many former El Paso (1972)
standouts and former El Paso
business manager Willie San-
chez is now general manager of
the Dukes. However, LA offi-
cials were not enthused with
Paul's plan.

THE DIABLO-MINER game
will be a night time affair
(7:30), giving new El Paso
manager Jim Williams and his
crew their first look at Dudley
under the lights.
