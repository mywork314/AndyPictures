‘Hitterish’
Miners
Take Two

“Hitterish’’ was the way
coach Andy Cohen described
the U.T. El Paso miner base-
ball team today after they took
Highlands University 13-4 and
10-3 at Dudley Field.

“We put everything together
and hit the ball real well,”
Cohen said. “It just seemed
like everything jelled for us.”

TWO OF COHEN'S lefties
collected wins for the day.
Marc Bombard went the dis-
tance in the first and Harry
Millner the distance in the sec-
ond.

“Both pitchers were real
steady throughout the game,”
Cohen said.

‘Leading the Miners at the
plate were Al Molberg, Bob
Poprokowicz and Rick Butler.

Cohen said, “after the game
their coach came up to me and
ask how we lost to Regis the
way we did.”’

Jim Greggerson got the call
for the Miners on the mound
