FORT YEARS AGO
January 11, 1928
(Wednesday)

Duties collected at the port
of E] Paso were up more than
a third last year over 1926.
The bulk of the increase,
according to W. W. Carpenter,
assistant collector of customs,
was from cattle imports.

Local sports fans saw a good
chance for Andy Cohen, El
Paso baseball star, to stay
with the New York Giants in
the sale of Rogers Hornsby to
Boston, announced today.

Miss Bertha Pool, sister of
State Rep. Adrian Pool, died
yesterday in the home of her
sister, Mrs. Van C. Wilson,
3205 Pershing.
