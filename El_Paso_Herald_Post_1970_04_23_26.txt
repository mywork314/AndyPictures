EL P.
Against Miners—

Wildcats
May Be
Revengetul

Andy Cohen takes his U.T
El Paso Miner baseball team
to Arizona tomorrow for a
three game series.

He will probably face a
“revengeful” Wildcat team in
the series.

NOT ONLY will the Arizona
team be hot under the collar
from the loss they were issued
by the Miners during their
stay in El! Paso when the two
met before, but they are com-
ing off a three game sweep by
New Mexico last week.

Cohen will undoubtedly go
with his lefthanders again in
the series. Marc Bombard
should get the call in the open-
er with Jim Greggerson and
Harry Milner doing duty in the
doubleheader Saturday.

Greggerson was the only
Miner pitcher to win against
the Cats in the El Paso series.
Greggerson’s record is now
5-2.

BOMBARD is coming offa
masterful job on the Arizona
State Sun Devils. He hurled
what Cohen called the finest
game of his career handcuf-
fing the Devils 11-1.

The Miners will face Wildcat
ace Leon Hooten in the opener
with Mickey O’Hara and Larry
Dierks working the twin bill.

Bob Propokowicz still leads
the Miners in hitting with a
454 average. Rick Butler is
next in line at .352.
