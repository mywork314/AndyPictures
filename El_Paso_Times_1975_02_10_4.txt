TWENTY YEARS AGO
February 10, 1955
(Thursday)

Andy Cohen, who reached the
top as a schoolboy basketball
player for El Paso High School
and as a professional player,
has been named the first
member of the El Paso Athletic
Hall of Fame.

The State Supreme Court in
Austin upheld the validity of El
Paso’s $1.5 million bond issue
for construction of off-street
parking facilities.

The arrest of several burgla-
ry suspects ended a grueling.
four-day high-tension stakeout
by officers of El Paso Police
Department.

W. S. “Bill” Crombie Jr.,
chairman of the Central YMCA
Membership Campaign,
termed the annual drive a “big
success.”
