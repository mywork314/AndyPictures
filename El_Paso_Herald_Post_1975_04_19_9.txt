NM Lobos Shove
Miners in Cellar

Special to The Herald-Post

ALBUQUERQUE — Andy
Cohen today hoped his U.T. El
Paso Miners could avoid a one-
inning collapse such as oc-
curred yesterday when they
lost the first game of a Western
Athletic Conference series
against the New Mexico Lobos.

They got off to a three-run
lead over the Lobos but New
Mexico came back with six of
their own in the sixth to win 6-3
and shove the Miners in the
WAC cellar with an 6-7 record.
New Mexico is 1-6.

After holding the Lobos
scoreless through the fifth, Ron
Wrobel was touched for six
runs in the sixth. Errors helped
cause his downfall. Lobo Jim

_ Weber went all the way for New
Mexico and hurled eight in-
nings of shutout ball.
