UTEP Splits With Aggies-
Cohen Encouraged

AfterMiner Win

By MIKE PEMBERTON

Coach Andy Cohen was
encouraged today when his
U.T. El Paso Miner baseball-
ers began preparation for a
Western Athletic Conference
series with New Mexico Uni-
versity.

Cohen’s reason for encour-
agement, today, is his win
over the New Mexico State
Aggies in a split twin-bill at
Hugo Meyer Field yesterday
which the Aggies won the
opener 9-8 and the Miners the
second bout 2-1.

COHEN SAID, “‘the differ-
ence in the two ball games
was the pitching. We got the
pitching in the second game
and when we finally got it in
the first we were too far
behind to catch up.”

“In the first game my pitch-
ers got behind on the batters
and then had to serve them
the fat pitch. In the first
inning we gave uptwo walks
that eventually scored; this
might have been the differ-
ence in the score,”” Cohen
said.

Despite an 11 hit batting
attack, which included three
homers, the Miners could only
get within one run off the
Aggies. The homers were

lashed out by Ron Hix and
