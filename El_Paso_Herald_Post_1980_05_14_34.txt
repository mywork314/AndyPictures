By JIM THOMAS

Despite losing nearly all of their
Western Athletic Conference-South
Division baseball games (1-22-1) and
most of their games overall (22-29-1),
former Miner head baseball coach
Larry McFarlin still saw some posi-
tive things.

Unfortunately, he won't be around
— at least not at the head job as he
handed in his resignation Monday —
next season to watch his first real
recruited class continue,

“When I took over the program (he
replaced long time coach Andy Cohen

who retired) I knew I would be in for a
big challenge but it was something I
had to do,” McFarlin explained. “The
program at UTEP was down a bit and
there was a lot of work to be done.
Because I took over so late (towards
the end of summer 1978) I couldn't
really recruit, I had to go with what
we had.”

THE MINERS last season beat
powerful Oklahoma University three
out of four games, dropped top-five
rated Wichita State and started off the
WAC season with a win over San
Diego State. The Miners then ran out
of gas and lost their remaining 17
straight.

“T was able to recruit my first class
after the season ended and I think we
came up with some pretty good peo-
ple. I went after pitching mostly and

picked up a few players,’ he said,

Those players went on to have a
good season for the Miners and their
demanding expanded schedule. The
1980 team set 11 school records and it
was his new recruits which did a ma-
jority of the work, Newcomer Jimmy
Walker, who transfered in from Mesa,
Ariz., Junior College, estabilished a
new RBI mark of 50. But by the time
the season had ended senior third ba-
seman Mike Brooks reset the mark at
52. Ironically, McFarlin and his assis-
tant Bob Alsup, held the record of 48
in 1967 and 1977, respectively.

JC transfer first baseman Tommy
Thompson set the record for most tri-
ples with seven (Alsup and Charlie
West '67 held the old standard of six).
Outfielder Dan Haygood drew a rec-
ord 41 walks.

Besides individual marks, several
team records were set including most
hits in a season (518; old mark 490 set
in 1970); most at bats (1,678; old 1,556
in 1978); most home runs (38; old 31 in
1968); most doubles (95; old 31 in
1968); most team RBI (365; old 276 in
1976); most stolen bases (64; old 45 in
1978); most sacrifices (42; old 35 in
1978) and most times walked (275; old
219 in 1977).
