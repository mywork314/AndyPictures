Special to The Heraid-Post

PHOENIX — Two simple
plays proved to be the key for
Arizona State as they were
able to down U.T.-El Paso 5-2
last night.

The two teams played an
afternoon game today and the
second game of the double-
header was set tonight with
Miner coach Andy Cohen
expected to start junior right-
hander Mike Kruta (0-1) on
the mound.

Arizona State had scored an
unearned run in the first
inning via an error by U.T.-El
Paso’s Herb von Hagen. The
Sun Devils loaded the bases in
the bottom of the second on an
infield grounder.

“(Jeff) Hogan didn’t get
over to first to cover the bag,”
remarked Cohen. “Gary
Atwell then hit a line drive
just over Von Hagen’s head
and, what should have been a
single, got in bteween Larry
McKay and Malcolm McLean
and rolled all the way to the
wall for an inside-the-park
home run.”

DESPITE THE mistakes,
Cohen was pleased “with the
way the boys hung in there to
the end and almost pulled the
game out.”

Miner relief pitcher Bob
Knox extended his string of
innings without allowing an
earned run to 342-3 by hurling
6 1-3 frames last night. He was
in trouble several times with
his control as he walked five
and struck out only two.

“His control was not as good
as it has been,"’ Cohen added,
“but he baffled them the rest
of the way.

“ARIZONA State's pitcher
Ed Bane was the difference in
the game. He is a smart pitch-
er with good control. He only
walked one and struck out 14.

Cohen was worried over the
injuries to Ray Greggerson
and Barry Alburtis.

“]T was planning on Starting
Greggerson last night, but he |
stepped on a sprinkler head
with his pivot foot at the
motel. We have him bandaged
up right mow and we don’t
know when he will return.
Alburtis rehurt his shoulder
while diving for a ground ball
in the first inning. We will see
on how it heals before we play
him again.”
