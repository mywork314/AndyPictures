Las Cruces
To Host

Tournament

Special to EL PASO TIMES

LAS CRUCES, N.M. — Las
Cruces will host the American
Legion state baseball tourna-
ment Aug. 15-17 at Apodaca
Park.

Tournament director Al Apo-
daca says that six teams will
take part in the tournament
for the state title. Taking part
will be teams from Los Ala-
mos, Albuquerque, Alamogor-
do, Las Cruces and either Lov-
ington or Roswell. The Las
Cruces team will defend its
title won last year in Santa
Fe.

Las Cruces Joe Quesenberry
Post 10 of the American
Legion will host the participat-
ing players to a banquet at
New Mexico State University’s
Milton Student Center on Aug.
14.

Dr. Edwin L. Peterson of
Logan, Utah, National Vice
Commander of the American
Legion will be the main speak-
er. Also scheduled to address
the banquet is Andy Cohen of
El Paso, a former major
league baseballl player.
