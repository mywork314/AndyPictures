Face ASU

In Series

If it wasn’t for the fact that
his Miners must face double
tough Arizona State in a
three-game series this week-

end, Coach Andy Cohen

would, no doubt, feel good
about the way his Texas-El
Paso baseballers opened
Western Athletic Conference
play.

The Miners chilled the New
Mexico Lobos in two of the
three opening game series at
Albuquerque last week and
now are tied with Arizona
State for the southern division
leadership in the 'WAC. Both
the Miners and ASU stand 2-1
after the Sun Devils swept
two of three from Arizona last
week.
