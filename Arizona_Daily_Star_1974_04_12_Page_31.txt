By DAVE ADAM
Star Staff Writer

Chapter Two of the David and Goliath story
unfolds today when the Texas-El Paso Miners
invade Wildcat Field for the first game of a
three-game Western Athletic Conference
Southern Division series against the top-
ranked University of Arizona baseball team

Playing the title role of David is UTEP
right-hander Roy Stringer. Goliath is the Wild-
cat batters who sport a solid .353 average and

have scored better than 11 runs per game

The two teams play a 3 p.m. single game
today and wind up the series with a 1 p.m.
doubleheader Saturday. KTUC-Radio (1400)
will broadcast today’s game and the first half
of the twin bill Saturday. KZAZ-11 will televise
the first game Saturday.

Stringer, a 175-pound junior, picked up the
biggest win of his pitching career when he
shocked defending WAC champion Arizona
State 7-5 in El Paso last Friday

The transfer from Yavapai Junior college is
6-0 for the season but was used mostly in the
outfield at Yavapai. He was listed as an out-
fielder by the UTEP baseball press guide at
the beginning of the season and he bats for
himself when he is on the mound
