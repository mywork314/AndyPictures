Tom Lea’s Works Inspire
Library Association Party

Merienda
To Be Held

Sunday

A Merienda hosted by the El
Paso Public Library Assn. will
open the Library’s exhibition
of Tom Lea’s published works
from 3 to 5 p.m. Sunday. The
public is invited to attend the
reception which is being held
in conjunction with the publi-
cation of “A Bibliography of
Writings and Illustrations by
Tom Lea.”
