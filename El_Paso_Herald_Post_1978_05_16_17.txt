Cohen retires
as UTEP coach

Andy Cohen has announced his retirement as
baseball coach of the U.T, El Paso, but likely will
stay on as a consultant on the team.

Cohen turned in his resignation following the end
of the season Saturday. He gave the reason as his
age.

He coached the Miners for 16 years. He's had a
long, successful and a colorful career in the game,
So much so that he’s known as “Mr. Baseball” in El
Paso.
