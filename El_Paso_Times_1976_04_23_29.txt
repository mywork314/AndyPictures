Miners Test
Mighty ASU

Special To The El Paso Times

TEMPE, Ariz. — The
University of Texas at El Paso
will begin a three-game series
at 7 p.m. Friday against
Arizona State's Sun Devils, the
nation’s top-ranked baseball
team.

A day-night doubleheader
Saturday will conclude the first
round of the Western Athletic
Conference action.

The mighty Sun Devils were
surprised in Albuquerque last
week by New Mexico by a 6-4
count sandwiched between two
victories. The WAC loss
dropped ASU's conference
record to 5-1. Overall, Arizona
State is 44-7 while the Miners
are 7-13 and 1-5in the WAC.

“The thing about my team is
that the better the teams we
play, the better we seem to
play,’’ said UTEP Coach Andy
Cohen. ‘They're the number
one team in the nation and it'll
be interesting to see how we
do against Arizona State.”

UTEP is currently riding a
six-game losing streak, dating
back to the second game of a
doubleheader in El Paso
against New Mexico's Lobos
April 10.

Right-hander Dave Jannusen
will hurl Friday’s game against
ASU while Jeff Hogan and
Jerry Walker will work the two
Saturday games.
