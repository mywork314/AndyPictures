Hunt, Lujan To Start
For Miner Nine Today

By RODGER McKNOWN

Lynn Hunt and Jesse Lujan are slated
to get the nods as starting pitchers for
the Miners Friday when University of
Texas at El Paso opens its 1968 baseball
with a doubleheader at Dudley Field
against University of New Mexico.

First game of the twin bil! is set for 11
am. with the second game to follow
about 20 minutes after the end of the
first

Hunt and Lujan were the mainstays in
the Miner mound staff last season, win-
ning eight games each, and coach Andy
Cohen is counting on them to handle a
good share of the hurling duties again
this year.

Hunt, a southpaw, compiled an 8-3
record last season with a 1.73 earned run
average. Lujan had an 8-1 mark with a
4.12 earned run average.

The righthanded Lujan is a 5-foot-9,
150-pound sophomore. Hunt, a junior, is
5-foot-1044 and weighs 168 pounds.

The same two teams meet again at 10
a.m. Saturday in a single game at Dudley
Field.

Cohen is undecided on his starting
hurler for the Saturday contest. If he
wants a lefthander, it will probably be
Terry Witt, a freshman from Andress
High, or Bill Onick, a freshman from
Irvin. If Cohen decides to go with a right-
hander, the probable choice would be

Bill Diesselhorst, a frestman from Fair-
fax, Va

The Miners appear to be well set in
the hitting and fielding de partment.
Choen has all but one player returning
from a squad that compiled a team bat-
ting average of .326 last season. Seven of
the returnees topped the .300 mark.

Charlie West, the Miner’s All-America
safety in football, tops the returning hit-
ters. In 1967, he had a .366 average with
10 home runs and drove in 48 runs.

West will be returning to the outfield
along with John Murphy. And the entire
infield from the 1967 crew returns. The
Miners compiled a team fielding average
of .948 last season.

Probable starters in the infield for U.
T. El Paso are John Miller at first base,
Juan Sandoval at second, Danny Blanco
at third and Robert Aguirre at shortstop.

In the outfield, it will be West in cen-
ter, junior college transfer Mike Ward in
left and right field will be between John
Murphy and Sam McLellan. Ronnie Hix,
another JC transfer, is the probable start-
er behind the plate.

Cohen will probably shake up his line-
up somewhat in the second game in order
to get a look at some other players who
have done well in practice this spring.

season, the Miners came out on top with
