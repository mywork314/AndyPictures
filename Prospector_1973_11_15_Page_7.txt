Baseball squad

opens practices

Baseball practice is being held at
3:00 pm. every Monday,
Wednesday and Friday at Dudley
Field beginning last week. Coach
Andy Cohen says it is unusual for
the team to practice this late in
the fall, but they will continue to
do so as long as weather permits.

Although he is concentrating on
fundamentals, Cohen says he plans
to hold one or two scrimmage
games a week to allow him to
evaluate his new players.

Last year the baseball team
finished the season with an 18-25
record. The coach thinks they will
do much better this year because
of the squad’s spirit and
willingness to work.

Pitching was a problem for the
team last year, Cohen said, but he
feels his five returning pitchers and
four or five newcomers will be
able to handle it this year.

When weather becomes 1
problem, Cohen says the
aftermoon sessions will stop. :
Formal practice starts in January.

The Miners open their season
against last year’s junior college ~
champion, Ranger Junior College,
in February. The first conference
game is scheduled for April.
