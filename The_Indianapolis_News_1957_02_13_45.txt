Andy Cohen

Due Here
Tomorrow

Andy Cohen, the new man-
ager of the Indianapolis In-
dians, is coming to town to-
morrow for a noon press con-
ference.

The Indianapolis News (Indianapolis, Indiana) - Wed, Feb 13, 1957 -

Cohen recently returned to
the States from the Canal Zone
where he managed the Chester-
field club in a winter league.
It will be Cohen’s first visit
to Indianapolis since his play-

ing days in the 30s with Min-
neapolis.

Cohen, who managed New
Orleans the last two years,
was appointed to the Indianap-
olis job with consent of the
Chicago White Sox after Kerby
Farrell moved up to the Cleve-
land club. The community-
owned Indians have a working
agreement with the White Sox
for the 1956 season.

The Indianapolis mana ge-
ment also announced today
that Joe Tipton, a catcher on
the club’s “restricted” list, has
been sold to Birmingham. Tip-
ton quit the Memphis club in
midseason last year after a
dispute with Manager Jack
Cassini and sat out the rest
of the season at his home.
