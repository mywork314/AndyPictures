Could be five-club tangle in Southern

From, almost a month of piay it now looks as if the
Southern League chase could become a five-club affair.
Atlanta, Birmingham, Memphis and Nashville were liked
for the first division before the first official pitch.

Those Vols went into a tailspin, but in knocking off the Chicks
in a double-header, they showed it wouldn’t be
wise to count ’em out. New Orleans has come
up with a pretty fair club and Andy Cohen will
get everything out of the Pells.

Every club in the league is waiting on help
coming down from the majors and triple-A clubs.
Two good players could make pennant winners
out of any of the five top clubs.

The Southern needs a close race to make up
for the loss in attendance during the cold streaks.
Seldom, since chain baseball, have the four big

MARION cities—Atlanta, New Orleans, Birmingham and
Memphis—been at one another’s throats. This would. be a
good year for a return of the big cities to the first division.

Red Marion, manager of the Louisville club, left Denver,
saying “Denver is definitely the club to beat in my book.”
Red can read standings as well as. anyone. ... The Bears
won 10 of their first 11 starts and there’s more help coming down
from the Yankees. ;

Clyde King, who said he had 22 double-A players, still. be-
lieves in his Crackers. “A manager couldn’t ask for better
cooperation or more harmony,” says the bespectacled Cracker
manager. : : .

King, an old pitcher, used 54 pitchers in the Crackers’
first 19 games. So it is pitching help the Crackers want
from the Milwaukee Braves.
