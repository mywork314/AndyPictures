Miners Lose No. 1 Shortstop

By MARK LEIBSON
Times Sports Writer

The University of Texas at El
Paso baseball team has yet to
begin the 1976 season and
already Coach Andy Cohen is
faced with a major obstacle.

It was learned Monday
morning that starting shortstop
Dave Nance will be ineligible
for the upcoming season. “‘It
was something I wasn’t ex-
pecting,”’ said Cohen after
finding out that Nance had
already used up his 10
semesters allowed under
NCAA rules. “It'll really hurt
the team,”

Without Nance at short to
shore up the infield, the Miners
have been forced to turn their
attention toward unproven
Dave Patt, a part-timer last
season,

“I'm not entirely a
newcomer at the position,”
said Patt. “It'll take some time
for me to get in the grove.”

Time is not exactly what the
Miners have. Friday and
Saturday, UTEP hosts Ranger
Junior College in back-to-back
doubleheaders beginning at 1
p.m. both days at the Ysleta
Cutoff.

Cohen’s tentative starting
lineup has sophomore Art
Bravo, a graduate of Austin
High School, leading off and
playing second, Newcomer

David Carrillo from Van Horn
will be playing center and
batting second.

Then comes the meat of the
lineup with Hills Junior College
transfer Keith Dwinell at first
and Glenn Villarreal, this
year’s captain, in the cleanup
position and playing third base,

Outfielders Terry
Dillingham and Keith Rushing
follow in the batting order
before designated hitter Steve
Dau and freshman catcher
Terry Goodro take their turns
at bat. Patt will be ninth in the
lineup and Jeff Hogan is slated
to hurl the opener against
Ranger.

“T think we've got one of the
best groups of outfielders we've
ever had out here,'’ said Cohen
as he looked over the 40-plus
athletes trying out for the 27-28
man team.

“Besides the kids I have
Starting, there are people like

“I think we're going to peak
and stay there longer than last
year because a lot of the guys
are interested in baseball this
year. You can see it when they
stay after workouts to try to
better themselves.”

Cohen expects his pitching
staff to consist primarily of
Hogan, Mike Rima, Andy
Grooms, Dau, Gary Kobat,
