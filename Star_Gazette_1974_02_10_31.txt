. Athletes Are in Sports Halls

Elmira’s Sports Halls of Fame, originated in 1958 with
the Softball Hall, have undergone some drastic revisions in
the last year.

The originators of the Halls, Businessman M. Louis
Paltrowitz, Star-Gazette Sports Editor Al. Mallette, City
Recreation Director Allie Quatrano and former School Board
member Bernard (Bus) Hartnett, carried the task of sending

out and counting ballots for each category every year.

At the start, 48 Elmira, Elmira Heights and Horseheads

sports boosters met to draw up lists of candidates for each

; sport and from these master lists, voted on candidates for
entry into each hall.

In the beginning, each Hall accepted upwards to 10
members in a single year, then decreased the number of

inductees yearly. Today, in the four original Mall sports —
softball, baseball, football and basketball — only the top one
‘or two in voting are inducted.
