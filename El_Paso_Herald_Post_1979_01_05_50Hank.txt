Last Call For Sills

The reigning queen of the opera, Beverly Sills,
will retire from singing after this year at the Met.
so the El Paso folks who trip over to Dallas every
May to see the touring Met company will havea last
chance to hear her.

She will be singing in “Don Pasquale” on Thurs-
day, May 10. Luciano Pavarotti will be heard in
“Tosca” on May 11. Jon Vickers is set for a role in
‘The Bartered Bride” in the Saturday matinee and
that night, May 12, Jess Thomas will be heard in
‘““Tannhauser.”’ There’s a limited number of reser-

vations, so call Josephine Fernstrom pronto if you
want to get on the list.

Greener pastures

Hank Cohen bowed out of the lead in “Natalie
Needs a Nightie,”’ the current dinner theater farce
holding forth at Gillespie's. He's on his way to audi-
tion for “Barefoot in the Park” at a dinner theater
in Omaha, Neb. Hank, who has been in several of
the CMC Productions here and in Las Cruces is
serious about an acting career. He fired off re-
sumes to a bunch of dinner theater around the
country and came up with a nibble.
