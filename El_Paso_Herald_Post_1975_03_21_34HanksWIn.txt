‘Tampering Case’
given Kennedy
By Buffalo Team

BUFFALO, N.Y. (UPI) —
The National Basketball Assn.
Buffalo Braves asked NBA
commissioner J. Walter
Kennedy to investigate possible
“tampering” with the team’s
general manager by the New
York Knicks.

Braves president Paul
Snyder sent a lengthy telegram
to Kennedy and the Knicks
after two New York City
Newspapers reported that
Braves general manager Eddie
Donovan would leave the team
to return to the Knicks.
Donovan was general manager
of the New York team until
1970.

Donovan denied the reports,
Saying “there is no truth to the
rumors at all.’”

Snyder asked Kennedy to
conduct an immediate in-
vestigation of the reports and to
add the topic to the agenda for
next Tuesday’s board of
governors meeting in Chicago.

Donovan’s contract with the
Braves expires next month, but
Snyder wrote Kennedy that
“the Braves have been paying
Donovan pursuant to the terms
of a new three-year contract
orally accepted in 1974 by
Donovan.”
