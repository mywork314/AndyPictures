EL PASO — A third place
finish in the Southern Divi-
sionof the Western Athletic
Conference baseball race
will be at stake tonight and
Saturday as the University
of New Mexico takes on
Texas-El Paso in a thee
game series.

The Lobos dropped to 26-
21 onthe seasonat3-12in
the WAC last week, losing to
Arizona State by scores of 6-
1, 18-4 and 4-0. Meanwhile
Texas-El Paso continued to
have its problems in WAC
play, losing three to Arizona
to drop 0-15 in conference
play and 15-26 overall. The
Miners lost 15-12 in 11 in-
nings, 6-4 and 8-2 to the
Wildcats.

New Mexico needs one
victory in the series to as-
sure itself of a third place
finish while the Miners need
to sweep the series to gain a
tie for third with the Lobos.
