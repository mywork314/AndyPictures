Cohen’s Worry:
No. 1 Wildcats

Fans continue to turn away in
record droves for University of
Texas at El Paso baseball
games but Andy Cohen refuses
to concern himself with that
problem. ‘‘Arizona’s baseball
team is a far bigger obstacle,”
the UTEP mentor emphasized.

The Miners host Western
Athletic Conference foe
Arizona at noon tomorrow in
Dudley Field and again at 10
a.m. Saturday in a twinbill. The
U of A series at Dudley marks
the first time since April 11 the
Orange have played at home.

“UTEP concerns me,"’
Arizona coach Jerry Kindall
commmented. ‘‘They’ve
proved that they can beat the
top teams having already
defeated us and Arizona State
this year. We cannot afford a
letdown.”’

The Wildcats, ranked No. 1
nationally, are in a dogfight for
the WAC Southern Division
diamond title with three-time
defending champion Arizona
State. The Cats are 50-4 overall
and 10-2 in WAC play while ASU
is second at 9-3. The Miners are
24-21 in overall play and 2-10 in
