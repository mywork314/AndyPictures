Wasiak Takes
Over From His
Old Manager

Stan Wasiak arrived in El]
Paso today to take the mana-
gerial reins of the Sun Dodgers
from Andy Cohen who took
them from Monty Basgall
after Tuesday night’s game
with the Alexandria Aces at
Dudley Field.

Andy, losing a chance to
become the only undefeated
Texas League manager of the
season and maybe in history,
when: the Sun Dodgers bowed
to Alexandria last night 5-1,
looked forward to working
with one of his proteges this
season.

Wasiak. coming here
from Daytona Beach, played
for Andy back in the early
1940s when Andy managed
the Dayton, O., club in the
Mid-Atlantic League.

“HE WAS a hustling third
baseman and a_ good all-
around ball player,” said Andy.
“I'm sure he'll do a good job
here and the fans and players
will like him.”

Andy is expecting to contin-
ue as coach of the Sun Dodg-
ers. In fact, he has just agreed
to make his first road trip with
the team, starting Saturday.
So far this season he’s coached
only at home.

Several years ago, he was a
one-night manager of the Phil-
adelphia Phils. They beat
Milwaukee that night. So Andy
has the distinction of being the
only undefeated manager in
big league baseball.

and that came in the eighth.

“Some of Alexandria's hits
