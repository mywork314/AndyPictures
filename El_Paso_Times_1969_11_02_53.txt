Cohen Named
Vets Parade
Official

ANDY COHEN

Andy Cohen, a member of
the Jewish War Veterans
Chapter No. 749, will represent
the chapter as deputy Parade
Marshal in the Veterans Day
Parade, Manny O. Rivas,
chairman of the parade com-
mittee, said Saturday.

Rivas also announced that
the United American Veterans
Administration of El Paso will
hold a meeting at 7:30 p.m.
Monday at the American
Legion Post No. 58 at 4725 Vul-
can to discuss final plans for
the parade. Parade marshal
will be named later.

Cohen, a native El Pasoan,
spent 30 years as a baseball
player, manager and coach,
including time coaching the
baseball team at the Universi-
ty of Texas at E] Paso. He was
the first member to be select-
ed to El Paso Athletic Hall of
Fame.

A veteran of World War II
and past commander of Amer-
ican Legion Post No. 58, Cohen
is a member of the Downtown
Lions Club. He is married and
has three children.
