“Boy, this is just like winning

Miner starter Jeff Hogan won
his second game of the season
as the big, husky right-hander
pitched the entire nine innings
while allowing seven hits and
two bases on balls while
striking out three Lobos.

“You have to give Hogan
credit,” praised the Miner
skipper. “Jeff had good stuff
today and always kept the ball
around the plate. It would have
been a shame if be had lost a 1-0
decision.”

New Mexico's lone run was
recorded off Hogan in the
eighth inning. Strassle singled
with no outs and was moved to
second base by Bill Smith's
sacrifice bunt in front of home
plate.

Strassle then took third on a
passed ball by Miner catcher
Joe Dehn and scored when Ron
Smith slashed a single between
third and short as Cohen had
his field drawn in for a
possible play at the plate.

For alll the seniors on the U.T.

WAC records.
